📌 PURPOSE

This method statement defines the comprehensive procedure for installation, inspection, testing, and commissioning of the luxury bath tub system according to project standards, manufacturer specifications, and international plumbing codes. It ensures proper coordination of high-end fixtures (KOHLER & FANTINI) with existing plumbing infrastructure while maintaining quality standards expected for premium residential installations.

📋 SCOPE

This method statement covers complete installation including:

  • KOHLER K-1166-C1-0 luxury bath tub installation and leveling
  • FANTINI floor-standing mixer (Model 2901N580SB+19001680A) with Murano glass knob installation
  • KOHLER K-7223-PB bath tub drain connection to 50mm UPVC Silenta waste system
  • Hot and cold water supply connections via PPR piping under floor
  • Testing, flushing, commissioning, and handover procedures
  • Pre-installation inspections, quality control, and documentation

🔧 INSTALLATION PROCEDURE
1. Pre-Installation Activities

1.1 Material Inspection & Approval

  • Verify all equipment against approved shop drawings and submittals
  • Check manufacturer nameplates match specifications (KOHLER K-1166-C1-0, FANTINI models)
  • Inspect bath tub for any damage, scratches, or defects on acrylic surface
  • Inspect floor-standing mixer and Murano glass knob for damage or finish defects
  • Verify drain kit components are complete (overflow, drain, gaskets)
  • Check PPR and UPVC piping materials for proper size and specification
  • Verify quantities as per Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and purchase orders
  • Obtain Material Inspection Request (MIR) approval from consultant before proceeding

1.2 Site Preparation & Coordination

  • Review and verify approved shop drawings for Master Bathroom layout
  • Mark bath tub final position on floor as per drawings
  • Mark floor-standing mixer location (distance from tub, centerline)
  • Verify structural floor loading capacity is adequate for filled tub weight
  • Confirm hot and cold water supply PPR pipes are installed and accessible under floor
  • Confirm 50mm UPVC Silenta waste line is installed with proper slope (min. 1:50)
  • Check floor levelness using spirit level; note any deviations (max. 3mm per meter)
  • Coordinate with other trades (tiles, marble, electrical, painting) for access and completion
  • Ensure bathroom finishes (floor tiles, wall tiles) are complete around installation area
  • Protect finished surfaces with drop cloths and protective padding
2. Bath Tub Installation

2.1 Bath Tub Positioning and Leveling

  1. Transport KOHLER K-1166-C1-0 bath tub carefully to Master Bathroom using minimum 2 workers
  2. Remove protective packaging carefully to avoid scratching acrylic surface
  3. Inspect tub for any damage incurred during transport
  4. Keep protective cardboard on tub bottom until final positioning
  5. Position bath tub at marked location as per approved shop drawings
  6. Check tub levelness in both directions (length and width) using 1200mm spirit level
  7. If floor is not level, use plastic shims under tub feet (DO NOT use wooden shims)
  8. Ensure maximum deviation is within ±2mm across entire tub length
  9. Verify tub is stable and does not rock when pressure applied at corners
  10. Check that tub rim is level and properly seated against wall surfaces
  11. Verify minimum clearances are maintained: 50mm from walls (for maintenance access)

2.2 Drain Installation

  1. Install KOHLER K-7223-PB drain kit as per manufacturer instructions
  2. Apply plumber's putty around drain flange (do not use silicone for this connection)
  3. Insert drain into tub drain hole from top side
  4. From underneath tub, attach drain body and tighten firmly (do not over-tighten)
  5. Install overflow assembly at marked location on tub wall
  6. Connect overflow pipe to drain assembly using provided slip joint connections
  7. Install rubber gaskets at all connections (check gaskets are not twisted or damaged)
  8. Hand-tighten all slip nuts first, then use wrench to secure (avoid over-torquing acrylic)
  9. Wipe excess putty from drain rim with clean cloth
  10. Install trip lever overflow cover and test operation (should open/close smoothly)

2.3 Waste Connection

  1. Measure and cut 50mm UPVC Silenta waste pipe to connect drain outlet to main waste line
  2. Ensure minimum slope of 1:50 (2%) towards main waste stack
  3. Deburr all cut pipe edges using deburring tool
  4. Dry-fit all waste connections before solvent cementing
  5. Apply PVC solvent cement to pipe and fitting socket as per manufacturer instructions
  6. Insert pipe fully into fitting and hold for 15 seconds
  7. Wipe excess cement with clean cloth
  8. Allow minimum 2 hours curing time before water testing
  9. Support waste pipe properly with pipe clips at maximum 600mm intervals
  10. Install P-trap (if not already part of drain kit) ensuring proper trap seal depth (50mm minimum)
  11. Verify trap arm length does not exceed code requirements (typically max. 1.5m)
3. Floor-Standing Mixer Installation

3.1 Water Supply Preparation

  1. Shut off hot and cold water supply to Master Bathroom area
  2. Drain existing water from supply lines (open downstream taps)
  3. Locate hot and cold PPR supply pipes under floor at marked mixer location
  4. Verify pipe locations match shop drawing dimensions
  5. Measure and cut floor openings (if required) for mixer supply risers
  6. Install floor escutcheons (cover plates) at pipe penetrations to hide rough openings

3.2 Mixer Installation

  1. Unpack FANTINI floor-standing mixer Model 2901N580SB+19001680A carefully
  2. Inspect mixer body and Polished Nickel PVD finish for any damage or defects
  3. Check that all components are included: mixer body, mounting hardware, flexible hoses
  4. Install isolation angle valves on hot and cold water supply PPR pipes (if not pre-installed)
  5. Wrap angle valve threads with PTFE tape (3-4 wraps in direction of thread)
  6. Connect flexible braided stainless steel hoses to angle valves (hand-tighten first, then use wrench)
  7. Measure distance from floor to mixer inlets; cut flexible hoses if excessive length
  8. Install mixer body on floor at marked location (check vertical alignment using spirit level)
  9. Secure mixer to floor using manufacturer-provided mounting hardware (if applicable)
  10. Connect flexible hoses to mixer hot and cold inlets (observe hot = left, cold = right)
  11. Hand-tighten connections first, then use wrench for final 1/4 turn (do not over-tighten)
  12. Install Murano glass knob (Model 2901N448C5) onto mixer control spindle
  13. Secure knob using set screw (if provided) and verify smooth operation
  14. Install hand-held shower head on mixer outlet (if mixer includes this feature)
  15. Check mixer height is comfortable for tub filling operation (typically 650-900mm above tub rim)
4. Final Connections and Finishing
  • Verify all piping connections are properly tightened (no loose joints)
  • Check that no tools or foreign materials left inside tub or drain
  • Clean tub interior using manufacturer-approved cleaning solution
  • Remove any installation markings or tape from fixtures
  • Install drain cover/strainer in tub drain opening
  • Verify overflow cover is properly installed and aligned
  • Check that mixer operates smoothly through full range of motion
  • Verify hot/cold indicator markings are correct on mixer handle
  • Install floor escutcheons and ensure proper fit around mixer pipes
  • Apply silicone sealant around tub rim where it meets wall tiles (use mold-resistant sealant)
  • Tool sealant joint to smooth, professional finish
  • Clean any sealant residue from tub or tile surfaces immediately
  • Keep tub area undisturbed for 24 hours to allow sealant curing
5. Inspection & Documentation
  • Submit Work Inspection Request (WIR) to consultant for pre-testing inspection
  • Document all installation stages with clear photographs showing:
  • • Bath tub positioning and leveling
  • • Drain and overflow installation
  • • Waste piping connections
  • • Floor-standing mixer installation
  • • Final completed installation
  • Obtain consultant approval signature on WIR before proceeding to testing phase
  • Record any deviations from shop drawings or specifications (if applicable)
  • Update as-built drawings with actual installed conditions

1. Bath Tub Installation

  • Tub is level in both directions (max. deviation ±2mm)

  • Tub rim properly seated against wall surfaces

3. Floor-Standing Mixer

  • Mixer is perfectly vertical (checked with spirit level)
  • Polished Nickel PVD finish is intact with no scratches or damage
  • Murano glass knob properly installed and secure
  • Mixer handle operates smoothly through full range
  • Hot (left) and cold (right) water connections correctly identified
  • Flexible hoses properly connected with no kinks or excessive stress
  • Angle valves accessible for future maintenance
  • Floor escutcheons properly installed to cover pipe openings
4. Connections and Finishing
  • All threaded connections properly sealed with PTFE tape

  • Silicone sealant neatly applied around tub rim

5. Documentation and Cleanliness
  • All quality control checklists completed and signed

🔬 TESTING & COMMISSIONING
1. Pre-Commissioning Verification
  • Complete visual inspection passed and documented
  • All quality control checks completed with satisfactory results
  • Consultant WIR approval obtained for all installation stages
  • Water supply system cleaned and flushed prior to final connection
  • Verify isolation valves are in closed position before testing
  • Ensure waste system is open and flowing freely
  • Confirm all workers and inspectors are present for witness testing
2. Leak Testing

2.1 Drain and Waste System Leak Test

  1. Plug tub drain using watertight test plug
  2. Fill bath tub with water to overflow level
  3. Observe overflow operation - water should discharge smoothly through overflow
  4. Maintain water level for 30 minutes
  5. Inspect all waste connections underneath tub for any leakage
  6. Check P-trap, slip joints, and UPVC solvent-cemented joints
  7. Mark any leaks and repair before proceeding
  8. Remove test plug and observe drain flow rate
  9. Drain should empty tub completely within 5-8 minutes (approximate)
  10. Verify trip lever operates drain plug correctly (open/close)

2.2 Water Supply Leak Test

  1. Slowly open isolation angle valves at mixer connections
  2. Observe all connections for any leakage during pressurization
  3. Check flexible hoses, mixer connections, and angle valve threads
  4. Operate mixer handle slowly to test hot and cold water flow separately
  5. Observe for leaks at mixer body during operation
  6. Tighten any connections showing minor seepage (do not over-tighten)
  7. If persistent leaking occurs, isolate water, disassemble, and reinstall with new gaskets/tape
  8. Maintain system under pressure for 2 hours minimum
  9. Re-inspect all connections after pressure test period
  10. Document test results (pass/fail) with photographs
3. Functional Performance Testing

3.1 Water Flow and Temperature Testing

  1. Turn on hot water supply and allow system to reach operating temperature
  2. Measure hot water temperature at mixer outlet using thermometer (target: 45-55°C)
  3. Verify cold water supply temperature (typical: 15-25°C)
  4. Test mixer operation through full range from cold to hot
  5. Verify smooth operation with no binding or stiffness
  6. Check for proper mixing - should achieve warm water at mid-position
  7. Measure flow rate by filling measured container for 60 seconds (typical: 12-20 liters/minute)
  8. Verify flow rate is adequate for comfortable tub filling (target: fill time 8-12 minutes)
  9. Test hand-held shower operation (if mixer includes this feature)
  10. Check water pressure at mixer using pressure gauge (typical: 2-4 bar)

3.2 Drain System Performance

  1. Fill tub to normal bathing level (approximately 150-200 liters)
  2. Open drain using trip lever
  3. Time complete drainage (should be 5-8 minutes typically)
  4. Observe drain flow - should create proper vortex with no slow drainage
  5. Listen for any gurgling sounds (indicates possible venting issue)
  6. Verify P-trap maintains proper water seal after draining (50mm minimum)
  7. Repeat fill and drain cycle 3 times to verify consistent performance
  8. Check for any leaks that may develop after thermal cycling
4. System Integration Testing
  1. Test complete bathing cycle: filling, bathing simulation, draining
  2. Verify mixer temperature control is responsive and stable
  3. Check that overflow functions correctly when tub is overfilled
  4. Verify drain operates smoothly with trip lever mechanism
  5. Test isolation valves for proper shut-off (no bypass leakage)
  6. Confirm no vibration or noise during water flow
  7. Check tub stability under full water load (no movement or flexing)
  8. Verify all finishes remain intact after testing (no water damage)
5. System Demonstration & Training
  • Conduct comprehensive system demonstration to client representative and facility maintenance staff
  • Demonstrate normal operation: filling tub, adjusting temperature, draining
  • Explain mixer operation and temperature control features
  • Show location and operation of isolation valves for emergency shut-off
  • Explain trip lever drain operation (how to open/close)
  • Demonstrate overflow safety feature
  • Provide guidance on proper cleaning and maintenance of acrylic tub surface
  • Explain care requirements for Polished Nickel PVD finish on mixer
  • Show proper cleaning procedure for Murano glass knob (gentle cleaning only)
  • Advise on maintenance schedule and warranty requirements
  • Answer any questions from client or maintenance staff
  • Provide emergency contact information for warranty service

1. PURPOSE

This method statement describes the detailed and standardized procedure for the installation of twin cold water booster pump sets mounted on a common stainless-steel skid with 2½″ suction and discharge headers, forming part of the domestic water supply system for the CEO Villa Project. It ensures compliance with manufacturer recommendations, project specifications, SMACNA/ASHRAE standards, and IHCC Quality and HSE procedures.

2. SCOPE

This method statement applies to the supply, handling, positioning, installation, alignment, piping connections, and inspection of booster pump sets including associated valves, strainers, flexible connectors, and control accessories.

Scope includes: - Pre-installation verification and inspection of materials and site conditions. - Mechanical fixing and alignment of pump skid on the plinth. - Connection of suction and discharge piping with valves and flexible joints. - Electrical termination and control interfacing to the VFD panel. - QA/QC inspection and pre-commissioning checks prior to testing and handover.

8. INSTALLATION PROCEDURE
8.1 Pre-Installation Checks
  1. Verify that all pumps, motors, and valves are approved through MIR and free from damage or rust.
  2. Ensure all civil works related to the pump plinth are completed and approved by civil engineers.
  3. Verify that the area is clean, waterproofed, and painted before starting installation.
  4. Ensure all tools, lifting gear, and materials are available and inspected.
  5. Confirm shop drawings and layout match the as-built site conditions.
8.2 Handling and Transportation
  1. Offload the skid carefully using a forklift or crane with nylon slings.
  2. Avoid dragging the pump skid or components on rough surfaces.
  3. Inspect for physical damages such as dents, bent shafts, or cracked flanges.
  4. Store in a shaded, dry area protected from dust and moisture.
8.3 Installation Steps

Step 1: Positioning and Mounting - Mark anchor bolt locations on the plinth as per base plate holes.
- Drill and fix anchor bolts with chemical grouting if required.
- Place neoprene pads below the pump base to reduce vibration.
- Set the booster skid on the plinth and align using a spirit level.
- Tighten bolts with torque wrench as per manufacturer’s recommendation.

Step 2: Piping Connections - Connect suction and discharge headers (2½”) using flexible connectors.
- Install valves and fittings as per approved drawings: - Suction: Isolation Ball Valve (GV-38 & GV-39) - Discharge: Check Valve + Isolation Ball Valve (GV-41 & GV-42) - Ensure strainers are installed on suction lines upstream of pumps.
- Maintain proper alignment to prevent stress on pump flanges.
- Provide pipe supports to maintain line stability.

Step 3: Electrical Connection - Connect motors to the VFD panel (Xylem HVL4.040-A0010) following approved wiring diagrams.
- Install Danfoss MBS1900 pressure sensor on discharge header and connect to the control panel.
- Verify grounding and insulation resistance before energizing.
- Set VFD control logic for duty/standby operation as per design.

Step 4: Final Alignment & Inspection - Check level and alignment after tightening.
- Ensure mechanical clearance between rotating and fixed parts.
- Ensure piping connections are stress-free and no misalignment exists.
- Clean the installation area and remove debris.

8.4 Post-Installation & Testing.
  1. Conduct internal inspection with QA/QC before consultant WIR.
  2. Perform hydrostatic testing of suction/discharge piping as per specifications.
  3. After successful testing, connect electrical supply and perform motor rotation check.
  4. Verify system operation through VFD control – observe pressure, current, and flow.
  5. Record readings and submit to Consultant for approval prior to commissioning.

INSTALLATION PROCEDURE
1. Pre-Installation Activities
1.1 Material Inspection and Approval
  1. Verify all ceramic fixtures against approved shop drawings and material submittals
  2. Check manufacturer nameplates and model numbers match specifications
  3. Inspect all fixtures for:
  • Surface cracks, chips, or defects
  • Glaze uniformity and color consistency
  • Transit damage or packaging issues
  • Completeness of accessories (seats, bolts, gaskets)
  1. Verify quantities as per Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
  2. Obtain Material Inspection Request (MIR) approval from consultant
  3. Store fixtures in dry, secure area with protective packaging until installation
  4. Reject any damaged or non-conforming items immediately
1.2 Site Preparation and Coordination
  1. Review and verify approved shop drawings and as-built drawings
  2. Mark fixture locations on finished surfaces as per drawings
  3. Verify wall and floor finishes are complete and cured
  4. Check structural provisions:
  • Wall blocking for wall-hung fixtures is installed and secure
  • Floor loading capacity adequate for bath tub (when filled)
  • Anchor points accessible and properly located
  1. Verify plumbing rough-in is complete:
  • Water supply lines stubbed out at correct height and location
  • Drain connections properly positioned and sealed
  • Flush tank systems installed and pressure tested
  1. Coordinate with tiling contractor to ensure proper waterproofing
  2. Verify electrical connections for bath tub pump are ready (240V)
  3. Ensure adequate working space and access for installation
2. Wall-Hung WC Installation
2.1 Preparation

  1. Verify concealed flush tank frame (GROHE Rapid or GEBERIT Duofix) is properly installed and secure

  2. Check frame is level both horizontally and vertically (tolerance: ±2mm)

  3. Confirm waste connection is at correct height: 180-230mm from finished floor

  4. Verify wall finish thickness matches design (for proper WC projection)

  5. Remove protective packaging from WC carefully

2.2 Mounting Bracket Installation

  1. Install mounting brackets onto concealed frame threaded rods

  2. Adjust bracket height to achieve finished WC rim height: 400-420mm from floor

  3. Verify bracket is horizontal using spirit level

  4. Tighten mounting nuts hand-tight initially (final torque after WC mounting)

2.3 WC Pan Installation
  1. Clean WC outlet and flush tank waste connection thoroughly
  2. Install rubber gasket onto waste connection (supplied with frame)
  3. Carefully align WC pan with mounting bracket and waste connection
  4. Push WC pan gently onto waste connection ensuring proper seal
  5. Insert fixing bolts through WC mounting holes into brackets
  6. Hand-tighten mounting bolts, checking WC remains level
  7. Apply uniform torque to mounting bolts: 15-20 Nm (use torque wrench)
  8. CRITICAL: Do not over-tighten bolts - risk of cracking ceramic
  9. Install decorative bolt covers
2.4 Seat Cover Installation
  1. Position soft-close seat hinges over mounting holes on WC pan
  2. Insert fixing bolts from underside through WC pan
  3. Tighten fixings hand-tight (avoid over-tightening)
  4. Test soft-close mechanism operates smoothly
  5. Adjust seat position if needed for proper alignment
2.5 Final Checks and Sealing
  1. Check WC pan is firmly secured with no movement
  2. Apply sanitary silicone sealant around WC base where it meets wall
  3. Tool silicone joint smoothly for neat finish
  4. Clean any excess sealant immediately with damp cloth
  5. Allow sealant to cure per manufacturer instructions (typically 24 hours)
  6. Leave protective film on WC until final cleaning
3. Floor-Mounted WC Installation
3.1 Preparation
  1. Verify floor waste connection location matches WC outlet position
  2. Check floor is level at installation location (tolerance: ±2mm/m)
  3. Ensure waste pipe stub extends 50-100mm above finished floor
  4. Clean waste connection thoroughly, remove any debris or construction material
3.2 Marking and Positioning

  1. Place WC in position and check alignment with waste outlet

  2. Mark fixing holes on floor through WC base mounting holes

  3. Mark outline of WC base on floor with pencil

  4. Remove WC and set aside safely

3.3 Floor Preparation and Drilling

  1. Drill holes at marked positions using appropriate masonry bit

  2. Hole diameter: Typically 10mm for M8 expansion bolts

  3. Hole depth: 50-60mm (check bolt length requirements)

  4. Clean holes thoroughly with brush and vacuum

  5. Insert wall plugs or expansion anchors into holes

3.4 WC Pan Connection

  1. Install rubber gasket or wax ring onto WC outlet

  2. Alternative: Apply flexible sealant around waste connection

  3. Carefully lower WC pan into position over waste connection

  4. Ensure proper alignment and full engagement with waste pipe

  5. Press down firmly to compress seal

  6. Check WC is level using spirit level in both directions

  7. Use shims if needed to achieve levelness (place under base, not visible)

3.5 Securing to Floor

  1. Insert fixing bolts through base mounting holes into floor anchors

  2. Add rubber or plastic washers between bolt and ceramic (prevent cracking)

  3. Tighten bolts gradually in diagonal pattern (similar to wheel lug nuts)

  4. Torque specification: 10-15 Nm maximum

  5. CRITICAL: Do not over-tighten - ceramic will crack

  6. Install decorative bolt covers

3.6 Built-in Flush Tank Connection

  1. Connect water supply line to flush tank inlet valve

  2. Use flexible hose with compression fittings

  3. Wrap PTFE tape on threaded connections (3-4 wraps clockwise)

  4. Tighten connections hand-tight plus 1/2 turn with wrench

  5. Do not over-tighten plastic components

3.7 Sealing and Seat Installation
  1. Apply sanitary silicone sealant around base of WC where it meets floor
  2. Tool joint smoothly for professional finish
  3. Install seat cover as per manufacturer instructions
  4. Clean any excess sealant with damp cloth
  5. Allow sealant to cure 24 hours before testing
4. Under-Counter Wash Basin Installation
4.1 Counter Preparation

  1. Verify counter/vanity top is installed and level

  2. Check cutout dimensions match basin rim dimensions (560mm x 350mm)

  3. Ensure cutout edges are smooth with no sharp corners

  4. Verify water supply lines and drain connections are properly positioned

  5. Clean cutout area thoroughly

4.2 Basin Installation

  1. Apply continuous bead of silicone sealant on underside of counter around cutout

  2. Position under-mount clips at manufacturer-specified locations (typically 4-6 clips)

  3. Carefully lift basin and position under countertop cutout

  4. Center basin in cutout ensuring equal overhang on all sides

  5. Press basin firmly against underside of counter to compress silicone

  6. Secure under-mount clips, tightening screws evenly

  7. CRITICAL: Do not over-tighten clips - risk of cracking basin

  8. Wipe away excess silicone from visible areas

4.3 Waste and Trap Installation

  1. Install waste outlet through basin drain hole

  2. Apply plumber's putty or rubber gasket under flange

  3. Tighten waste outlet nut from underneath hand-tight plus 1/4 turn

  4. Connect P-trap or bottle trap to waste outlet

  5. Connect trap to wall drain connection

  6. Use rubber gaskets at all connections

  7. Ensure trap maintains proper water seal height

  8. Hand-tighten all trap connections (do not use excessive force on plastic)

4.4 Faucet/Mixer Installation

Note: Faucet installation to be coordinated with the brass fittings installation team as per separate method statement.

5. Over-Counter Wash Basin Installation
5.1 Counter Preparation

  1. Verify counter surface is level, clean, and dry

  2. Mark basin position on counter using template or basin itself

  3. Drill drain hole in counter if not pre-drilled (typically 35-40mm diameter)

  4. Ensure faucet mounting holes match basin configuration

5.2 Basin Installation

  1. Apply continuous bead of silicone sealant on underside of basin base

  2. Alternative: Apply sealant on counter surface within marked outline

  3. Carefully position basin on counter, aligning drain hole with counter cutout

  4. Press down firmly to spread silicone evenly

  5. Check basin is level using spirit level

  6. Wipe away excess silicone with damp cloth immediately

  7. Allow silicone to cure per manufacturer recommendations before use

5.3 Waste Installation

  1. Install waste outlet and trap as described in Section 4.3 above

6. Counter Basin with Semi-Pedestal Installation
6.1 Wall Mounting Bracket Installation

  1. Mark basin height on wall: Standard rim height 800-850mm from finished floor

  2. Position basin on marked height and mark fixing bracket holes on wall

  3. Drill holes at marked positions (typically 10mm diameter)

  4. Insert wall plugs or expansion anchors

  5. Secure mounting bracket to wall using appropriate screws

  6. Verify bracket is level and firmly attached

6.2 Basin Installation

  1. Apply silicone sealant on back edge of basin where it meets wall

  2. Hook basin onto wall bracket

  3. Check basin is level (may need to adjust bracket)

  4. Install waste outlet and trap as described previously

6.3 Semi-Pedestal Installation

  1. Position semi-pedestal against wall beneath basin

  2. Ensure it properly conceals waste pipes and trap

  3. Mark fixing holes on wall through pedestal

  4. Drill and install wall anchors

  5. Apply silicone sealant where pedestal meets wall

  6. Secure pedestal to wall using appropriate screws

  7. Apply silicone where pedestal meets floor

  8. Clean excess sealant

7. Bath Tub Installation (KOHLER SOK Effervescence)
7.1 Pre-Installation Preparation

  1. CRITICAL SAFETY: Minimum 3 persons required for handling and installation

  2. Verify floor structure can support loaded bath tub weight (water + user ≈ 800-1000 kg)

  3. Check bath tub dimensions fit through doorway and access route (104 inches/264cm length)

  4. Verify plumbing rough-in is complete:

  5. Hot and cold water supply lines at correct locations

  6. Waste drain positioned correctly (1-1/2 inch connection)

  7. Overflow drain connection ready

  8. Verify electrical rough-in complete: 240V dedicated circuit for pump (4KW)

  9. Floor must be level within ±3mm tolerance

7.2 Bath Tub Positioning

  1. Remove all protective packaging carefully

  2. Inspect bath tub for any damage or defects

  3. Use lifting straps or padded supports to protect acrylic surface

  4. Carefully maneuver bath tub into bathroom

  5. Position bath tub in designated location as per drawings

  6. Ensure adequate access clearance for service and maintenance

  7. Check bath tub is level in both directions using long spirit level

  8. Adjust feet/leveling system if provided by manufacturer

  9. If no feet provided, use mortar bed or adjustable supports under base

7.3 Drain Installation (KOHLER K-7223-PB)

  1. Install brass waste drain outlet through bath tub drain hole

  2. Apply plumber's putty under drain flange

  3. Thread drain strainer into drain body from top

  4. Tighten from underneath with appropriate wrench

  5. Install overflow assembly following manufacturer instructions

  6. Connect overflow tube to drain shoe (T-connection)

  7. Connect drain assembly to floor waste pipe (1-1/2 inch)

  8. Use appropriate P-trap with proper water seal depth

  9. Ensure all connections are watertight with rubber gaskets

7.4 Pump Installation (4KW, 240V)

  1. CRITICAL: Licensed electrician must perform all electrical connections

  2. Position pump motor at designated access panel location

  3. Verify pump is easily accessible for future service/maintenance

  4. Connect pump suction and discharge piping as per manufacturer diagram

  5. Install check valve in discharge line to prevent backflow

  6. Ensure pump is properly grounded per electrical code

  7. Connect dedicated 240V power supply with appropriate circuit breaker

  8. Install GFCI protection as required by code

  9. Test pump operation briefly (with water in system) before final connections

7.5 Final Installation and Sealing

  1. Apply silicone sealant around bath tub rim where it meets walls

  2. Use mildew-resistant sanitary silicone

  3. Tool joints smoothly for professional finish

  4. Install faucet/mixer assembly (coordinated with brass fittings team)

  5. Install access panel for pump service

  6. Clean bath tub interior with approved non-abrasive cleaner
  7. Leave protective covering on bath tub until final client handover
8. Shower Tray Installation (ROCA L5332)
8.1 Base Preparation

  1. Verify floor structure is level and properly waterproofed

  2. Check drain location matches shower tray outlet (typically center or corner)

  3. Ensure floor drain is at correct elevation to allow proper slope

  4. Clean installation area thoroughly

8.2 Shower Tray Installation

  1. Test-fit shower tray (90cm x 90cm) in designated location

  2. Ensure proper alignment with walls (equal gaps if recessed installation)

🔬 TESTING PROCEDURES

1. Pre-Test Preparation

1.1 Visual Inspection

  • Verify all drainage and waste piping installation is complete

  • Check that all piping is properly supported and anchored

1.2 System Preparation

  • Identify test sections and boundaries
  • Install test plugs at designated locations
  • Ensure all openings are properly plugged except for test connections
  • Install transparent standpipe at test point (minimum 3.5 meters height)
  • Secure standpipe vertically using proper supports
  • Connect standpipe to drainage system using flexible hose
  • Verify fixtures are accessible for flow testing
  • Have consultant present for witnessing (submit WIR in advance)

2. Water Column Test Procedure

2.1 Test Method (Water Column Pressure Test)

  • Connect standpipe to highest point of drainage system being tested
  • Fill standpipe slowly with clean water using funnel
  • Fill to exactly 3 meters height above top of horizontal drain pipe
  • Measure height accurately from top of pipe to water surface
  • Mark initial water level clearly on standpipe
  • Start timer immediately after reaching 3-meter height
  • Test duration: 15 minutes minimum
  • Observe water level throughout test period

2.2 Understanding Water Column Pressure

  • 3-meter water column = approximately 0.3 bar (4.35 psi) pressure
  • This pressure is applied at the bottom of the system
  • Water column test is equivalent to low-pressure air test
  • More practical than air test for drainage systems
  • Easier to detect leaks (water seepage visible immediately)

2.3 Acceptance Criteria

  • Water level must remain at 3-meter mark for minimum 15 minutes
  • Water level drop must not exceed 50mm (2 inches) during test
  • No visible water leaks at any joints or connections
  • No water seepage from pipe walls
  • No dampness appearing at joints
  • All test plugs remain secure and dry

2.4 If Test Fails

  • Note the water level drop and location of leaks
  • Mark all leak locations clearly
  • Drain water from system completely
  • Repair all leaks as required
  • Re-install test plugs if needed
  • Refill standpipe and re-test the repaired section
  • Repeat test until acceptance criteria is met

3. Water Flow Test Procedure

3.1 Test Method

  • After successful water column test, remove standpipe
  • Remove test plugs from system
  • Fill all fixtures and pour water down all drains simultaneously
  • Test each floor drain, sink, shower, and toilet drain
  • Run water at maximum flow rate for each fixture
  • Observe drainage performance at all points
  • Check for proper venting (gurgling indicates poor venting)
  • Verify all drains flow freely to main stack

3.2 Acceptance Criteria

  • Water drains freely without backup or overflow
  • No pooling or slow drainage at any fixture
  • No gurgling sounds indicating venting issues
  • No visible leaks at joints during flow test
  • All fixtures drain at design flow rates
  • Proper trap seal maintained at all fixtures
  • No cross-contamination between fixtures

3.3 If Test Fails

  • Identify blockage or restriction points
  • Check for improper slopes or backfall
  • Verify adequate venting is provided
  • Clear blockages and repair as needed
  • Adjust slopes if required
  • Re-test until acceptance criteria is met

6.2 Underground Drainage Installation (Basement)

Materials: UPVC Class-5 pipes with solvent cement joints (SAPPCO pipes + APLACO fittings)

A. PIPE CUTTING & PREPARATION

Measure accurately - Measure twice, cut once!

  • Account for socket depth in fittings (typically 40-60mm)
  • Cut perpendicular to pipe axis (90° angle)
  • Remove all burrs from inside and outside of cut edge
  • Chamfer outside edge at 10-15° angle
  • Clean cut surface with dry cloth
B. SOLVENT CEMENT JOINTING

⚠️ CRITICAL: Solvent welding creates permanent joints. No mistakes allowed!

  • Clean socket and spigot with dry cloth
  • Apply PVC cleaner/primer to remove grease, dust
  • Dry fit first to check fit and alignment
  • Apply solvent cement to spigot first, then socket
  • Insert pipe with slight twisting motion (¼ turn)
  • HOLD STEADY FOR 30 SECONDS MINIMUM (critical!)
  • Wipe excess solvent with rag
  • Curing time: Light duty 15 min, Water-tight 1 hour, Full cure 24 hours
C. PIPE LAYING & SUPPORT
  • Minimum slope: 1% (1:100) for 100mm pipes
  • Minimum slope: 1.25% (1:80) for pipes <100mm
  • Use laser level to check slope continuously
  • Support at every 1.0m maximum spacing
  • Install cleanouts at every direction change >45°
  • Cleanout should face upstream (against flow)
6.3 Vertical Stack Installation (Above Ground)

Materials: Silenta Soundproof System with push-fit rubber ring joints

A. PUSH-FIT JOINT PROCEDURE

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Unlike solvent joints, these can be disassembled if needed.

  • Clean socket and spigot end thoroughly
  • Check rubber ring is properly seated in socket groove
  • Apply rubber lubricant on spigot end (NOT oil or grease!)
  • Mark insertion depth on spigot
  • Push pipe into socket firmly until it stops
  • PULL BACK 10mm - allows for thermal expansion!
  • Wipe off excess lubricant
B. STACK ASSEMBLY
  • Start from basement and work upwards
  • Install base connection first (to horizontal underground drain)
  • Use 45° or 90° bend (45° preferred for smoother flow)
  • Install cleanout at base of stack
  • Add pipes section by section (max 3.0m per section)
  • Support every 2.0m vertically with heavy-duty pipe clamps
  • Check plumb every 2 floors with laser level
  • Connect branch pipes at each floor using 45° Wye fittings
  • NEVER use 90° Tee fitting for drainage (causes blockages)
C. VENT EXTENSION (Above Roof)
  • Extend stack minimum 150mm above roof finish
  • Install standard roof flashing/boot
  • Ensure watertight connection
  • Install vent cowl with insect screen
  • Keep 3m away from windows/air intakes.
7.0 TESTING & COMMISSIONING
7.1 Water Test (Underground & Concealed Piping)

Objective: Verify watertight integrity before backfilling/concealing

  • Ensure all joints have cured (24 hours for solvent joints)
  • Block all openings with test plugs
  • Fill system with clean water from highest point
  • Test pressure: Minimum 3.0m water head (0.3 Bar)
  • Test duration: 2 hours minimum
  • Acceptance: No visible leaks, water level drop max 10mm in 2 hours
  • Document with photos and test report
7.2 Air Pressure Test (Above-Ground Systems)

Objective: Test stack integrity without using water

  • Block all fixture openings with test plugs
  • Close all cleanouts tightly
  • Pump air to 350mm water gauge (35 mbar / 0.5 psi)
  • Hold pressure for 15 minutes
  • Acceptance: Pressure drop max 25mm water gauge
  • Check all joints with soapy water (bubbles indicate leak)
7.3 Performance Test (Final Functional Test)
  • Test each fixture individually - water should drain smoothly
  • No gurgling sounds (indicates vent problem)
  • Test multiple fixtures simultaneously
  • System should handle combined flow without backup
  • Check for sewer odors - none should be detectable
  • Verify all cleanouts can be opened and are accessible

7.2 Floor Drain Installation
7.2.1 Standard Floor Drain Installation (150×150mm)

**Step 1: Position Verification**
 - Mark exact drain location as per approved drawings
- Verify minimum 600mm clearance from walls/columns
- Check floor slope towards drain location (minimum 1% grade)
- Ensure drain is at lowest point of the area

**Step 2: Drain Body Installation**
 - Connect drain outlet to drainage piping using PVC solvent cement
- Apply PVC primer to both pipe and drain outlet
- Apply solvent cement liberally and join immediately
- Hold connection for 30 seconds, allow 2 hours cure time
- Install drain body vertically using spirit level
- Adjust height to match finished floor level (FFH)

**Step 3: Waterproofing Integration**
 - Coordinate with waterproofing contractor
- Install waterproofing membrane clamping ring (if applicable)
- Apply waterproofing membrane extending 150mm beyond drain perimeter
- Ensure membrane is properly bonded to drain body
- Test membrane integrity before proceeding

**Step 4: Concrete Work & Leveling**
 - Support drain body securely during concrete pouring
- Ensure drain remains level and at correct elevation
- For recessed drains: Set body 10-15mm below finished floor
- For flat drains: Set top flush with finished floor
- Create proper slope in concrete screed towards drain

**Step 5: Odour Trap Installation (Kessel Model 43500)**
 - Clean drain body interior thoroughly
- Install rubber gasket in drain body groove
- Insert odour trap unit into drain body
- Ensure trap is seated properly and sealed
- Check trap water level (minimum 50mm water seal)
- Fill trap with clean water

**Step 6: Grating Installation**
 - Clean drain frame and grating
- Inspect grating for damage or defects
- Install grating into frame ensuring proper seating
- For lockable gratings: Test locking mechanism
- Ensure grating sits flush with finished floor
- Check that grating can be easily removed for maintenance

7.2.2 Parking Area Drain Installation (Cast Iron, 381mm-D)
  • **Step 1:** Mark drain location at lowest point of parking area slope
  • **Step 2:** Ensure proper connection to 160mm drainage main line
  • **Step 3:** Set drain body at correct elevation for asphalt/concrete finish
  • **Step 4:** Install heavy-duty cast iron frame with traffic-rated grating
  • **Step 5:** Ensure frame is anchored securely to withstand vehicle loads
  • **Step 6:** Test grating stability and load-bearing capacity
  • **Step 7:** Apply bituminous coating to cast iron for corrosion protection
7.3 Linear/Channel Drain Installation

**Step 1: Channel Preparation**
 - Verify channel drain length against site measurements
- Check channel body for damage or defects
- Ensure all sections are same model and compatible
- Inspect stainless steel grating quality (SS-316)

**Step 2: Setting Out**
 - Mark channel drain centerline accurately
- Mark start and end points as per drawings
- Verify slope requirements (typically 1-2% slope)
- Ensure proper alignment with floor pattern

**Step 3: Channel Installation**
 - Connect channel outlet to drainage pipe (typically 50mm)
- Support channel body at 500mm intervals during installation
- Install channel sections in sequence ensuring proper alignment
- Use spirit level to verify slope and levelness across width
- Secure channel with concrete anchors or embedment

**Step 4: Waterproofing**
 - Coordinate waterproofing membrane installation
- Ensure membrane is properly sealed along channel edges
- Install membrane clamping system as per manufacturer
- Test waterproofing before floor finishing

**Step 5: Floor Finishing**
 - Set channel top edge flush with finished floor
- Create slope towards channel from both sides
- Ensure smooth transition between floor and channel edge
- Remove protective covering from stainless steel grating
- Install grating sections ensuring proper seating

**Step 6: Final Checks**
 - Verify channel is level across width
- Check longitudinal slope meets design requirements
- Test grating removal and reinstallation
- Ensure no gaps between floor and channel frame

7.4 Floor Cleanout Installation
  • **Step 1:** Identify cleanout locations as per drainage layout drawings
  • **Step 2:** Install cleanout body at strategic locations for drainage system access
  • **Step 3:** Ensure cleanout is accessible and not obstructed by furniture or fixtures
  • **Step 4:** Install cleanout at same level as floor drains or drainage mains
  • **Step 5:** Connect to drainage piping ensuring watertight seal
  • **Step 6:** Install cleanout cover plate flush with finished floor
  • **Step 7:** For recessed cleanouts (FD 7030 SR): Set 10mm below floor finish
  • **Step 8:** For flat cleanouts (FD 7034 SS): Set flush with floor finish

  • **Step 10:** Mark cleanout locations on as-built drawings
  • **Step 11:** Label cleanout with system identification tags
7.5 Drainage Piping Installation
  • Underground HDPE Piping (Basement & Parking)
  • Install HDPE pipes in properly bedded trenches with sand bedding
  • Maintain minimum slope of 1% for gravity drainage
  • Use electrofusion welding for all HDPE joints (qualified welder required)
  • Pressure test all welded joints before backfilling
  • Backfill in 150mm layers with proper compaction
  • Maintain minimum 300mm cover over pipes
  • Above-Slab PVC Piping
  • - Use Schedule 40 PVC pipes for all above-slab drainage
  • - Apply PVC primer followed by solvent cement for all joints
  • - Hold joints for minimum 30 seconds, cure for 2 hours before testing
  • - Support horizontal pipes at maximum 1.5m intervals
  • - Maintain minimum 2% slope for horizontal drainage runs
  • - Install cleanouts at all direction changes and maximum 15m intervals
  • Pipe Supports & Hangers
  • - Use stainless steel (SS-304) clamps and brackets
  • - Install at maximum 1.5m spacing for horizontal runs
  • - Use rubber-lined clamps to prevent pipe abrasion
  • - Secure supports with concrete anchors or expansion bolts
  • - Allow for thermal expansion in long runs
  • Penetrations Through Structure
  • - Use properly sized sleeves for all structural penetrations
  • - Seal penetrations with fire-rated sealant (where required)
  • - Maintain minimum clearance around pipes in sleeves
  • - Coordinate with structural engineer for slab penetrations

  • Install air release valve at highest point
  • Prepare water source for filling system
  • Have consultant present for witnessing (submit WIR in advance)

2. Hydrostatic Pressure Test Procedure

2.1 System Filling

  • Connect water source to lowest point of system
  • Open air release valve at highest point
  • Begin filling system slowly with clean water
  • Allow air to vent completely through release valve
  • Continue filling until water flows from air release valve
  • Close air release valve when system is completely filled
  • Verify no air pockets remain in system

2.2 Test Method (Per ASTM F2389 & DIN 8078)

  • Connect hydrostatic test pump to test point
  • System working pressure: Typically 6-10 bar (87-145 psi)
  • Test pressure: 1.5 times working pressure
  • Example: If working pressure = 10 bar, test pressure = 15 bar (217 psi)
  • Gradually increase pressure over 5-10 minutes
  • Do NOT shock load - increase pressure smoothly
  • Monitor for leaks during pressurization
  • Hold at test pressure for minimum 60 minutes
  • Record pressure at start and end of test period
  • Monitor system continuously throughout test

2.3 Temperature Compensation

  • PPR pipes expand/contract with temperature changes
  • Pressure readings will fluctuate with temperature
  • Record ambient temperature at start and end of test
  • Pressure may drop 1-2 psi per 10°F temperature drop
  • This is normal and acceptable - not a leak
  • Conduct test during stable temperature conditions if possible

2.4 Acceptance Criteria

  • System must hold test pressure for minimum 60 minutes
  • Pressure drop must not exceed 2% (excluding temperature effects)
  • No visible leaks at any joints, fittings, or connections
  • No weeping or moisture at fusion joints
  • No deformation or damage to piping
  • All valves operate properly under pressure

2.5 If Test Fails

  • Depressurize system slowly and safely
  • Drain water from system if needed to locate leaks
  • Inspect all fusion joints carefully for defects
  • Check all threaded connections and valve joints
  • Mark all leak locations clearly
  • Cut out and re-fuse any defective joints
  • Replace any damaged fittings or valves
  • Re-fill, re-pressurize, and re-test
  • Repeat until acceptance criteria is met

2.6 Post-Test Activities

  • After successful test, gradually depressurize system
  • Drain test water from system completely
  • Remove test caps/plugs
  • Re-install permanent caps or connect to fixtures/equipment
  • System is now ready for insulation (if required)
  • Proceed to final connections and commissioning

1. Hydrostatic Pressure Testing
  • Close all drain valves and isolation valves
  • Fill system slowly with clean water, venting air at high points
  • Pressurize system to 1.5 × working pressure (15 bar for 10 bar rated system)
  • Maintain test pressure for minimum 2 hours
  • Inspect all joints, connections, and equipment for leaks
  • Pressure drop must not exceed 0.2 bar over 2-hour period
  • Document test results with pressure readings at 15-minute intervals
  • Repair any leaks found and re-test
  • Obtain consultant approval on pressure test results
2. Sediment & Carbon Filter Testing
  • Verify inlet pressure matches design (typically 2-4 bar)
  • Measure differential pressure across each filter
  • Initial ΔP should be < 0.3 bar with new cartridges
  • Check for bypass flow (all flow must pass through filters)
  • Verify isolation valves operate correctly
  • Test parallel operation (operate one filter, keep other isolated)
  • Test switchover procedure between filters
  • Measure outlet turbidity: Must be < 5 NTU
  • For carbon filter: Test chlorine removal (outlet < 0.1 mg/L)
  • Document filter performance data
3. Water Softener Performance Testing
  • Verify CLACK WS1 controller powers on and displays correctly
  • Program controller with project parameters:
  • - Resin capacity: 170 liters
  • - Regeneration volume: Per water hardness test results
  • - Regeneration time: Typically midnight (off-peak)
  • - Backwash duration: 5 minutes
  • - Brine draw duration: 30 minutes
  • - Fast rinse duration: 10 minutes
  • - Brine refill duration: 10 minutes
  • Test incoming water hardness (typical: 20-30 GPG)
  • Initiate manual regeneration cycle to verify operation:
  • - Stage 1: Backwash - verify water flows upward through resin
  • - Stage 2: Brine draw - verify brine solution draws from tank
  • - Stage 3: Slow rinse - verify proper rinse flow
  • - Stage 4: Fast rinse - verify high-flow rinse
  • - Stage 5: Brine refill - verify float valve operation
  • Measure softened water hardness: Must be < 1 GPG
  • Verify drain line handles backwash flow without flooding
  • Test bypass valve operation
  • Measure flow rate through softener: Target 20 GPM
  • Check for resin carryover in outlet water (visual inspection)
  • Verify control valve responds to manual commands
  • Test flow meter accuracy by comparing to external measurement
  • Document all test results and water quality parameters
4. UV Disinfection System Testing
  • Verify electrical connections and grounding
  • Power on UV lamp and verify illumination
  • Allow 5-minute warm-up period for lamp stabilization
  • Verify UV intensity meter shows reading in acceptable range
  • (Typically > 60% of new lamp intensity)
  • Test UV lamp shut-off when water flow stops (if equipped)
  • Test bypass valve operation for UV maintenance
  • Verify isolation valves operate smoothly
  • Check water flow rate through UV: Target 50 GPM
  • Verify UV lamp life indicator functions correctly
  • Verify alarm activates if UV intensity drops below setpoint
  • Document UV intensity reading and lamp serial number
5. Integrated System Testing

Run complete water treatment cycle:

  • Water enters sediment filters → measure pressure & turbidity

  • Water passes through carbon filter → test chlorine removal

  • Water enters softener → test hardness reduction

  • Water passes through UV → verify disinfection

    Measure key parameters at each stage:

  • Inlet: Pressure, hardness, chlorine, turbidity

  • After sediment: Turbidity, pressure

  • After carbon: Chlorine level, pressure

  • After softener: Hardness, pressure, TDS

  • After UV: Final water quality, pressure

    Verify system meets design specifications:

  • Flow rate: 50 GPM at softener inlet

  • Softened water hardness: < 1 GPG

  • Chlorine: < 0.1 mg/L

  • Turbidity: < 5 NTU

  • UV intensity: > 60% of rated

    Test automatic regeneration sequence activation

  • Verify all alarms and safety interlocks function

  • Test system response to various flow conditions

  • Simulate failure modes and verify system protection

  • Run system continuously for 24 hours to verify stability

  • Document all integrated system test results

2. EQUIPMENT INSTALLATION

2.1 Sediment Filter Installation (SCF-1 & SCF-2)

  • Transport filter housings carefully to installation location
  • Install mounting brackets securely to wall or floor structure
  • Mount filter housings vertically using provided brackets
  • Ensure housings are plumb using spirit level
  • Install inlet and outlet pressure gauges (0-16 bar range)

  • Install drain valves (DR-01, DR-02 for SCF-1; DR-03, DR-04 for SCF-2)

2.2 Activated Carbon Filter Installation (CCF-01)
  • Mount carbon filter housing vertically downstream of sediment filters
  • Secure housing to wall/floor using mounting brackets
  • Verify housing is level and plumb
  • Install inlet and outlet pressure gauges
  • Install isolation valves (GV-31 inlet, GV-32 outlet)
  • Install drain valves (DR-05, DR-06)
  • Label housing clearly as "CCF-01 - Carbon Filter"
  • Ensure easy access for cartridge replacement
2.3 Water Softener Tank Installation (WS-01)
  • Position softener tank (16" dia × 65" height) on level floor
  • Check tank is perfectly vertical using spirit level
  • Ensure minimum clearance of 600mm around tank for access
  • Verify tank is positioned to allow control valve installation on top
  • Do not remove protective caps until control valve installation
  • Check resin bed level inside tank (if visible through inspection port)
  • Secure tank base to floor if required to prevent movement
2.4 Control Valve Installation (CLACK WS1)
  • Carefully remove protective caps from softener tank top
  • Clean tank opening threads thoroughly
  • Apply thread sealant tape (Teflon) to tank threads
  • Mount CLACK WS1 control valve on tank top
  • Tighten control valve hand-tight, then wrench-tight per manufacturer specs
  • Ensure control valve is oriented correctly (inlet/outlet positions)
  • Install pressure gauge on control valve inlet (0-10 bar)
  • Connect 1" inlet and outlet piping connections
  • Do not energize controller until system is filled with water
2.5 Brine Tank Installation (BT-01)
  • Position brine tank (18" dia × 33" height) adjacent to softener
  • Ensure tank is level and stable
  • Install float valve assembly inside brine tank
  • Adjust float valve to maintain proper water level (per manufacturer)
  • Connect brine line from control valve to brine tank
  • Connect overflow/drain line from brine tank to drainage
  • Ensure brine tank has easy access for salt addition
  • Label tank clearly as "BT-01 - Brine Tank"
2.6 UV Disinfection System Installation (UV-01)
  • Mount UV unit horizontally downstream of softener
  • Secure UV unit using provided mounting brackets
  • Install isolation valves (GV-43 inlet, GV-45 outlet)
  • Install bypass valve (GV-44) for maintenance
  • Connect electrical supply to UV lamp ballast
  • Install UV intensity meter (Model 212300) in visible location
  • Connect intensity meter sensor to UV chamber
  • Label unit clearly as "UV-01 - UV Disinfection"
  • Ensure easy access for lamp replacement

TESTING & COMMISSIONING
Pre-Commissioning Verification

1. Hydrostatic Pressure Testing
  • Close all drain valves and isolation valves
  • Fill system slowly with clean water, venting air at high points
  • Pressurize system to 1.5 × working pressure (15 bar for 10 bar rated system)
  • Maintain test pressure for minimum 2 hours
  • Inspect all joints, connections, and equipment for leaks
  • Pressure drop must not exceed 0.2 bar over 2-hour period
  • Document test results with pressure readings at 15-minute intervals
  • Repair any leaks found and re-test
  • Obtain consultant approval on pressure test results
2. Sediment & Carbon Filter Testing
  • Verify inlet pressure matches design (typically 2-4 bar)
  • Measure differential pressure across each filter
  • Initial ΔP should be < 0.3 bar with new cartridges
  • Check for bypass flow (all flow must pass through filters)
  • Verify isolation valves operate correctly
  • Test parallel operation (operate one filter, keep other isolated)
  • Test switchover procedure between filters
  • Measure outlet turbidity: Must be < 5 NTU
  • For carbon filter: Test chlorine removal (outlet < 0.1 mg/L)
  • Document filter performance data
3. Water Softener Performance Testing
  • Verify CLACK WS1 controller powers on and displays correctly
  • Program controller with project parameters:
  • - Resin capacity: 170 liters
  • - Regeneration volume: Per water hardness test results
  • - Regeneration time: Typically midnight (off-peak)
  • - Backwash duration: 5 minutes
  • - Brine draw duration: 30 minutes
  • - Fast rinse duration: 10 minutes
  • - Brine refill duration: 10 minutes
  • Test incoming water hardness (typical: 20-30 GPG)
  • Initiate manual regeneration cycle to verify operation:
  • - Stage 1: Backwash - verify water flows upward through resin
  • - Stage 2: Brine draw - verify brine solution draws from tank
  • - Stage 3: Slow rinse - verify proper rinse flow
  • - Stage 4: Fast rinse - verify high-flow rinse
  • - Stage 5: Brine refill - verify float valve operation
  • Measure softened water hardness: Must be < 1 GPG
  • Verify drain line handles backwash flow without flooding
  • Test bypass valve operation
  • Measure flow rate through softener: Target 20 GPM
  • Check for resin carryover in outlet water (visual inspection)
  • Verify control valve responds to manual commands
  • Test flow meter accuracy by comparing to external measurement
  • Document all test results and water quality parameters
4. UV Disinfection System Testing
  • Verify electrical connections and grounding
  • Power on UV lamp and verify illumination
  • Allow 5-minute warm-up period for lamp stabilization
  • Verify UV intensity meter shows reading in acceptable range
  • (Typically > 60% of new lamp intensity)
  • Test UV lamp shut-off when water flow stops (if equipped)
  • Test bypass valve operation for UV maintenance
  • Verify isolation valves operate smoothly
  • Check water flow rate through UV: Target 50 GPM
  • Verify UV lamp life indicator functions correctly
  • Verify alarm activates if UV intensity drops below setpoint
  • Document UV intensity reading and lamp serial number
5. Integrated System Testing

Run complete water treatment cycle:

  • Water enters sediment filters → measure pressure & turbidity

  • Water passes through carbon filter → test chlorine removal

  • Water enters softener → test hardness reduction

  • Water passes through UV → verify disinfection

    Measure key parameters at each stage:

  • Inlet: Pressure, hardness, chlorine, turbidity

  • After sediment: Turbidity, pressure

  • After carbon: Chlorine level, pressure

  • After softener: Hardness, pressure, TDS

  • After UV: Final water quality, pressure

    Verify system meets design specifications:

  • Flow rate: 50 GPM at softener inlet

  • Softened water hardness: < 1 GPG

  • Chlorine: < 0.1 mg/L

  • Turbidity: < 5 NTU

  • UV intensity: > 60% of rated

    Test automatic regeneration sequence activation

  • Verify all alarms and safety interlocks function

  • Test system response to various flow conditions

  • Simulate failure modes and verify system protection

  • Run system continuously for 24 hours to verify stability

  • Document all integrated system test results

6.2 Pipe Cutting and Preparation

CRITICAL: Proper cutting and preparation ensure leak-free fusion joints.

A. Measuring and Marking

  • Measure required pipe length allowing for fitting insertion depth
  • Mark cutting line using a square to ensure 90° cut
  • Double-check measurements before cutting - PPR pipes cannot be re-used once cut

B. Cutting

  • Use PPR pipe cutter or fine-tooth saw for clean cuts
  • Cut must be perfectly square (90°) to ensure proper joint
  • Avoid crushing or deforming the pipe during cutting

C. Deburring and Cleaning

  • Remove all burrs and rough edges using deburring tool or sandpaper
  • Clean pipe end and fitting socket with alcohol or approved cleaner
  • Wipe dry with lint-free cloth - any contamination prevents proper fusion
  • Mark insertion depth on pipe using marker (based on pipe diameter)

6.3 Socket Fusion Welding Process

IMPORTANT: Fusion welding creates a monolithic joint stronger than the pipe itself when done correctly.

A. Machine Preparation

  • Set fusion machine temperature to 260°C (±5°C)
  • Allow machine to heat for minimum 15 minutes until green light indicates ready
  • Install correct size heating sleeve (mandrel) and socket on machine
  • Clean heating elements with cotton cloth before each use

B. Heating Phase

  • Insert pipe end into heating mandrel up to marked depth
  • Simultaneously insert fitting socket onto heating socket
  • Apply light pressure - do not force
  • Hold for specified heating time (see heating time table below)
  • Watch for uniform melt bead forming around pipe and fitting

C. Joining Phase

  • Remove pipe and fitting from machine simultaneously
  • CRITICAL: Join within 4 seconds of removing from heat
  • Insert pipe into fitting with firm, steady pressure
  • Push to full insertion depth (until pipe contacts fitting shoulder)
  • Hold joint straight and motionless during cooling time
  • NO ROTATION allowed after insertion begins

D. Cooling Phase

  • Hold joint absolutely still for specified cooling time (see table)
  • Do not stress, bend, or apply load to joint during cooling
  • Check for uniform bead around joint circumference
  • Wait for full cooling before handling or continuing installation

Proper support prevents sagging, maintains slope, and reduces stress on joints.

A. Support Spacing

B. Support Types and Installation

  • Trapeze hangers with galvanized steel split clamps (lined with rubber)
  • All-thread rods suspended from ceiling structure or beams
  • Clamps must not compress pipe - allow for thermal expansion
  • Install support at every change of direction and before/after valves
  • Vertical risers: Support at each floor with guide clamps
  • Allow for thermal expansion: PPR expands 0.15mm per meter per 10°C

6.6 Valve Installation

A. Isolation Valves

  • Install at each fixture branch line for individual isolation
  • Install at zone isolation points (one per floor minimum)
  • Use PPR-to-threaded adapters for valve connections
  • Apply PTFE tape 3-4 wraps clockwise on male threads
  • Valve handle should be accessible and not obstructed

B. Pressure Reducing Valves (PRV)

  • Install one PRV per floor on cold water main line
  • Set outlet pressure to 3.5 bar using adjustment screw
  • Install pressure gauge on outlet side to verify setting
  • Provide isolation valves on both inlet and outlet sides
  • Install bypass line with valve for maintenance access

C. Thermal Balancing Valves (Danfoss MCTV)

  • Install on hot water return lines at strategic locations
  • Follow manufacturer's commissioning procedure for flow balancing
  • Ensure correct flow direction (arrow marking on valve body)

6.7 Insulation Installation

Hot water supply and return lines must be insulated to reduce heat loss and improve energy efficiency.

A. Preparation

  • Clean pipe surface - remove dust, dirt, or oil
  • Complete all pressure testing before insulation
  • Allow pipes to cool to room temperature

B. Installation Process

  • Use pre-slit closed-cell rubber foam tubes (20mm thickness)
  • Slide insulation over pipe ensuring tight fit
  • Apply contact adhesive to slit edges before closing
  • Press slit closed firmly for proper seal
  • Butt joints tightly together - no gaps allowed
  • Wrap all joints and seams with PVC tape (50mm overlap)

C. Fittings and Valves

  • Cut insulation at 45° angles for elbows and tees
  • Ensure complete coverage with no exposed pipe
  • Leave valve handles exposed for operation
  • Use pre-molded insulation for valves where possible

7. TESTING & COMMISSIONING

7.1 Visual Inspection

Objective: Verify installation quality before pressure testing

  • Check all fusion joints for uniform bead formation
  • Verify proper pipe support spacing and alignment
  • Confirm all valves are installed in correct orientation
  • Ensure pipe penetrations through walls/floors are properly sealed
  • Check insulation completeness (no gaps or exposed areas)

Acceptance: Pass visual inspection before proceeding to pressure test

7.2 Hydrostatic Pressure Test

Objective: Verify system integrity and leak-free installation

A. Test Preparation

  1. Close all valves and install test plugs at open ends
  2. Fill system slowly with clean water (potable quality)
  3. Vent all air from high points using air release valves
  4. Allow system to stabilize at ambient temperature (minimum 2 hours)

B. Test Execution

  1. Connect calibrated test pump and pressure gauge
  2. Slowly increase pressure to 1.5 times working pressure (15 bar for SDR 7.3)
  3. Hold test pressure for minimum 1 hour
  4. Inspect all joints, fittings, and connections for leaks
  5. Monitor pressure gauge - no pressure drop allowed
  6. Document test results with photos and signed test report

Acceptance Criteria:

  • No visible leaks at any joint or connection
  • Maximum pressure drop: 0.5 bar over 1 hour period
  • All joints remain dry and intact

7.3 System Flushing

Objective: Remove construction debris and contaminants

  • Flush system with clean water at maximum flow rate
  • Flush each branch line individually until water runs clear
  • Continue flushing for minimum 10 minutes per branch
  • Check water quality visually - no discoloration or particles
  • Drain and refill system with fresh water

Acceptance: Clear water flow with no visible contaminants

7.4 Hot Water Circulation Testing

Objective: Verify proper hot water circulation and temperature maintenance

  • Start hot water heaters and bring water to 60°C
  • Start hot water circulation pumps (HWRP-1)
  • Verify proper flow direction through return line
  • Check temperature at furthest fixture - should reach 55°C within 2 minutes
  • Adjust thermal balancing valves to achieve uniform temperature distribution
  • Test standby pump (HWRP-2) automatic switchover

Acceptance: All fixtures receive hot water within 2 minutes with temperature above 55°C

  • Apply Lock-Out Tag-Out (LOTO) on isolation valves

2. PRV Installation

2.1 Pipe Preparation

  • Cut pipe at marked location using pipe cutter
  • Ensure cut is square and clean
  • Deburr pipe ends thoroughly
  • Clean pipe threads (if threaded connection)
  • Apply PTFE tape or approved thread sealant
  • Check pipe alignment and levelness

2.2 PRV Installation Steps

Install PRV following this sequence:

  1. 1. Verify flow direction arrow on PRV body matches water flow direction
  2. 2. Install unions on inlet and outlet sides of PRV
  3. 3. Position PRV at marked location
  4. 4. For ceiling installation, ensure proper support is provided
  5. 5. Install PRV with adjustment knob/screw accessible for maintenance
  6. 6. Recommended orientation: Horizontal pipe, PRV cap vertical (preferred)
  7. 7. Alternative: Vertical pipe installation is acceptable
  8. 8. Maintain minimum 5 × DN straight pipe length after PRV
  9. 9. Tighten unions hand-tight first, then use wrench
  10. 10. Torque as per manufacturer recommendations
  11. 11. Do NOT over-tighten to avoid thread damage
  12. 12. Ensure PRV body is not stressed by pipe misalignment
  13. 13. Install isolation valves before and after PRV (if not existing)
  14. 14. Install pressure gauge on outlet side (recommended)
  15. 15. Label valves with identification tags

2.3 Support Installation

  • Install pipe supports/hangers on both sides of PRV
  • Support spacing: Maximum 2 meters for horizontal runs
  • Use vibration isolation supports if required
  • Ensure supports do not stress the PRV body
  • Allow for thermal expansion (hot water lines)
  • Secure all supports to ceiling structure adequately

2.4 Insulation (Hot Water Lines Only)

  • Clean and dry pipe surface
  • Apply pipe insulation to hot water lines
  • Insulation thickness as per specifications
  • Ensure insulation covers all fittings
  • Leave PRV adjustment mechanism accessible
  • Seal insulation joints properly
  • Install vapor barrier on insulation exterior

3. Pressure Adjustment & Calibration

3.1 Initial Setting Verification

  • Factory preset is 3 bar (standard)
  • If different pressure required, adjust as follows
  • Ensure downstream isolation valve is CLOSED
  • Gradually open upstream isolation valve
  • Monitor inlet pressure on upstream gauge
  • Check PRV is not leaking

3.2 Pressure Adjustment Procedure

Close downstream valve completely

Open upstream valve fully

To INCREASE outlet pressure:

  • Turn adjustment screw clockwise (+)
  • Open and close downstream valve briefly
  • Check outlet pressure on gauge
  • Repeat until desired pressure is achieved

To DECREASE outlet pressure:

  • Turn adjustment screw counter-clockwise (-)
  • Open and close downstream valve briefly
  • Check outlet pressure on gauge
  • Repeat until desired pressure is achieved

Typical outlet pressure setting: 3-4 bar

Record final pressure setting

Lock adjustment screw if lock nut provided

2. Hydrostatic Pressure Test

  • Isolate PRV section with upstream and downstream valves
  • Fill system with clean water
  • Remove all air from system
  • Connect calibrated pressure gauge
  • Pressurize to 1.5 × working pressure (but not exceeding PRV rating)
  • Test pressure: Approximately 6-7 bar (if working pressure is 4-5 bar)
  • Hold pressure for minimum 30 minutes
  • Monitor for pressure drop
  • Inspect all connections for leaks
  • Acceptable leakage: Zero leakage
  • Record test pressure, duration, and results
  • Depressurize system slowly after test

3. PRV Performance Testing

  • Open upstream isolation valve fully
  • Record inlet pressure
  • Gradually open downstream valve
  • Monitor outlet pressure with varying flow rates
  • Outlet pressure should remain stable (±0.2 bar variation)
  • Test at minimum flow (single fixture)
  • Test at design flow (multiple fixtures)
  • Test at maximum flow (all fixtures open)
  • Verify pressure reduction is maintained
  • Check for noise or vibration
  • Noise level should be <20 dB
  • Record all test results

4. System Integration Testing

  • Test PRV operation with complete water system
  • Verify all fixtures receive adequate pressure
  • Check system operates correctly in all modes
  • Test simultaneous operation of multiple zones
  • Verify no water hammer or pressure surges
  • Check safety relief valve operation (if installed)
  • Test isolation valves for proper operation
  • Verify no cross-connection between cold and hot water
  • Document any issues and corrective actions

5. Final Verification

  • All tests completed successfully
  • All leaks identified and repaired
  • Outlet pressure stable at set value
  • System operates quietly without noise
  • All documentation completed
  • Client/consultant walkthrough completed
  • Training provided to maintenance staff
  • Obtain final sign-off
