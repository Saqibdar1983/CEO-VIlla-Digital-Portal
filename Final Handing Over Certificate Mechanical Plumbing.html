2. Scope of Handover

The following Mechanical Plumbing System components, covered under this handover, have been installed, tested and commissioned in accordance with the approved drawings, technical specifications, and project requirements:

  • Cold & Hot Water Supply System: Water Supply Booster Pumps, Hot Water Circulation Pumps, distribution piping & fixtures
  • Drainage & Sanitary System: Sewerage drainage pumps, sanitary fixtures, sewerage network
  • Storm / Rain Water Drainage System: Sump pits, submersible drainage pumps, float-switch controls
  • Water Treatment & Softening System: Water Softener Plant, sediment / carbon filtration, UV disinfection
  • Electric Water Heating System: Electric Water Heaters and re-circulation network
  • Cold Shower Chiller System & Cold Shower System
  • Irrigation System: Distribution network and irrigation pumps
  • Controls & Automation: Associated valves, control panels, instrumentation, and BMS monitoring points
3. Documents & Records Handed Over

The following documents and records have been compiled and are being handed over together with this certificate:

  • Approved shop drawings, single line diagrams (SLD/SDL) and as-built drawing register
  • Equipment schedules — specified, selected and name-plate data for major plumbing equipment
  • Operation & Maintenance (O&M) manuals for installed equipment
  • Testing & Commissioning (T&C) reports and pre-commissioning checklists
  • Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM) schedules and check-lists
  • Recommended Spare Parts List (RSPL)
  • Manufacturer / supplier contact directories and warranty information
  • Asset register and purchase order records
4. Declaration
This is to certify that the Mechanical Plumbing System for the CEO Villa Project (Project No. 10144), Jeddah, has been completed, inspected, tested and commissioned in accordance with the approved drawings, project specifications, and applicable codes and standards and is hereby formally handed over in good working condition to the Client / Facility Management team for operation and maintenance.

By signing below, the undersigned representatives acknowledge receipt of the system described above, together with the supporting documentation listed in Section 3, and confirm acceptance of the handover on behalf of their respective organizations.
