2. Scope of Handover

The following Mechanical HVAC System components, covered under this handover, have been installed, tested and commissioned in accordance with the approved drawings, technical specifications, and project requirements:

  • VRF System: Indoor Units (FXAQ Wall Mounted & FXSQ-A Ceiling Concealed) and Outdoor Units (RXYTQ series)
  • Fresh Air Handling Unit (FAHU): Associated ductwork & controls
  • Hood Make-Up Unit (HMU): Kitchen exhaust make-up air system
  • Package Air Conditioning Units: BARD DP15CH series & DCC102 serving plant / utility areas
  • Split Air Conditioning (SAC) Units
  • Ventilation Fans: Supply & exhaust fans, FAF / EXF series, ducting & accessories
  • Variable Air Volume (VAV) boxes: Associated duct heaters
  • Controls & Integration: Associated controls, BMS interface points, and electrical connections to mechanical equipment
3. Documents & Records Handed Over

The following documents and records have been compiled and are being handed over together with this certificate:

  • Approved shop drawings, single line diagrams (SLD/SDL) and as-built drawing register
  • Equipment schedules — specified, selected and name-plate data for major HVAC equipment
  • Operation & Maintenance (O&M) manuals for installed equipment
  • Testing & Commissioning (T&C) reports, air balancing reports and pre-commissioning checklists
  • Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM) schedules and check-lists
  • Equipment error-code / troubleshooting references
  • Manufacturer / supplier / vendor contact directories and warranty information
  • Purchase order records and as-built drawing register
4. Declaration
This is to certify that the Mechanical HVAC System for the CEO Villa Project (Project No. 10144), Jeddah, has been completed, inspected, tested and commissioned in accordance with the approved drawings, project specifications, and applicable codes and standards and is hereby formally handed over in good working condition to the Client / Facility Management team for operation and maintenance.

By signing below, the undersigned representatives acknowledge receipt of the system described above, together with the supporting documentation listed in Section 3, and confirm acceptance of the handover on behalf of their respective organizations.
