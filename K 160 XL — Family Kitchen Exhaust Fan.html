Troubleshooting Checklist

Symptoms • Causes • Corrective Actions
SymptomNo.Possible CauseCorrective Action
Fan does not start1No power supply to fan.Check incoming power supply, isolator, fuse, circuit breaker and terminal connections. Restore correct voltage before operation.
2Loose, disconnected or incorrect wiring.Isolate power, inspect wiring against approved wiring diagram and reconnect securely.
3Integral thermal contact has tripped due to motor overheating.Allow motor to cool, reset as per fan/control arrangement, then identify cause of overheating before restarting.
4Speed controller, 5-step transformer or control device faulty/not set.Check controller output voltage and control setting. Repair or replace controller if no output is available.
5Motor winding damaged or motor defective.Check winding resistance/insulation. Contact Systemair/service provider or replace motor/fan assembly as approved.
Fan stops during operation1Motor overheated and thermal protection operated.Check airflow, ambient temperature, duct blockage, fan rotation and electrical load. Reset only after the fault is corrected.
2Voltage fluctuation, low voltage or power interruption.Measure supply voltage during operation and correct power supply issue.
3Controller overloaded or unsuitable for fan motor.Verify controller capacity/type is suitable for K 160 XL Sileo. Replace with approved speed control device.
4Excessive duct resistance causing high motor load.Inspect duct, damper, filter, grille and silencer. Remove obstruction or rebalance system.
Low or insufficient airflow1Incorrect rotation direction or wrong electrical connection.Confirm fan rotation/airflow direction against arrow on casing and correct wiring if required.
2Blocked duct, dirty grille, closed damper or clogged filter.Clean duct/grille/filter and open/balance dampers.
3Fan speed set too low.Increase speed through approved speed controller or transformer setting within design limits.
4High external static pressure due to long duct route or added accessories.Measure static pressure and compare with fan selection. Reduce resistance or review fan selection.
5Air leakage in duct or poor connection to fan spigot.Inspect joints, clamps and flexible connections. Seal leakage and tighten clamps.
Excessive noise1Fan running at high speed or system pressure too high.Check speed setting and system static pressure. Adjust speed or rebalance duct system.
2Fan not properly fixed or bracket/clamp loose.Tighten mounting bracket, screws and FK clamps. Ensure fan is securely supported.
3Vibration transferred to ductwork.Install or check flexible mounting clamps/anti-vibration arrangement. Avoid rigid stress on fan casing.
4Foreign object inside impeller or duct.Isolate power, open access, remove debris and inspect impeller for damage.
5Bearing or motor noise.Check motor/bearing sound. If abnormal, replace fan/motor assembly or contact service.
Excessive vibration1Impeller dirty, damaged or unbalanced.Clean impeller carefully. If damaged or still vibrating, replace impeller/fan assembly.
2Fan installed under duct stress or misalignment.Release duct stress, realign duct connections and ensure fan casing is not twisted.
3Loose support, mounting bracket or fasteners.Tighten all supports, brackets, clamps and fixing screws.
4Air turbulence near fan inlet/outlet.Check installation arrangement. Provide proper straight duct/accessory arrangement as practical.
Motor overheating1Insufficient airflow over motor due to blockage or closed damper.Clean duct/filter/grille and ensure dampers are open. Restore required airflow.
2Fan operating outside selected duty point.Measure airflow and static pressure. Compare with approved selection and correct system resistance.
3Incorrect voltage or unstable supply.Measure voltage and current. Correct power supply before restarting.
4Ambient temperature too high or unsuitable installation location.Check installation condition and ventilation around fan. Relocate or improve ventilation if required.
5Frequent starts/stops or unsuitable control method.Review control sequence and speed controller. Use approved control arrangement only.
Controller/speed control problem1Stepless thyristor or 5-step transformer not compatible or undersized.Verify controller type and rating against fan motor data. Replace with approved Systemair-compatible controller.
2Control setting too low causing unstable operation.Increase setting to stable operating range and confirm fan starts/runs reliably.
3Loose control wiring or poor terminal connection.Isolate power and tighten terminals. Check continuity of control wiring.
4Controller failure.Check input/output voltage at controller. Replace controller if faulty.
Water ingress / outdoor installation issue1Fan installed outdoors without proper weather protection or incorrect cable entry.Check installation orientation, cable gland and terminal box sealing. Correct water path and reseal.
2Condensation or moisture accumulation in duct.Check duct insulation, drainage and installation slope. Correct condensation source.
3Terminal box cover not properly closed.Isolate power, dry components if needed and refit cover/gasket correctly.
Electrical protection trips1Short circuit or insulation fault.Megger test motor and cable. Repair cable or replace motor/fan if insulation is poor.
2Overload due to blocked impeller or high static pressure.Inspect impeller and duct system. Remove blockage and recheck running current.
3Wrong fuse/MCB rating or incorrect protection setting.Check protection rating against approved electrical data and adjust/replace as per electrical design.
4Motor fault.Check current, winding resistance and thermal contact. Contact service or replace fan/motor assembly.
Poor performance after maintenance1Fan reinstalled in wrong direction.Check airflow arrow on casing and reinstall fan in correct direction.
2Duct clamp or joint not sealed after removal.Refit clamps and seal duct joints properly.
3Accessory installed incorrectly, such as filter, silencer, grille or damper.Check accessory orientation and cleanliness. Correct installation and rebalance airflow.
4Speed controller setting changed.Restore approved commissioning speed setting and record final value.
Prepared for site use. Verify final action with approved submittal, wiring diagram, and project safety procedure.
