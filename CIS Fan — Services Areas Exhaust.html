Troubleshooting Checklist

Symptoms • Causes • Corrective Actions
SymptomNo.Possible CauseCorrective Action
Excessive Noise1Defective or loose motor bearings.Replace motor with same frame size, RPM, HP.
2Ventilator base not securely anchored.Reset properly.
3Loose or unbalanced wheel/propeller.Tighten screws, remove build-up, balance wheel/propeller.
4Misaligned pulleys or shaft.Correct alignment.
5Loose or damaged wheel/propeller.Replace wheel/propeller.
6Wheel running in wrong direction.Reverse direction.
Fan Inoperative1Blown fuse or open circuit breaker.Replace fuses or circuit breaker.
2Loose or disconnected wiring.Shut off power and check wiring for proper connections.
3Defective motor.Repair or replace motor.
4Broken belts.Replace belts.
Insufficient Airflow1Open access doors or loose sections of ducts.Check for leakage.
2Clogged filters.Clean filters.
3Operation in wrong direction.Correct rotation of wheel.
4Insufficient make-up air direction.Add make-up fan or louver opening.
Water Leaking into Ductwork or Collection of Grease Under Fan1Fan installed with slope in the wrong direction.Slope should be fitted in the direction of the drainage opening or grease collection box and drain spout.
2Clogged drain spout.Clean drain spout.
3Cooling tube or motor dome top removed.Install new cooling tube with gasket and dome top.
4Grease container full.Empty grease box.
Motor Overheating1Belt slippage.Adjust tension or replace belts.
2Overvoltage or under voltage.Contact power supply company.
3Operation in wrong direction.Reverse direction of motor.
4Fan speed too high.Slow down fan by opening variable pitch pulley on motor shaft.
5Incorrect motor (service factor 1.0, low ambient temp.).Replace motor with correct open, NEMA service factors (1.15 or higher) with 40 degrees ambient.
6Blocked cooling tube or leaky gasket.Remove blockage and seal cooling tube in place.
7Insufficient airflow to kitchen hood fan operating on low speed with kitchen in full operation.Check airflow under hood and adjust kitchen equipment output.
8Undersized motor.Check motor ratings with catalog speed and air capacity chart.
