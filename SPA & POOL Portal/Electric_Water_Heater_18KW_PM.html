3-MONTHLY PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST

SECTION 1:  VISUAL & MECHANICAL INSPECTION
1Inspect heater body, enclosure, wall/floor mounting screws, and brackets for security and corrosion
2Inspect inlet and outlet pipe connections — check unions, O-rings, and jubilee clips for leaks or weeping
3Inspect return pipe safety loop (kick-up) at heater outlet — confirm intact, unobstructed, and securely clipped
4Verify heater enclosure is installed in dry, weatherproof area — check for moisture ingress or condensation inside enclosure
5Confirm minimum internal bore of supply and return pipework is 32mm — inspect for any pinching or deformation
6Check power supply cable externally for damage, heat marks, abrasion, or crush hazards along full routed length
SECTION 2:  ELECTRICAL SAFETY CHECK
7ISOLATE heater from main power supply at distribution board before proceeding with any electrical inspection
8Remove access cover (per Fig. 8 of O&M manual); at heater terminal block, verify all power connections are correctly wired per label inside heater and firmly tightened
9At distribution board, verify cable connections are secure and cable cross-section is adequate for installed cable length
10RESTORE power; test dedicated 30mA RCD Safety Circuit — press TEST BUTTON, confirm RCD trips instantly and cuts power to heater
11Inspect PCB control board for visible damage, burn marks, or corrosion; check internal fuse condition — replace if blown
SECTION 3:  OPERATIONAL CHECKS
12Start circulation pump; verify HEATER DELAY message appears on display for approximately 2 minutes before heating commences
13Confirm Red light illuminates and heating mode commences after 2-minute startup delay
14Hold selector dial button for 5 seconds to enter calibration mode — verify displayed temperature against independent thermometer reading; adjust within ±2°C maximum if deviation exceeds ±1°C
15Record inlet and outlet water temperature; calculate temperature differential
16Verify heater shuts to standby (Amber light) when set point temperature is reached; confirms it maintains ±0.5°C differential
17Confirm enclosure temperature is below 60°C threshold — SHUTDOWN HIGH AMBIENT protection activates automatically above 60°C
SECTION 4:  FLOW RATE CHECK
18Confirm pool circulation pump is delivering minimum required flow rate: 4,000 L/hr minimum for 18kW heater
19Verify flow rate does not exceed maximum permitted: 17,000 L/hr — install bypass valve if flow rate exceeds this limit
20Purge trapped air pockets from heater and pipework — confirm return pipe safety loop is functioning correctly to prevent air locks
SECTION 5:  WATER QUALITY CHECK
21Test pool water pH: acceptable range 6.8 – 8.0
22Test Total Alkalinity (TA): acceptable range 80 – 140 ppm
23Test Chloride content: maximum 150 mg/L (exceeding this limit will void manufacturer warranty)
24Test Free Chlorine: maximum 2.0 mg/L
25Test Total Bromine: maximum 4.5 mg/L
26Test TDS/Calcium Hardness: acceptable range 200 – 1,000 ppm; log all readings and take corrective dosing action if out of range

SECTION 1:  VISUAL & SAFETY INSPECTION
1Inspect heater enclosure, body, and all external fixings for corrosion, physical damage, or moisture ingress
2Inspect inlet/outlet pipe unions, O-rings, and all pipe connections — tighten or replace deteriorated seals as required
3Verify return pipe safety loop (kick-up) at heater outlet is intact, unrestricted, and securely clipped in place
4Confirm heater is mounted securely (wall or floor fixing screws tight, per Fig. 2 of O&M manual) and installation remains in dry, weatherproof area
5Test dedicated 30mA RCD Safety Circuit — press TEST BUTTON, confirm RCD trips instantly; reset and verify power is restored to heater
SECTION 2:  DRAIN, CLEAN & ELEMENT INSPECTION
6ISOLATE heater from main power supply at distribution board before commencing any maintenance work
7Close inlet and outlet isolation valves; drain heater completely to allow full inspection (drain also prevents frost damage during winter shutdown)
8Remove flow tube access and inspect heating element(s) — check for scale build-up, sludge, corrosion, or physical damage
9Clean heating elements thoroughly — remove all scale and debris deposits using appropriate non-corrosive cleaning agent
10Clean flow tube interior — remove all scale deposits, blockages, and bio-fouling
11Inspect flow tube O-rings and all internal seals — replace any deteriorated or compressed seals before reassembly
12Reassemble flow tube and all connections ensuring watertight joints; reinstate isolation valves; refill heater and purge all trapped air before energizing
SECTION 3:  ELECTRICAL INSPECTION
13With power isolated, inspect power supply cable from distribution board to heater terminal block — check insulation condition, routing, and termination tightness at both ends
14Remove access cover (per Fig. 8); at heater terminal block, check all connections are tight and correctly wired per label inside heater; verify cable is not exposed to sharp edges, hot surfaces, or crush hazards
15Verify cable cross-sectional area is adequate for installed length (minimum 5A/mm² up to 20 metres) — upgrade if insufficient
16Inspect PCB control board for burn marks, corrosion, or blown internal fuse — replace fuse if required; document all findings
17Restore power; verify Amber standby light illuminates on controller display
18Test 30mA RCD — press TEST BUTTON to confirm trip; reset and verify power restored to heater; obtain Electrician sign-off and record in inspection log
SECTION 4:  OPERATIONAL RECOMMISSIONING
19Start circulation pump — confirm NO FLOW message clears and HEATER DELAY message appears on display for approximately 2 minutes
20Confirm Red light illuminates after delay — heater enters active heating mode
21Measure and confirm flow rate is within operational limits: minimum 4,000 L/hr, maximum 17,000 L/hr
22Enter calibration mode (hold dial button 5 seconds) — verify temperature display accuracy against independent thermometer; adjust within ±2°C maximum if required
23Record set point temperature, actual pool water temperature, and temperature differential across heater
24Confirm heater maintains pool temperature within ±0.5°C of set point; record all operating data in equipment history log
SECTION 5:  WATER QUALITY
25Test pool water pH: acceptable range 6.8 – 8.0
26Test Total Alkalinity (TA): acceptable range 80 – 140 ppm
27Test Chloride content: maximum 150 mg/L
28Test Free Chlorine: maximum 2.0 mg/L
29Test Total Bromine: maximum 4.5 mg/L
30Test TDS/Calcium Hardness: acceptable range 200 – 1,000 ppm; log all readings
SECTION 6:  DOCUMENTATION
31Record all maintenance activities, findings, and corrective actions taken in equipment history log
32List any replacement parts installed — provide description and quantity for each item
33Update next 3-monthly and 6-monthly maintenance schedule dates in maintenance planner
34Submit completed checklist to Supervisor for review and countersignature
