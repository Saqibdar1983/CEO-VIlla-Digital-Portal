A.  SAFETY — BEFORE STARTING INSPECTION
1CRITICAL SAFETY RULE: Pump must be OFF before changing valve handle position — rotating handle with pump running will cause severe injury and major property damage; confirm pump is off before touching handle
2To change handle position: depress handle downward to release pressure, then rotate clockwise to desired position; confirm pointer is accurately seated in position marking before restarting pump
3Never operate pump with valve handle in CLOSED position — this blocks all flow and will damage the pump
B.  HANDLE & POSITION OPERATION CHECK
4With pump OFF, test handle movement through all valve positions — depress handle and rotate clockwise through: FILTER → BACKWASH → RINSE → WASTE → RECIRCULATE → CLOSED → WINTERIZE; handle must move freely without sticking, jumping, or requiring excessive force at any position
5Confirm handle returns firmly to each position — pointer must align accurately and fully with each position marking on the valve label; a misaligned pointer means the diverter is not fully seated and flow will cross-contaminate between ports
6Inspect handle and handle pin — confirm handle pin is secure and handle does not rock or move on the shaft; a loose handle pin requires immediate repair
7Return handle to FILTER position before restarting pump
C.  OPERATIONAL LEAK CHECK (Pump Running — FILTER Position)
8Start pump with valve in FILTER position; allow system to reach operating pressure
9Record filter pressure gauge reading: _______ PSI — this is the baseline operating pressure; note: maximum operating pressure must not exceed 50 PSI; backwash is required when pressure reads 10 PSI above the clean-filter baseline
10Inspect all valve port pipe connections (Pump port, Return to pool port, Waste/Backwash port) for water leaks at fittings, unions, and pipe joints — any leakage at connections must be investigated and repaired
11CRITICAL CHECK — Observe waste/backwash port discharge while pump is running in FILTER position: any water flowing from the waste port in FILTER position indicates spider gasket leakage; record: Waste port leakage in FILTER position — None / Minor / Significant
D.  SPIDER GASKET TROUBLESHOOTING (only if leakage to waste port was observed above)
12Troubleshooting Step 1 (Praher manual procedure): Stop pump; depress valve handle fully in FILTER position and release quickly; repeat 4 to 5 times; restart pump in FILTER position and re-check waste port — this dislodges debris that may be holding gasket open; if leakage stops, record as resolved
13Troubleshooting Step 2 (if leakage continues after Step 1): Stop pump; place handle in WINTERIZE position; have assistant hold handle in WINTERIZE position; start pump for approximately 30 seconds to flush debris from valve body; stop pump; repeat Step 1; restart in FILTER and re-check waste port
14If leakage persists after Steps 1 and 2, spider gasket replacement is required — do not defer; schedule gasket replacement and record in maintenance log; continue to operate filter but monitor waste port at each shift until gasket is replaced
E.  VALVE BODY EXTERNAL INSPECTION
15Inspect valve body cover (top section) for cracks, crazing, UV degradation, chemical damage, or discoloration — the Praher manual states all components should be inspected frequently and replaced at least every ten years or if damaged
16Inspect valve body (bottom section) for cracks, leaks at body-to-piping connections, or physical damage
17Inspect all cover bolts and nuts around perimeter — confirm all bolts are present and secure; tighten any loose bolts; do not over-tighten as excessive force can crack the plastic cover
18Inspect sight glass (if fitted on valve body) for condition, clarity, and integrity of sight glass gasket
19Inspect external air bleeder (fitted on Pentair Hi Flow units — SFV-02 and SFV-03) — confirm bleeder valve is present and functional; bleeder should release air before opening valve at system start-up
20Confirm valve is correctly labelled and position markings are legible — label must be readable for safe operation by maintenance personnel
F.  DOCUMENTATION
21Record: Filter operating pressure (baseline): _______ PSI; Date of next required backwash (when pressure reaches baseline + 10 PSI): monitor ongoing
22Record: Waste port leakage observed: Yes / No; Troubleshooting steps performed: N/A / Step 1 only / Steps 1 and 2; Result: Resolved / Gasket replacement required
23Record: Handle operation: Free / Stiff / Requires repair; Body condition: No damage / Defects noted (describe in Remarks)
24Record: Next 3-monthly service due date: _______

A.  PRE-SERVICE SAFETY & PREPARATION
1Stop pump and confirm power is isolated at distribution board before starting any disassembly
2Release all pressure from the system — open filter air relief valve to confirm system is depressurised before opening valve body
3Drain system or close isolation valves upstream and downstream of valve to prevent flooding when valve is opened — confirm system is drained or isolated before removing cover bolts
4Prepare tools: screwdriver, wrench for cover bolts, hammer and punch (for handle pin), silicone lubricant (silicone-based only — never petroleum-based grease), clean cloth, replacement parts if available (spider gasket, O-ring set)
B.  DISASSEMBLY
5Place handle in FILTER position before disassembly
6Remove all bolts and nuts around perimeter of valve cover — note: the internal spring is under compression; maintain control of the cover during removal to prevent it from being pushed off suddenly
7Remove cover by lifting straight up — note orientation of cover tab alignment with body tab for correct reassembly (see valve cover alignment marking)
8Remove handle pin using hammer and punch: pull top of valve cover down 1/2 to 5/8 inch; tap pin out of shaft; remove handle and set aside with pin
9Remove washer — set aside for inspection
C.  INTERNAL COMPONENT INSPECTION
10Inspect spring — confirm spring is not corroded, kinked, bent, or weakened; a weakened spring prevents the diverter from seating properly against the spider gasket; replace if corrosion or deformation is found
11Inspect internal plastic washer — replace if cracked, warped, or deformed
12Remove diverter from valve body — inspect the diverter bottom surface (the surface that presses against the spider gasket) for wear, scoring, grooves, chemical attack, or deformation; any damage on the diverter contact face allows cross-flow leakage and requires diverter replacement
13Inspect diverter shaft O-rings — O-rings must be supple, round in cross-section, and free of cracks or flat spots; if O-rings are hardened, flattened, cracked, or show chemical swelling, remove and replace
14Clean diverter shaft O-ring grooves — remove any chemical deposits or debris from grooves before reinstalling or replacing O-rings
15Apply silicone lubricant to diverter shaft O-rings — use silicone-based lubricant only; do not use petroleum-based grease or WD-40 as these degrade rubber O-rings; per Praher manual troubleshooting section, silicone lubricant on diverter shaft O-rings is required at service
16Inspect cover O-ring (around perimeter of valve cover, if present) — replace if flattened, cracked, or deformed
D.  SPIDER GASKET INSPECTION & REPLACEMENT (if required)
17Inspect spider gasket in valve body — gasket must be seated in groove with rounded bead facing upward; inspect for cracks, tears, missing sections, chemical attack, permanent compression, or deformation in any segment of the gasket; note: even one damaged segment of the spider gasket causes cross-flow leakage to the waste port
18If spider gasket is in good condition: leave in place; do not remove unless replacement is needed — the Praher manual states once the replacement step is started it must be completed without interruption
19If spider gasket replacement is required — NOTE: Once started, proceed through all replacement steps without interruption
20Clean gasket groove in valve body thoroughly — groove must be completely free of water, grease, oil, debris, and remnants of old gasket before installing new gasket
21Insert new spider gasket into groove with rounded bead facing upward — press gasket firmly into all groove areas to seat new gasket evenly around the full circumference
22Allow gasket cure time before reassembly — minimum cure time is 2 hours; full cure time of 24 hours is recommended for full strength per Praher manual; do not return valve to service before minimum cure time has elapsed
E.  REASSEMBLY
23Reinstall diverter into valve body — ensure diverter shaft O-rings are lubricated with silicone lubricant before inserting
24Reinstall spring over diverter shaft; reinstall internal plastic washer
25Reinstall handle on cover — align handle pointer to FILTER position before pinning; tap handle pin into place lightly with hammer and punch; confirm pin is fully seated
26Before reinstalling cover, confirm diverter and handle are both in FILTER position — cover must be reinstalled with both in the same position as when it was removed
27Align cover tab with body tab (use alignment marking on valve body); press cover down onto body — press down on cover or set handle to WINTERIZE position to allow bolts to engage nuts; tighten all bolts securely and evenly around perimeter (do not over-tighten — plastic cover will crack)
28After reassembly, confirm handle depresses and rotates freely through all positions: FILTER, BACKWASH, RINSE, WASTE, RECIRCULATE, CLOSED — pointer must align accurately at each position
F.  RECOMMISSIONING & POST-SERVICE CHECK
29Open isolation valves and restore system; confirm pump power is reconnected
30Start pump with valve in FILTER position; observe all port connections and valve body for leaks during initial pressurisation
31Record operating pressure immediately after annual service: _______ PSI — record as new baseline for 3-monthly comparisons
32CRITICAL CHECK: Observe waste/backwash port with pump running in FILTER position — confirm no water flow from waste port; leakage after service indicates spider gasket is not correctly seated; stop pump and re-inspect gasket if leakage is present
33Cycle handle through BACKWASH and RINSE positions briefly to confirm valve operates correctly after reassembly; return to FILTER position for normal operation
34Confirm air bleeder (SFV-02 and SFV-03) is functioning correctly — bleed air before starting pump after any valve disassembly
G.  DOCUMENTATION
35Record: Date of annual service; spider gasket condition: OK / Replaced; diverter shaft O-rings: OK / Replaced and lubricated with silicone; cover O-ring: OK / Replaced; spring: OK / Replaced; diverter: OK / Replaced
36Record: Post-service baseline filter pressure: _______ PSI; waste port leakage after service: None (pass) / Leakage (investigate)
37Record: Next annual service due date: _______; next 3-monthly inspection due date: _______
38Update equipment maintenance history log with all parts inspected, replaced, and findings
39Submit completed annual service checklist to Supervisor for countersignature
