A.  SAFETY — BEFORE APPROACHING UNIT
1CHEMICAL HAZARD: Do not inhale fumes from the chlorinator or chemical container — chlorine gas hazard; approach unit from upwind side; if strong chlorine odour is detected, ensure adequate ventilation before proceeding; wear eye and skin protection
2Confirm chlorine feeder flow control valve is at the correct dosing position during pump operation — valve must NOT be at ZERO or OFF during normal circulation; confirm valve setting is appropriate for current chlorine demand
3CRITICAL — Before stopping pump for any extended period: set flow control valve to ZERO position to prevent chlorine gas buildup in system when flow stops; this is a mandatory operational requirement per Hayward manual
B.  CHLORINE RESIDUAL CHECK (Manual requirement — Daily)
4Test free chlorine residual in pool/spa water using quality manual test kit — Hayward manual states: "Check chlorine residual daily and adjust the dial valve for more or less chlorine"; record: Free Chlorine _______ ppm (target: 1.0–3.0 ppm; ideal 2.0 ppm)
5If chlorine residual is low (below 1.0 ppm): increase flow control valve setting to higher dosing position; if residual is high (above 3.0 ppm): reduce flow control valve setting; note: chlorine demand varies based on bather load, temperature, and sunlight
6Inspect around chlorinator body for chlorine gas odour, white chemical residue (crystallised chlorine), or moisture at cover seal — any cover leak requires prompt isolation and inspection; do not allow bathers near pool until leak is resolved and chlorine levels are confirmed safe
7Confirm chlorine feeder is installed in service — feeder only doses when circulation pump is running and water flows through the unit; confirm pump is running during inspection

A.  CHLORINE TABLET LEVEL CHECK & REFILL PROCEDURE
1SAFETY: Wear safety glasses and chemical-resistant gloves before opening chlorinator cover — unit may be under pressure; do not inhale fumes when cover is opened; approach from upwind side
2REFILL PROCEDURE — Step 1: Shut off all pumps and pump timers
3REFILL PROCEDURE — Step 2: Turn chlorine feeder flow control valve to OFF position
4REFILL PROCEDURE — Step 3: Verify chlorine feeder return line to pool is unrestricted before opening cover
5REFILL PROCEDURE — Step 4: Wait a minimum of one minute to relieve system pressure before attempting to remove cover — NEVER open cover under pressure; cover removal under pressure is a serious hazard
6REFILL PROCEDURE — Step 5: If installed in a flooded system, shut off isolation valves to isolate chlorinator before opening cover
7Open cover — pry off Logo Cap (CLX200E); unscrew and remove cover retaining screw (CLX200G); confirm both slip washers (CLX200W) are in place; slip cover free of Cover Cap (CLX200C)
8Inspect interior of chlorinator — check tablet level; inspect for sludge, undissolved residue, or tablet fragments that may be blocking the feeder tube or flow control valve; remove any blockage
9Record tablet level before refill: _______ (Full / Half / Low / Empty)
10If refilling — add slow dissolving Trichlor Chlorine Tablets only: DANGER: NEVER use fast dissolving Trichlor tablets; NEVER mix Trichlor tablets with Calcium Hypochlorite or any other form of chlorine or chemicals — fire and/or explosion will result; use only Trichlor tablets as specified (NSF qualification tested with 99% active Trichlor-s-triazinetrione)
11Secure cover — reinstall cover onto Cover Cap; replace slip washers in correct positions (on stem inside cover and under head of retaining screw); reinstall retaining screw (CLX200G) and Logo Cap (CLX200E); confirm cover is firmly secured
12If isolation valves were closed, open valves to restore flow from pump to pool
13Turn flow control valve to required dosing setting; restart pump; inspect all connections for leaks — re-tighten as required (hand tight plus 1 to 1.5 turns maximum; never over-tighten)
14Record: Tablets added: Yes / No; Flow control valve setting after refill: _______
B.  CONNECTIONS & FLOW INDICATOR CHECK
15Confirm flow indication device installed upstream of CL200 is in service — NSF-50 requires a flow indicator with all flow-through chemical feeders; chlorinator must not be considered operational if flow indicator is absent or not functioning
16Inspect inlet and outlet pipe connections (1-1/2" threaded unions) at both ports of chlorinator — check for chemical weeping, white residue, or moisture at joints; re-tighten if any seepage is found; do not over-tighten
17Inspect check valve assembly (CLX220CV) at outlet port — confirm check valve body is present and undamaged; shake check valve gently and confirm internal ball "clicks" freely inside (per manual: "dot marked; ball clicks when shaken" confirms ball is not seized); a seized or missing check valve allows chlorine gas to backflow into the system when pump stops
18Inspect flow control valve handle — confirm handle is secure on stem and pointer is clearly readable; a loose handle must be refitted per manual procedure before next use

A.  PRE-SERVICE SAFETY & SHUTDOWN
1SAFETY: Wear safety glasses and chemical-resistant gloves throughout this service — chlorine residue is present inside the unit; opening the cover releases chlorine fumes; always approach from upwind side
2Shut off all pumps and pump timers; turn flow control valve to OFF position; verify return line to pool is unrestricted; wait one minute for pressure to relieve; close isolation valves if system is flooded
B.  COVER O-RING INSPECTION & LUBRICATION (Part: CLX200K)
3Open cover — pry off Logo Cap (CLX200E); unscrew cover retaining screw (CLX200G); confirm both slip washers (CLX200W × 2) are located correctly (one on stem inside cover; one under head of retaining screw); remove cover from Cover Cap
4Inspect interior of chlorinator body — remove any tablet remnants, sludge, or chemical deposits; rinse interior with clean water if scale or residue is present
5Remove O-ring (CLX200K) from cover — inspect for deformation, cracking, hardening, chemical swelling, or permanent compression on any section of the O-ring; replace O-ring if any defect is found
6LUBRICATION — CRITICAL RULE: Lubricate O-ring (CLX200K) with Hayward Jack's Lube No. 327 (Part No. SP032712) only — NEVER use petroleum-type lubricants (Vaseline, WD-40, or general grease) on the O-ring; petroleum lubricants degrade the O-ring rubber and cause cover leakage per Hayward manual
7Reinstall O-ring (CLX200K) — ensure O-ring seats evenly in groove around full circumference; no twisting, folding, or pinching of O-ring
8Reinstall cover — confirm both slip washers (CLX200W) are correctly positioned (on stem inside cover, and under retaining screw head); reinstall cover retaining screw (CLX200G) securely; press on Logo Cap (CLX200E)
9Record: O-ring CLX200K condition: OK / Replaced; O-ring lubricated with Jack's Lube No. 327: Yes / No
C.  FLOW CONTROL VALVE INSPECTION (Part: CLX200PA)
10Inspect flow control valve assembly (CLX200PA) — rotate valve from ZERO to full open and back; valve must turn smoothly through full range without sticking, binding, grinding, or requiring excessive force; chemical scale is the most common cause of a stiff flow control valve
11If valve is stiff or jammed, clean valve assembly — remove valve handle per manual procedure (set pointer to FULL; insert screwdriver in slot opposite pointer; lift and rotate handle counter-clockwise to clear index lock tab); flush valve body with fresh water to dislodge scale; reassemble
12If valve remains stiff or damaged after cleaning, replace control valve assembly (CLX200PA)
13After inspection, confirm flow control valve pointer is accurately aligned with position markings — misaligned pointer gives false dosing rate indication; re-align per manual procedure if required
14Record: Flow control valve condition: Free / Stiff (cleaned) / Replaced (CLX200PA)
D.  CHECK VALVE & BODY INSPECTION
15Inspect check valve assembly (CLX220CV) at outlet port — shake valve and confirm internal ball clicks freely; if ball is seized, remove check valve body and inspect ball and seat for scale or corrosion; clean or replace (CLX220CV) as required
16Inspect chlorinator body (Cover CLX200B, Cover Cap CLX200C, Base CLX200BS) for cracks, UV degradation, chemical attack, or discoloration — any crack in body, cover, or base requires immediate component replacement; do not continue operating a cracked chlorinator body under pressure
17Inspect feeder tube (CLX200H, if fitted) — confirm tube is free of blockage, scale, or kinking; replace if blocked or degraded
18Inspect drain plug with gasket (SPX1700FA) at base of unit — confirm drain plug is present, fully seated, and not leaking; replace gasket if any moisture or seepage is found at drain plug location
19Inspect inlet and outlet connection threads (1-1/2" female) and union connectors (SPX1500UNPAK, if fitted) — confirm all connections are free of chemical damage, thread damage, or leakage; confirm unions are tight (hand tight plus 1 to 1.5 turns)
E.  RECOMMISSIONING & POST-SERVICE CHECK
20Open isolation valves (if closed); restart pump; set flow control valve to required dosing setting
21Inspect all connections and cover for leaks after restart — re-tighten as required; do not over-tighten fittings
22Test free chlorine residual after 30 minutes of operation: _______ ppm; adjust flow control valve setting if residual is outside target range (1.0–3.0 ppm)
23Confirm check valve is functioning — briefly stop pump and confirm no backflow of chlorinated water or gas from outlet direction (a functioning check valve holds back pressure when pump stops)
F.  DOCUMENTATION
24Record: Date of 3-monthly service; O-ring CLX200K: OK / Replaced; O-ring lubricated (Jack's Lube No. 327): Yes / No; Flow control valve CLX200PA: OK / Cleaned / Replaced; Check valve CLX220CV: OK / Cleaned / Replaced
25Record: Body condition: No cracks / Defects noted; Feeder tube CLX200H: OK / Replaced; Drain plug SPX1700FA: OK / Gasket replaced
26Record: Post-service chlorine residual: _______ ppm; Flow control valve setting: _______; Next 3-monthly service due date: _______
