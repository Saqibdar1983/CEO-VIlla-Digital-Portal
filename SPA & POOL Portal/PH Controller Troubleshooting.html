HAYWARD PH CONTROLLER HCC 2000 TROUBLESHOOTING
Symptom / Operational ConditionPotential Root Cause AnalysisCorrective Action / Engineering Remedy
No lights are illuminated when controller is powered ON.1. Upstream circuit breaker tripped or wall receptacle failed.
2. Power cord or terminal connection interface damaged.		1. Reset circuit breaker or inspect receptacle utility power. Ensure connection to a grounded-type GFCI protected source.
2. Verify cord integrity and replace if damage is detected.
Alarm light(s) and audible tone are active.1. Main filtration system loop failure or low flow.
2. Sensors or main power cables uncoupled.
3. Chemical dosing feeder mechanical failure.
4. Flow safety switch failed or uncoupled.		1. Verify filtration pump operation, ensure proper flow rates, and re-balance water chemistry baseline.
2. Check and secure all cable connectors.
3. Verify operational readiness of chemical feeders.
4. Check flow sensor seating.
Both pH and ORP digital readouts display illogical / erratic values.1. Sensor signal cables crossed or reversed.
2. Filtration system looping incorrectly or water balance skewed.		1. Ensure sensor lines are safely linked to their respective exact BNC ports on the controller casing.
2. Verify uniform hydraulic flow through the testing block.
ORP chemical feeder does not activate as expected.1. Unit set to wrong control mode.
2. Programmed setpoint limit variance.		1. Verify and select 'AUTO' ORP feed mode.
2. Review and adjust ORP mV setpoints to meet standard code demands.
pH chemical feeder does not activate as expected.1. Feed orientation parameter incorrect.
2. Unit set to wrong control mode.
3. Programmed setpoint limit variance.		1. Adjust acid/base feed configuration parameter to match chemical feed layout specifications.
2. Select 'AUTO' pH feed mode.
3. Review and adjust targeted pH setpoints.
Pool/Spa chlorine or bromine residual is too high or too low.1. Interferences from pH fluctuations, elevated cyanuric acid levels, or High Total Dissolved Solids (TDS).
2. Erroneous target setpoint setup.		1. Assess chemical impact of any recent manual chemical balancing adjustments. Re-verify free sanitizer residuals vs ORP values.
2. Recalibrate and optimize the master ORP setpoint.
pH requires overly frequent calibration / unstable readings.1. Reference junction contamination or oil blinding.
2. Sensor reached end of life cycle.		1. Execute the toothbrush-toothpaste sensor cleaning protocol.
2. If cleaning fails to stabilize values or response remains sluggish, replace with genuine HCC Professional Series sensors (PRO15-2 / PRO25-2).
HAYWARD SENSOR MAINTENANCE & OPERATION GUIDE
1. Sensor Cleaning Protocol
Task PhaseOperational Requirements & Engineering Instructions
Frequency IndicationsClean weekly or monthly depending on bather load. Immediate indicators for cleaning include: slow response times, an increased need for frequent pH calibration, and inconsistent or unstable readings.
DismantlingDisconnect sensors from the controller panel and carefully extract them from the flow cell assembly.
Reference Junction CleaningClean the reference junction (the white Teflon ring located at the bottom of the sensor body) using a soft toothbrush and regular toothpaste.
Oil/Grease RemovalUtilize a household liquid dishwashing detergent to safely break down and remove any surface oils or body grease.
ReinstallationRinse meticulously with fresh water. Replace the Teflon thread-seal tape on the threads. Reinstall sensors by hand-tightening ONLY. Do not use tools.
CRITICAL WARNINGNEVER allow a pH or ORP sensor tip to dry out completely. Complete drying permanently damages the reference junction and completely voids the manufacturer's warranty.
2. Asset Storage & Winterization Protocols
System ComponentMandatory Technical Storage Specifications
Sensor Storage CapsAlways store sensor tips inside their provided soaking caps if removed for longer than 1 hour. Fill caps with original storage solution or clean water. Alternatively, submerge tips individually in small glass/plastic containers of clean water.
Sensor Thermal ProtectionHayward sensors are freeze-resistant but must be protected from freezing environments during seasonal non-use intervals.
Controller Panel ProtectionThe controller board is highly vulnerable to Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) when sensor cables are uncoupled. Always reinstall the BNC protective covers prior to transport or storage.
Hydraulic Loop WinterizationThe acrylic flow cell block and all integrated poly connection tubing must be completely drained prior to freezing ambient conditions. Purge all internal moisture using compressed air, or thoroughly drain via open valve ports and disconnected tube unions.
