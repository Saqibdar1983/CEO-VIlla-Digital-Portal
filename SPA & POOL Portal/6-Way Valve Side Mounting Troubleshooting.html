SPA & POOL FACILITY6-Way Valve Side Mounting Troubleshooting
ProblemStepProbable Cause / CheckRemedy / Action to be Taken
Leakage to WASTE PORT1Possible debris under the valve gasket / seal.Shut pump OFF. Depress handle fully in FILTER position and quickly release handle. Repeat several times. If leakage still occurs through WASTE port, proceed to Step 2.
Leakage to WASTE PORT2Debris may be lodged on the gasket of the valve body.Shut pump OFF. Put handle in WINTER position. Have someone hold the handle in WINTER position. Start one pump for approximately 30 seconds; this should wash out any debris lodged on the gasket of the valve body. Repeat Step 1.
Leakage to WASTE PORT3Spider gasket may be damaged, or diverter bottom surface may be damaged at the spider gasket contact area.If Steps 1 and 2 do not resolve the problem, remove the key hub assembly and inspect the spider gasket for visible damage. Replace the gasket if damaged. Also inspect the diverter for any damage on the bottom surface that makes contact with the spider gasket. At this point, dismantle the top key hub assembly, clean the O-rings on the diverter shaft, and apply silicone lubricant.
Leakage to WASTE PORT4Gasket replacement is not an option / temporary control required.Install a ball valve into the WASTE port. Keep the ball valve closed in FILTER position. Keep the valve open in BACKWASH, RINSE, and DRAIN positions. Install an operation instruction plaque at the valve location. Opening the ball valve and correct operation of the multi-port valve is extremely critical. Water hammer could damage other equipment, including the valve.
Important Site Note
1Operate the pump and valve only by trained personnel. Incorrect operation can cause leakage, water hammer, and damage to the valve or other equipment.
2Before dismantling the valve top/key hub assembly, isolate the pump, release system pressure, and follow site safety procedure.
3Use silicone lubricant only on O-rings. Do not use unsuitable grease that can attack rubber parts.
