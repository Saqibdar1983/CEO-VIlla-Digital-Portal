A.  VISUAL & MECHANICAL INSPECTION
1Inspect pump body, volute, and strainer pot for cracks, corrosion, or physical damage
2Inspect all union fittings — check O-rings and union nuts for tightness and leaks
3Inspect indoor pool main drain suction cover — confirm undamaged, secured, and ANSI/ASME A112.19.8 compliant (anti-entrapment)
4Inspect balancing tank suction port cover — confirm undamaged and secured
5Inspect and clear all indoor pool jet nozzle orifices for scale, debris, or algae blockage
6Check pump mounting feet and base fixings — tighten loose fasteners
B.  STRAINER BASKET SERVICE
7Isolate pump power at circuit breaker before opening strainer pot — WARNING: do not open if pump recently ran dry or failed to prime (scalding vapor hazard)
8Relieve system pressure; remove strainer pot lid clamp (counter-clockwise) and lid carefully — verify pot is cool to touch
9Remove strainer basket (P/N 070387) — inspect for cracks; replace if cracked
10Clean basket thoroughly — remove all debris and rinse with clean water
11Clean strainer pot interior and lid sealing surface
12Inspect lid O-ring (P/N 350013) — clean, lubricate with silicone grease, and replace if compressed or deteriorated
13Reinstall basket (align notch with rib); fill pot with water; reinstall lid and clamp (clockwise to horizontal)
14Open filter air relief valve at sand filter if connected; start pump; close valve when steady water confirms prime — verify flow at all indoor jet nozzles
C.  ELECTRICAL CHECKS
15Inspect power supply cable from circuit breaker to motor for damage, heat marks, or abrasion
16Test GFCI — press TEST: power interrupts; press RESET: power restores; report any GFCI failure immediately
17Check and tighten all electrical terminal screws at motor terminal block
18Inspect motor fan guard and cooling vents — clear accumulated dust and debris
D.  PERFORMANCE VERIFICATION

20Confirm flow at all indoor jet nozzles — no blocked or reduced-flow nozzles

A.  VISUAL & SAFETY INSPECTION
1Inspect pump body, volute, strainer pot, all fittings, and pipe connections for cracks, corrosion, or damage
2Inspect all union fittings — replace deteriorated O-rings; tighten union nuts
3Inspect main drain and balancing tank suction covers — replace if cracked, loose, or non-compliant with ANSI/ASME A112.19.8
4Inspect and descale all indoor jet nozzle orifices — clean any scale or algae deposits
5Check and tighten all pump mounting fixings
B.  STRAINER BASKET & POT FULL SERVICE
6Isolate power at circuit breaker; de-pressurize system fully before opening pot
7Remove strainer pot lid carefully — verify cool and pressure-free
8Remove and clean strainer basket (P/N 070387) — replace if cracked
9Clean pot interior, drain plug ports, and lid sealing surface
10Remove both drain plugs (P/N 071131) and O-rings (P/N 192115) — replace O-rings; reinstall plugs securely
11Replace lid O-ring (P/N 350013) — lubricate with silicone grease
12Reinstall basket; fill pot with water; reinstall lid and clamp; start pump; confirm prime and flow at all indoor nozzles
C.  SHAFT SEAL & INTERNAL INSPECTION
13Inspect pump body around seal plate for water seepage or mineral scale indicating seal wear
14If seal leakage confirmed: isolate power; remove 6 strainer pot bolts; separate motor/hydraulics sub-assembly
15Inspect shaft seal (P/N PA-7 / PS1000 with ceramic seat) — replace if scored or damaged; do not scratch polished seal faces
16Inspect impeller (clean passages) — note LEFT-HAND thread on impeller lock screw (tighten counter-clockwise to 25 in-lbs)
17Inspect diffuser and diffuser O-ring — replace if worn
18Inspect seal plate gasket (P/N 357100) — replace if deteriorated
19Reassemble: seal plate to motor (70 in-lbs cross-pattern); impeller (25 in-lbs); pot to motor (110 in-lbs cross-pattern)
D.  ELECTRICAL INSPECTION
20With power isolated, inspect power cable — check insulation, routing, and termination tightness
21Tighten all electrical terminal connections at motor
22Test GFCI — confirm TEST trips; RESET restores; record result and date
23Inspect motor fan, fan guard, and cooling vents — clean all obstructions
E.  RECOMMISSIONING & VERIFICATION
24Prime pump; confirm no air in system; open filter air relief valve if required during startup
25Record suction pressure: _______ PSI; discharge pressure: _______ PSI
