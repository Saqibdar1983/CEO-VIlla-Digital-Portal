A.  OPERATIONAL STATUS CHECK
1SAFETY: Wear protective gloves and safety glasses before inspecting any chemical dosing pump or associated tubing and fittings — chemicals being pumped are hazardous
2Confirm pump motor is running — listen for steady pulsing/clicking sound of diaphragm stroking; absence of pulsing sound despite power being on indicates blockage, seized mechanism, or loss of prime; report immediately
3Confirm pump output indicator light is cycling (if fitted) — indicates pump is actively dosing chemical on demand signal from pH/ORP controller
4Check chemical solution tank level — tank must never be allowed to run dry; a dry tank causes immediate loss of prime and stops chemical dosing; if level is low, refill immediately and re-prime pump per start-up procedure in manual
5Inspect suction tubing for continuous air bubbles — air in the suction line indicates pump is losing prime or solution tank level has dropped below foot valve; investigate cause and re-prime if necessary
6Inspect all tubing coupling nut connections and pump head for any chemical leakage or drips — no chemical should leak at any point; report any leakage immediately and isolate pump if required
7Confirm downstream pool/spa water chemistry is within expected range as indication that chemical dosing is occurring — zero dosing effect despite pump running indicates injection point blockage or check valve failure

A.  OUTPUT CALIBRATION CHECK
1SAFETY: Wear protective gloves and safety glasses throughout this inspection
2Verify pump output rate using a graduated calibration cylinder — rated output is 30 GPD / 4.7 LPH; disconnect discharge tubing temporarily from injection line, direct into calibration cylinder, and measure actual output over a timed period; record: measured output _______ LPH | _______ GPD
3If measured output deviates from required dosing rate, adjust output using the output adjusting knob — loosen locking lever counter-clockwise, adjust knob (one full counter-clockwise revolution = maximum output; clockwise to reduce), tighten locking lever; CAUTION: adjust only while pump is running; never force the output adjusting knob; do not turn more than one full revolution from zero
4After adjustment, re-measure output to confirm correct dosing rate; reconnect discharge tubing and confirm hand-tight coupling nut; record: adjusted output _______ LPH
B.  TUBING INSPECTION
5Inspect full length of clear flexible suction tubing (approx. 4 ft / 1.21m from solution tank to pump suction port) for cracking, yellowing, hardening, kinking, UV degradation, or chemical attack — the manual safety instruction states "Inspect tubing regularly for cracking or deterioration and replace as necessary"; replace immediately if any cracking or deterioration is found
6Inspect stiff white discharge tubing (approx. 8 ft / 2.43m from pump discharge to injection point) for cracking, chemical attack, blockage, or UV degradation — if tubing is exposed to direct sunlight, confirm black UV-resistant polyethylene tubing is installed; white translucent tubing degrades rapidly in direct sunlight
7Inspect all tubing coupling nut connections at suction valve, discharge valve, bleed valve (if fitted), and injection assembly — confirm all nuts are hand-tight; DO NOT use a wrench as over-tightening cracks the pump head; verify no chemical leakage at any connection point
8Inspect bleed valve bypass tubing (if bleed valve is installed) — confirm bypass tubing is correctly routed back to the solution tank and is not blocked, kinked, or disconnected
C.  SOLUTION TANK & STRAINER CHECK
9Inspect solution tank interior for settling, sludge, or chemical crystallisation at the bottom — if sludge or crystallisation is present, clean the tank, strainer assembly, and foot valve; note: the foot valve should be positioned a few inches above the tank bottom to prevent future clogging by settled sludge
10Remove and inspect suction strainer assembly (foot valve with tube weight and strainer) — clean off any chemical scale, sludge, or crystallised deposits; confirm strainer mesh is not blocked; reinstall correctly with tube weight holding strainer fully submerged below solution level
11Confirm foot valve is fully submerged in chemical solution — foot valve above solution level will cause immediate loss of prime and pump will stop dosing; solution level must always be above foot valve
D.  INJECTION ASSEMBLY CHECK
12Inspect injection assembly (spring-loaded back check injection valve at injection point in pipe) — confirm arrow on injection fitting is pointing vertically upward; confirm fitting is in the main flow stream of the pipe (not in a pipe stub away from flow)
13If injection rate has been low or stopped, check injection fitting for scale blockage — clean injection fitting and check valve with 8% muriatic acid or undiluted vinegar per manual recommendation; rinse thoroughly after cleaning
14Confirm injection point is located downstream of filter and downstream of pH/ORP sensors (sensor must see untreated water; chemical injection after sensor gives correct feedback loop)

A.  PRE-SERVICE SAFETY
1SAFETY: Disconnect power to pump by unplugging from power outlet or isolating at distribution board before performing any maintenance — never repair or service metering pump while operating
2SAFETY: Wear protective gloves and safety glasses at all times when working on or near chemical metering pumps — chemicals present are hazardous
3Relieve pressure from suction and discharge tubing — close isolation valves if fitted, or depressurise system before disconnecting any tubing; always depressurise before disconnecting tubing per manual instruction
B.  ALL FOUR CHECK VALVES INSPECTION & CLEANING (Manual: every 4–6 months)
4Unscrew compression nut and remove suction tubing from suction check valve housing (bottom of pump head)
5Unscrew suction check valve body from pump head — carefully remove all seats, ball checks, and gaskets; lay out in order to ensure correct reassembly; inspect all components for wear, chemical attack, crystallisation, scale, deformation, or deterioration
6Unscrew discharge check valve body from top of pump head — remove and inspect all seats, ball checks, and gaskets; check for same defects; note: chemical crystallisation in check valves holds them open and prevents the pump from dosing
7Remove foot valve (strainer assembly at bottom of suction tubing in solution tank) — unscrew check valve body; inspect and clean internal ball check and seat inside foot valve
8Remove injection assembly (back check injection valve at pipe injection point) — unscrew check valve body; inspect and clean internal ball check and seat
9Clean all four check valve assemblies — remove scale and crystallised deposits gently; do not use abrasives; rinse all components with clean water; replace any ball check, valve seat, or gasket showing wear, deformation, chemical attack, or failure to seal cleanly; use KOPKIT KX100-AAAA replacement parts
10Reassemble all four check valves — reinstall components in correct sequence as shown in Parts Diagram in manual; reinstall valve bodies HAND-TIGHT ONLY; DO NOT use pipe tape or other sealants on valve threads; DO NOT use wrench
11Reinstall all tubing connections — hand-tighten all coupling nuts; do not use wrench
12Record: Suction check valve: OK / Parts replaced; Discharge check valve: OK / Parts replaced; Foot valve: OK / Parts replaced; Injection check valve: OK / Parts replaced
C.  DIAPHRAGM INSPECTION
13Remove four head screws from pump head face — remove pump head carefully; refer to Service and Repairs section of manual for full procedure
14Remove diaphragm by inserting one or two of the head bolts into diaphragm holes and turning counter-clockwise
15Inspect diaphragm for rupture, cracks, tears, deformation, or chemical attack on diaphragm material — a ruptured diaphragm causes chemical leakage around the pump head; replace immediately if any damage found; use KOPKIT KX100-AAAA for diaphragm replacement
16If diaphragm is intact and serviceable, reinstall — ensure drive bracket assembly is in fully retracted position; screw diaphragm in hand-tight, then back off one-quarter turn until screw holes align; replace pump head and tighten four head screws evenly and carefully to prevent cracking head
17If diaphragm requires replacement, install new diaphragm from KOPKIT KX100-AAAA following same reinstallation procedure; confirm discharge fitting arrow points vertically upward after head is replaced
18Record: Diaphragm condition: OK / Replaced (KOPKIT KX100-AAAA)
D.  PUMP HEAD & BODY INSPECTION
19Inspect pump head (SAN/PVC) for cracks, chemical attack, discoloration, or deformation — a cracked head must be replaced; cracks are often caused by over-tightening fittings with a wrench; always use hand-tight only
20Inspect suction valve housing and discharge valve housing for cracking, chemical attack, or deformation — replace damaged components
21Inspect bleed valve assembly (if installed) — confirm bleed valve knob operates freely; confirm bypass tubing is intact and correctly routed back to solution tank; confirm bleed valve is in closed position during normal operation
22Inspect motor housing for corrosion, heat damage, or physical damage — motor housing must cool sufficiently before handling; allow adequate time after shutdown before touching motor housing
23Inspect pump mounting — confirm pump is securely mounted on wall shelf, tank stand platform, or wall bracket; confirm pump is positioned so arrow on pump head is pointing vertically upward; confirm mounting keeps pump inaccessible to children and the public
24Confirm pump power cord is undamaged — inspect full length of power cord for abrasion, chemical attack, or damage; a damaged power cord must be replaced immediately per manual safety instruction
E.  RECOMMISSIONING & OUTPUT CALIBRATION
25Restore all tubing connections and check valve assemblies — confirm all coupling nuts are hand-tight; confirm arrows on pump head and injection fitting point vertically upward
26Reconnect power to pump; set output adjusting knob to maximum (loosen locking lever, turn knob one full counter-clockwise revolution, tighten locking lever) for priming
27Prime pump — if bleed valve is installed: with pump running, turn bleed valve knob counter-clockwise; run until a solid stream of chemical solution (no air bubbles) comes out of bypass tubing; close bleed valve by turning knob clockwise
28If no bleed valve: allow pump to prime by running at maximum output until chemical flow is confirmed at injection point; if prime is not achieved within a few minutes, disconnect power, depressurise, dampen discharge valve area with a few drops of solution, reconnect, and retry
29After successful priming, verify and set output calibration rate — measure actual output with graduated calibration cylinder; adjust output adjusting knob to required dosing rate; record: calibrated output _______ LPH | _______ GPD
30Inspect all connections for chemical leaks after recommissioning with pump running — no leakage at any connection; if leak is found, stop pump, depressurise, correct leak, and restart
F.  DOCUMENTATION
31Record all maintenance activities, parts inspected, and parts replaced — specify part name and source (KOPKIT KX100-AAAA)
32Record: Check valves serviced: Yes / No; Diaphragm inspected: Yes / No; Diaphragm replaced: Yes / No; Suction tubing condition: OK / Replaced; Discharge tubing condition: OK / Replaced
33Record: Post-service calibrated output: _______ LPH / GPD | Required dosing rate: _______ LPH / GPD
34Record: Next 3-monthly service due date: _______
