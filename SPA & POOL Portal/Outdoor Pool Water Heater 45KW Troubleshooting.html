HEATER TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX (INDOOR POOL 45KW)
Symptom / Operational ConditionDisplay Message / IndicatorPotential Root CauseTechnical Remedy / Action Required
Heater will not switch from Standby to ONNone (Amber Light Stay ON)Target temperature point has been achieved.Turn the temperature control dial to a value greater than the current pool water temperature to test command.
Heater will not switch from Standby to ONNO FLOW1. Circulation pump is switched off.
2. Pump is underpowered (< requirements).
3. Cartridge filter is dirty and needs replacement.
4. Sand/glass media filter back-pressure is too high.		Verify pump operation. Clean or replace cartridge filter. Perform a thorough backwash on the sand/glass media filter.
Heater will not switch from Standby to ONHEATER DELAYBuilt-in automated protective time delay active.Please refer to Page 9 of the technical manual for exact delay duration specs.
Heater will not switch from Standby to ONOUT OF RANGE1. Temperature sensor failure.
2. Internal flow tube temperature is outside measurement limits (0°C to 100°C). Often occurs in winter if water drops below 0°C.		Inspect sensor connections. If winter freezing is not the cause, replace the faulty temperature sensor.
Heater will not switch from Standby to ONSHUTDOWN HIGH AMBIENT!Internal electrical enclosure temperature has reached 60°C. Protects delicate electronics from damage. Caused by direct sunlight exposure or high ambient environments.The system will automatically restart heating once the internal enclosure temperature drops. Provide shade or auxiliary ventilation if problem persists.
No display on the heater when switched ONBlank Screen / No LightsExternal power supply failure.Inspect external fuses, Residual Current Devices (RCD), or isolator switches on the supply cable. Inspect the internal PCB control board fuse.
Flow tube feels warm / hot to touchNormal Display1. Heater is positioned in direct sunlight.
2. An air pocket is trapped inside the flow tube (highest risk point is at the top of the tank).		Note: Due to high thermal transfer efficiency, no warmth should normally be detectable on the tube. Purge trapped air from the tank and check shading.
Water entering the pool does not feel warmerNormal Running (Red Light ON)Temperature gain is completely proportional to water flow volume vs heater kW output. High flow rates dilute instantaneous warmth sensation.Normal condition. Example: A 6-kW heater with a 4,000 L/h pump yields a ~1.2°C temperature lift (undetectable to touch), but overall pool heating rate is identical.
Circuit breaker (MCB) trips after a few minutes/hoursPower Cuts Out1. Faulty or degraded circuit breaker.
2. Incorrectly sized MCB.
3. Insufficient power cable cross-sectional area.
4. Loose electrical terminal connections.		Contact a certified electrician to inspect installation. Check terminal connections at both the distribution board and heater terminal block. Upgrade cable/MCB size if needed.
Circuit breaker (MCB) or RCD trips INSTANTLYPower Cuts Out Immediately1. Short circuit in internal wiring or components.
2. Heating element damage or insulation breakdown to ground.
3. Internal moisture ingress.		Isolate power completely. Do not attempt to reset. Send the heater back to the manufacturer for factory repair / element replacement.
Power supply cable gets very hotNormal or High Load1. Insufficient cable cross-sectional area for current draw.
2. Loose terminal connections causing localized resistance heating.		Isolate system immediately. Contact an electrician to tighten loose connections and upgrade power supply cable cross-section to meet specifications.
ELECTRIC POOL HEATER - TROUBLESHOOTING & FUNCTIONAL TEST GUIDE
1. Quick Function Test
Phase / StepTest Procedure & Evaluation Details
Step 1Observe the main property electricity meter while the heater is actively running (Red Light 'ON').
Step 2Observe the meter again when the heater switches to Standby Mode (Amber Light 'ON').
ConclusionThe meter must record significantly more electricity consumption when the red light is 'ON'. If the electric heater draws power, that power is 100% converted into heat and transferred to the water (it is physically impossible for it to waste energy without heating).
2. Accurate Function Test (kW Output Verification)
Parameter / StepOperational Requirement & Calculation Methodology
Prerequisite 1The pool pump and heater must run continuously during the test period (Heater Red Light 'ON').
Prerequisite 2Turn OFF all other high-power appliances in the property (tumble dryers, showers, cookers, etc.) to ensure baseline accuracy.
Measurement T0Take the first reading from the property's main electricity meter.
Measurement T1Take the second reading from the main electricity meter exactly 1 hour (60 minutes) later.
CalculationSubtract the first reading from the second reading to find total units consumed (kWh).
Expected Results ExampleA baseline 1-HP domestic pool pump draws < 1.0 kW per hour. Therefore, a 6-kW heater combined with a 0.5-HP pump should result in an hourly consumption between 6.3 kW and 6.5 kW.
