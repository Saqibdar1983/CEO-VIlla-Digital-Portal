A.  VISUAL & MECHANICAL INSPECTION
1Inspect pump body, volute, and strainer pot for cracks, corrosion, or physical damage
2Inspect all union fittings — check O-rings and union nuts for tightness and leaks
3Inspect suction drain cover at cold plunge pool — confirm undamaged and secured; ANSI/ASME A112.19.8 compliant
4Inspect check valves on discharge line — confirm correct operation; no reverse flow
5Check pump mounting feet and base fixings — tighten loose fasteners
B.  STRAINER BASKET SERVICE
6Isolate pump power at circuit breaker before opening strainer pot — WARNING: do not open pot if pump recently ran dry (scalding vapor hazard per WhisperFlo manual)
7Relieve system pressure before opening pot; open and close isolation valves as required to de-pressurize
8Remove strainer pot lid clamp (counter-clockwise) and lid carefully — ensure pot is cool to touch before opening
9Remove strainer basket — inspect for cracks; replace if cracked (P/N 070387)
10Clean basket thoroughly — remove all debris; note any unusual sediment from cold plunge system
11Clean strainer pot interior and lid sealing surface
12Inspect lid O-ring (P/N 350013) — clean, lubricate, and replace if compressed or deteriorated
13Reinstall basket; fill pot with water; reinstall lid and clamp (clockwise to horizontal)
14Start pump; confirm primed and running normally; verify flow returns to cold plunge
C.  ELECTRICAL CHECKS
15Inspect power cable from circuit breaker to motor for damage, heat marks, or abrasion
16Test GFCI — press TEST: power interrupts; press RESET: power restores; report any failure immediately
17Check and tighten all electrical terminal screws at motor
18Inspect motor fan guard and cooling vents — clear accumulated dust and debris
D.  DUTY / STANDBY CHANGEOVER TEST
19Switch from duty pump (FP-CP-01) to standby pump (FP-CP-02) — confirm standby starts, primes, and runs without unusual noise or vibration
20Confirm cold plunge chiller continues to operate and cold water circulates during changeover
21Return system to duty pump after confirming standby is fully operational
E.  PERFORMANCE VERIFICATION
22Record suction pressure: _______ PSI; discharge pressure: _______ PSI
23Confirm no abnormal noise, vibration, or cavitation from operating pump
24Verify cold plunge water temperature is maintained within chiller setpoint range

#CHECKPOINTOK ✓NOT OK ✗Remarks
A.  VISUAL & SAFETY INSPECTION
1Inspect pump body, volute, strainer pot, all fittings, and pipe connections for cracks, corrosion, or damage
2Inspect all union fittings — replace deteriorated O-rings; tighten union nuts
3Inspect cold plunge suction drain cover — replace if cracked, loose, or non-compliant with ANSI/ASME A112.19.8
4Inspect check valves — confirm correct operation and no reverse flow
5Check and tighten all pump mounting fixings
B.  STRAINER BASKET & POT FULL SERVICE
6Isolate power at circuit breaker; de-pressurize system fully before opening pot
7Remove strainer pot lid carefully — verify cool and pressure-free
8Remove and clean strainer basket (P/N 070387) — replace if cracked
9Clean pot interior, drain plug ports, and lid sealing surface
10Remove both drain plugs (P/N 071131) and O-rings (P/N 192115) — replace O-rings; reinstall plugs securely
11Replace lid O-ring (P/N 350013) — lubricate with silicone grease before fitting
12Reinstall basket; fill pot with water; reinstall lid and clamp; start pump; confirm prime and flow
C.  SHAFT SEAL & INTERNAL INSPECTION
13Inspect pump body around seal plate for water seepage or mineral deposits indicating seal wear
14If seal leakage confirmed: isolate power; remove 6 bolts securing strainer pot; separate motor/hydraulics sub-assembly
15Inspect shaft seal (P/N PA-7 / PS1000 with ceramic seat) — replace if damaged; do not scratch polished seal faces
16Inspect impeller for wear, corrosion, or debris — clean passages; note impeller lock screw is LEFT-HAND thread
17Inspect diffuser and diffuser O-ring — replace if worn
18Reassemble: seal plate to motor (70 in-lbs cross-pattern); impeller (25 in-lbs); pot to motor (110 in-lbs cross-pattern)
D.  ELECTRICAL INSPECTION
19With power isolated, inspect power cable — check insulation, routing, and termination tightness at both ends
20Tighten all electrical terminal connections at motor
21Test GFCI — confirm TEST trips power; RESET restores; record result and date
22Inspect motor fan, fan guard, and cooling vents — clean all obstructions
E.  RECOMMISSIONING
23Prime duty pump; confirm no air in system; verify flow to cold plunge chiller circuit
24Switch to standby pump — confirm primes and runs; record pressures
25Return to duty pump; record suction pressure: _______ PSI; discharge pressure: _______ PSI
26Verify cold plunge water temperature maintained at chiller setpoint; confirm no leaks at any connection
F.  DOCUMENTATION
27Record all maintenance activities and findings in equipment history log
28List all parts replaced with description and part number (P/N)
29Update next service dates in maintenance planner
30Submit checklist to Supervisor for review and countersignature
