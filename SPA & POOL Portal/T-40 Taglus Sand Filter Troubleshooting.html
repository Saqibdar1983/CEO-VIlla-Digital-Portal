Safety Warning
TRAPPED AIR CAN LAUNCH THE CLOSURE WITH DEADLY FORCE!
Before repositioning valves or servicing the filtration system: disconnect power to the filter pump and automatic controls at the circuit breaker, open the manual air bleeder valve, and wait until all pressure is relieved.
Never adjust or tighten the closure while the system is pressurized or the filter pump is running.
After service, follow the start-up procedure exactly and replace worn or damaged parts immediately.
