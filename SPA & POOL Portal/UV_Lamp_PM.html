A.  PRE-SERVICE SAFETY ISOLATION
1Notify supervisor before commencing annual service — obtain permit to work if required
2Isolate and lock off electrical supply to UV unit at main isolator or distribution board — confirm power is OFF before proceeding
3Turn off circulation pump serving the UV unit to stop water flow
4Close isolation valves upstream and downstream of UV unit
5Allow unit to cool — quartz sleeve and lamp assembly will be hot immediately after operation; allow at least 10 minutes before handling
6Drain UV vessel completely before opening any union fittings or lamp housing
B.  UV LAMP REPLACEMENT  ·  Lamp Code: SP-UV-LAMP-EL
7Remove union fittings at each end of UV chamber to release vessel from pipe assembly
8Withdraw UV lamp and quartz sleeve assembly from housing — handle with care; do not apply excessive lateral force
9Remove used UV lamps from quartz sleeve — do not touch glass surface of lamps with bare hands (skin oils reduce UV output); use clean gloves
10Dispose of used UV lamps as hazardous waste in accordance with local regulations — lamps contain mercury and must not be placed in general waste
11Install 2 new UV lamps (lamp code SP-UV-LAMP-EL) — confirm lamp code on packaging matches before installation; do not touch glass surface of new lamps
C.  QUARTZ SLEEVE INSPECTION & CLEANING
12Remove quartz sleeve from housing for inspection
13Inspect quartz sleeve for cracks, chips, or fractures — if any damage is found, replace immediately; a cracked sleeve significantly reduces UV output and must not be reinstalled
14Inspect sleeve for limescale deposits — limescale blocks UV transmission and reduces disinfection effectiveness. In Jeddah hard water conditions, expect significant limescale accumulation annually
15If limescale is present, descale quartz sleeve using kettle descaling solution — soak per manufacturer recommendation; rinse thoroughly with clean water after descaling
16Dry quartz sleeve completely before reinstallation — do not reinstall a wet sleeve
17Reinstall clean and inspected quartz sleeve into housing
D.  O-RINGS, SEALS & BODY INSPECTION
18Remove and inspect O-ring seals (part code SP-UV-ORS) — replace O-rings if worn, flattened, cracked, or permanently compressed
19Install new O-rings (SP-UV-ORS) — ensure correct seating in groove; do not use petroleum-based lubricants on O-rings
20Inspect blue compression fittings for cracking, yellowing, or brittleness — replace if damaged or deformed
21Inspect body housing for cracks, UV degradation, or structural damage — replace housing if any cracking is found
22Inspect plastic body components for excessive wear — inspect body and compression fittings whenever lamp change is carried out (per manufacturer manual)
E.  DOSING TUBE REPLACEMENT
23Inspect dosing tube for kinks, blockages, hardening, cracking, or brittleness — any of these defects will restrict chemical dosing and must be corrected
24Replace dosing tube per manufacturer recommendation (every 6–12 months); replace regardless of age if any defect is found
25Reinstall new dosing tube — confirm correct connection to dosing pump and dosing port; tube must be free of kinks and not pinched along its length
F.  REASSEMBLY & RECOMMISSIONING
26Reassemble UV chamber — reinstall quartz sleeve with new lamps seated correctly; ensure all O-rings are correctly positioned and seated before closing housing
27Reconnect union fittings at both ends — hand-tighten firmly; do not use tools to over-tighten as this can crack union body
28Open isolation valves and restore water flow to UV unit
29Inspect all union fittings and connections for water leaks with water flowing — do not restore electrical power until all leak checks are complete and no leaks are present
30Restore electrical supply at main isolator — confirm UV lamp illuminates and controller activates normally
31Confirm flow switch is responding correctly — lamp should operate when flow is present and shut off when flow stops
32Confirm lamp life LED countdown timer resets to 9,000 hours after new lamp installation — record: Timer reset confirmed: Yes / No. If timer does not reset, follow manual reset procedure on controller
33Verify controller display shows normal operation with no fault codes or error messages after recommissioning
