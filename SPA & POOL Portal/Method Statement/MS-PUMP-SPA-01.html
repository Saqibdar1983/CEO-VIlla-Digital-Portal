1.  PURPOSE & SCOPE
1.1  Purpose

This Method Statement describes the approved procedure for the supply, handling, installation, testing, and commissioning of all pool circulation, feature, and drainage pumps serving the SPA & Pool Facility at CEO Villa, Al Murjan, Jeddah. It defines the work sequence, technical requirements, safety controls, and quality checks to ensure full compliance with the project specifications, manufacturer recommendations, and applicable international codes.

1.2  Scope of Work

This document covers the complete installation of the following 12 pumps:

Type A – Centrifugal End-Suction Pumps (Above-Ground / Pentair):
  • FP-ODP-01 – Outdoor Pool Filtration Pump No. 1  (Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-901, 2.2 kW)
  • FP-ODP-02 – Outdoor Pool Filtration Pump No. 2  (Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-901, 2.2 kW)
  • NP-ODP-01 – Outdoor Pool Waterfall Pump  (Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-801, 1.5 kW)
  • WFP-ODP-01 – Outdoor Pool Jet Nozzle Pump  (Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-751, 1.1 kW)
  • FP-INP-01 – Indoor Pool Filtration Pump No. 1  (Pentair SuperFlo SF-N1-1 1FET, 0.75 kW)
  • FP-INP-02 – Indoor Pool Filtration Pump No. 2  (Pentair SuperFlo SF-N1-1 1FET, 0.75 kW)
  • NP-INP-01 – Indoor Pool Jet Nozzle Pump  (Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-901, 2.2 kW)
  • FP-CPC-01 – Cold Plunge Filtration Pump No. 1  (Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-801, 1.5 kW)
  • FP-CPC-02 – Cold Plunge Filtration Pump No. 2  (Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-801, 1.5 kW)
Type B – Submersible Pumps (Pedrollo):
  • WFSP-01 – Feature Wall Submersible Pump No. 1  (Pedrollo TOP 3, 0.55 kW)
  • WFSP-02 – Feature Wall Submersible Pump No. 2  (Pedrollo TOP 3, 0.55 kW)
  • SP-01 – Sump Pit Drainage Pump  (Pedrollo TOP 4N, 0.75 kW)
The scope includes:
  • Receiving, handling, and inspection of all pump equipment at site
  • Civil & structural interface – verification of housekeeping pads, sump pit, and pump room drains
  • Mechanical installation of pumps, strainers, hair & lint baskets, and associated fittings
  • Piping connections: suction, discharge, unions, isolation valves, check valves, flexible connectors
  • Electrical connections and motor protection by a licensed electrician
  • Functional testing, hydraulic performance verification, and commissioning
  • Documentation, as-built records, and handover to IHCC / Client

5.  HEALTH, SAFETY & ENVIRONMENTAL REQUIREMENTS
5.1  General Safety
  • All personnel must complete the project site induction before commencing work
  • Daily toolbox talk (TBT) to be conducted by the Foreman before work starts each day
  • All works to comply with IHCC Safety Plan and Saudi labour regulations
  • Maintain clear emergency evacuation routes; post emergency contact numbers in plant room
  • Place a 5 kg dry powder fire extinguisher in each equipment room
5.2  Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) – Mandatory at All Times
  • Safety helmet, safety shoes (steel toe-cap), high-visibility vest
  • Safety gloves (mechanical grip), safety glasses, ear protection (>85 dB areas)
  • Electrical insulated gloves – Class 00 minimum – for all electrical connection work
5.3  Electrical Safety
WARNING:All electrical connection works must be performed by a licensed electrician only. Pool electrical systems require GFCI/RCD protection per NFPA 70 Article 680 – this is mandatory, not optional.
  • Implement Lock-Out / Tag-Out (LOTO) procedure before working on any electrical panel
  • Verify circuit is de-energised with approved voltage tester before touching terminals
  • GFCI/RCD (30 mA) protection required on every pool and submersible pump circuit
  • Earth-bond all pump motor frames and connected metalwork: min. 6 mm² green/yellow cable
  • Insulation resistance test must pass (≥ 100 MΩ at 500 V) before energising any motor
5.4  Mechanical & Lifting Safety
  • Inspect all lifting equipment for valid certification before use (SWL clearly marked)
  • No manual lift of items exceeding 25 kg without mechanical aid
  • Never stand under a suspended load; barricade plant room during installation
  • Do NOT lift submersible pumps (Pedrollo) by the electrical cable – use the pump handle or sling
5.5  Environmental
  • Collect all packaging waste and dispose via approved project waste contractor
  • Any oil or lubricant spillage to be contained immediately with absorbent pads
  • Pool chemicals must be stored only in the dedicated chemical store – not in the pump room
  • Conduct noisy operations during approved working hours only
6.  INSTALLATION PROCEDURE
6.1  Pre-Installation Activities
6.1.1  Document & Drawing Review
  1. Obtain and review the latest revision of approved Shop Drawings (SB-ASB-SD-ME-008-R1), pump data sheets, and this Method Statement before any work begins.
  2. Cross-check all pump tags (FP-ODP-01, FP-ODP-02, NP-ODP-01, WFP-ODP-01, FP-INP-01, FP-INP-02, NP-INP-01, FP-CPC-01, FP-CPC-02, WFSP-01, WFSP-02, SP-01) on drawings with physical marked locations in the equipment room.
  3. Verify motor voltages, phases, and power ratings on approved submittals match the available electrical supply (230 V / 60 Hz / Single Phase) before any pump is brought to site.
6.1.2  Material Receiving & Inspection
  1. Upon delivery, inspect all pump boxes for external damage before signing the delivery note. Photograph any damage immediately.
  2. Open each box and verify: pump model matches approved submittal; no visible cracks in casing, shaft, or impeller; motor shaft rotates freely by hand; strainer basket (centrifugal pumps) is intact and seated correctly.
  3. For submersible pumps (WFSP-01, WFSP-02, SP-01): inspect the full length of the H07RN-F cable for any nick, cut, or abrasion in the insulation. Any defect is cause for rejection.
  4. Submit Material Inspection Request (MIR) to IHCC and Consultant. Obtain written approval before any pump is installed.
  5. Store pumps in a dry, covered area. Avoid direct sunlight, water spray, or contact with pool chemicals.
6.1.3  Site Readiness Verification
  1. Verify concrete housekeeping pads (for centrifugal pumps) are fully cured, flat, and level within ± 2 mm per metre.
  2. For submersible pumps: verify feature wall sump (WFSP-01, WFSP-02) and sump pit (SP-01) are clean, correct dimensions, and waterproofing is complete.
  3. Confirm suction and discharge pipe stubs are installed and plugged at correct heights and orientations per drawings.
  4. Confirm electrical conduit stubs and pull-out boxes are in place at each pump location.
  5. Ensure plant room plastering, waterproofing, and floor drains are complete before pump installation.
CAUTION:No pump installation shall commence until the MIR is approved by the Consultant and all site readiness checks above have been signed off.
6.2  Type A: Centrifugal Pump Installation (FP-ODP-01/02, NP-ODP-01, WFP-ODP-01, FP-INP-01/02, NP-INP-01, FP-CPC-01/02)
NOTE:This procedure applies to all nine above-ground Pentair centrifugal pumps. WhisperFlo and SuperFlo models share the same installation method. Installation sequence is the same for each pump – complete one pump fully before starting the next.
6.2.1  Setting the Anti-Vibration Base
  1. Place neoprene anti-vibration pads (Shore 40–50, min. 6 mm thick) at the pump base-plate corner positions on the housekeeping pad.
  2. Level the pads: tolerance ≤ 2 mm per metre in both longitudinal and transverse directions.
  3. Mark anchor bolt hole positions on the housekeeping pad using the pump base plate as a drilling template.
  4. Drill anchor holes to the depth and diameter required by the chemical anchor system (minimum M10 stainless anchors). Clean holes with compressed air before installing anchors per Hilti/Rawl specification.
6.2.2  Pump Positioning & Anchoring
  1. Lower the pump onto the anti-vibration pads using a pump dolly or chain block. Do not drag the pump across the floor – the casing, impeller, and shaft seal are fragile.
  2. Align the suction port and discharge port with the corresponding pipe stubs shown on the drawing.
  3. Install base plate bolts (M10 × 50 mm stainless steel hex-head) through base plate into anchors. Do not tighten fully yet.
  4. Recheck level in both directions. Once confirmed, torque all anchor bolts to 45–50 Nm.
  5. Confirm maintenance clearances: minimum 500 mm on all four sides; minimum 300 mm above the strainer lid.
6.2.3  Suction-Side Piping Connections
CAUTION:Suction piping is critical. Incorrect suction piping is the primary cause of pump cavitation, air locking, and premature mechanical seal failure.
  1. Install a 2" Y-strainer (40 mesh) on the suction line, upstream of the isolation valve. Strainer blowdown plug must face downward.
  2. Install a 2" gate valve (not a ball valve) immediately before the suction port. Gate valves have low head loss on suction – ball valves do not.
  3. Install a 2" rubber flexible union connector between the gate valve and pump suction port to isolate vibration from the pipework.
  4. Install a 2" true-union fitting at the pump suction port to allow pump removal without disturbing the pipework.
  5. Suction pipe must run horizontally or slope downward toward the pump – no high points that can trap air. Use an eccentric reducer (flat side up) if reducing pipe size on suction – never a concentric reducer.
  6. Maximum suction lift: 1.5 m from water surface to pump centreline. Verify against each pump curve.
  7. Support all suction pipework independently on pipe hangers – do not allow pipe weight to rest on the pump suction port.
6.2.4  Discharge-Side Piping Connections
  1. Install a 2" true-union fitting at the pump discharge port.
  2. Install a 2" rubber flexible union connector after the true-union on the discharge side.
  3. Install a 2" swing check valve (NRV) with flow arrow in the correct direction, to prevent backflow through the pump when stopped.
  4. Install a 2" gate valve after the check valve as the discharge isolation valve.
  5. Install a glycerine-filled pressure gauge (0–4 bar, 63 mm) via an isolation petcock, after the discharge gate valve.
  6. Support all discharge pipework on hangers at maximum 900 mm spacing.
  7. Apply PTFE tape (3 wraps) plus thread compound to all male-threaded joints. Tighten hand-tight plus ¾ turn – never over-tighten plastic-threaded joints.
6.2.5  Strainer Basket Check
  1. Before closing pipework, open the strainer lid and confirm the basket is clean and properly seated in its housing.
  2. Verify the strainer lid O-ring is in good condition and correctly seated.
  3. Do not use solvents near the Noryl casing of WhisperFlo/SuperFlo pumps – most solvents degrade it.
6.3  Type B: Submersible Pump Installation (WFSP-01, WFSP-02, SP-01)
WARNING:Submersible pumps must never run dry. Even 30 seconds of dry running can destroy the motor. Fill the sump with water to the required level before any trial energisation.
6.3.1  Sump / Pit Preparation
  1. Inspect the sump (WFSP-01, WFSP-02 – feature wall sump) and sump pit (SP-01) for cracks, debris, and correct dimensions.
  2. Flush the sump with clean water and brush away any construction grit or debris from the base.
  3. Confirm a level, flat, solid base at the sump floor for the pump to rest on.
  4. Confirm the cable entry point (conduit sleeve or waterproof gland) in the sump wall is present and correctly sized for the H07RN-F cable.
6.3.2  Pump Lowering & Securing
  1. NEVER lift the pump by its electrical cable. Use the pump handle or a stainless steel sling under the pump body.
  2. Attach a stainless steel (SS316) suspension cable (minimum 4 mm diameter) to the pump handle using a shackle. This cable is the primary support during operation.
  3. Lower the pump gently to the sump floor. Ensure the pump sits level and the discharge port faces toward the discharge pipe stub.
  4. Fix the suspension cable to a SS316 eyebolt or bracket on the sump wall or covering slab. The cable must be taut – not loose or coiled underwater.
  5. Route the electrical cable out of the sump through the conduit sleeve. Form a drip loop before the junction box to prevent water ingress by capillary action.
6.3.3  Discharge Piping
  1. Connect the pump discharge port (1" BSP – TOP 3 and TOP 4N) to pipework using a flexible hose or union connector rated to the discharge pressure.
  2. Install a 1" non-return valve (NRV) at the pump discharge to prevent siphoning back through the pump when stopped.
  3. Install an isolation ball valve on the discharge line above the sump for servicing.
  4. Ensure the discharge pipe rises above the maximum water level in the sump before turning horizontal – this prevents siphoning.
6.3.4  Dry Run Protection (Float Switch)
  1. Install a float switch in each sump, wired to cut off power to the pump if the water level drops below the minimum safe submersion depth (minimum 100 mm above pump top).
  2. Use an IP67-rated junction box for the float switch wiring inside the sump.
  3. Label the float switch: "PUMP DRY RUN PROTECTION – DO NOT REMOVE OR BYPASS."
CAUTION:The Pedrollo TOP 3 and TOP 4N have built-in thermal overload protection, but this does not prevent damage from dry running. The float switch is mandatory secondary protection.
6.4  Electrical Connections – All Pumps
WARNING:All electrical works must be by a licensed electrician. Pool electrical systems are regulated under NFPA 70 Article 680 – non-compliance is a safety hazard and a contractual non-conformance.
  1. Verify that each pump circuit has a dedicated GFCI/RCD breaker: 30 mA sensitivity for all pool and submersible pump circuits (NFPA 70 Art. 680.21).
  2. Pull supply cable from DB through PVC conduit (minimum 20 mm diameter) to each pump local isolator or pull-cord switch.
  3. Cable sizing (minimum): 2.5 mm² for WFL-901 (10.7 A FLA); 2.5 mm² for WFL-801 (9.7 A FLA); 1.5 mm² for SuperFlo (confirm nameplate); 1.5 mm² for TOP 3 (3.7 A); 2.5 mm² for TOP 4N (5.0 A). Confirm with electrical engineer.
  4. For centrifugal pumps: terminate cable at motor terminal box. Verify supply voltage (230 V, 60 Hz). Connect L, N, Earth as per motor wiring diagram.
  5. For submersible pumps (WFSP-01, WFSP-02, SP-01): join the supply cable to the pump cable using an IP68-rated waterproof connector inside an IP67 junction box mounted above the sump water line. No splice connections inside the water.
  6. Install earth bonding conductor (minimum 6 mm² green/yellow) from each pump body and all connected metalwork to the main earthing system, per NFPA 70 Art. 680.26.
  7. Test insulation resistance at 500 V DC megohmmeter before first energisation. Acceptance: ≥ 100 MΩ. Record result for every pump.
  8. Energise and verify motor rotation: WhisperFlo and SuperFlo – counter-clockwise when viewed from the motor fan end. If rotation is wrong, de-energise and swap any two supply phase conductors.
  9. For Pedrollo submersible pumps (WFSP-01, WFSP-02, SP-01): rotation is factory-set and should not be modified.
6.5  Pump Priming – Centrifugal Pumps (All Type A Pumps)
WARNING:A centrifugal pump must NEVER be run dry. Even 30 seconds without water destroys the mechanical seal. Prime fully before every start.
  1. Close the discharge gate valve fully.
  2. Open the suction gate valve fully.
  3. Remove the strainer lid and fill the strainer basket housing with clean water until water overflows from the top.
  4. Replace the strainer lid and hand-tighten the lid knob. Do not over-tighten.
  5. Open the air vent cock on the pump casing (if fitted). The pump is primed when water flows steadily from the vent without air bubbles. Close the vent.
  6. Open the discharge valve to 25% (quarter-open) before starting the motor.
  7. Start the pump. Gradually open the discharge valve to full over 30 seconds. Monitor for vibration, noise, and correct discharge pressure.
CAUTION:If the pump fails to prime within 2 minutes, stop immediately. Re-prime and check all suction-side joints for air leaks. Repeated failure to prime always indicates a suction leak.
