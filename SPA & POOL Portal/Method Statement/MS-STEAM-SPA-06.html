1.  PURPOSE & SCOPE
1.1  Purpose

This Method Statement defines the approved procedure for the installation, plumbing connections, electrical connections, K3 controller installation, and commissioning of the SPA Steam Generator (Tag: SG-01) within the SPA & Pool Facility of the CEO Villa Project. The document ensures full compliance with the Amerec AK14 Installation and Operation Manual (Doc. 4211-1552), ANSI/UL Standard 499, CAN/CSA Standard C22.2 No. 64, applicable electrical codes, and the project quality and safety plan.

1.3  Scope of Work
  • Receiving, inspection, and MIR submission for SG-01 and K3 controller
  • Mounting the AK14 generator on wall or floor as per approved drawing (must be perfectly level)
  • Cold water supply connection: 3/8" NPT inlet with isolation valve and optional inline filter
  • Steam line installation: 1/2" hard-drawn copper pipe from generator to steam room
  • Steam head installation in steam room at correct height (460 mm above finished floor), outlet facing downward
  • Pressure Relief Valve (PRV, 10 PSI / 38 kPa) installation and discharge piping to floor drain
  • Tank drain connection: 3/4" NPT ball valve + union, piped to gravity floor drain
  • Electrical supply: 400-415V/3N, 30A dedicated 4-pole MCB; field reconfiguration from single-phase to 3-phase per AK IOM page 18-19
  • K3 controller installation in steam room and low-voltage control cable in separate conduit to generator
  • Water quality pre-check before first start-up (Jeddah hard water risk)
  • First start-up, K3 setpoint configuration, and full functional test
WARNING:The AK14 is factory-wired for single-phase. It MUST be reconfigured to 3-phase 400-415V~N3 by repositioning the internal jumper on the terminal block per the wiring diagram on pages 18-19 of the Amerec AK IOM (Doc. 4211-1552). This MUST be done BEFORE energizing the unit. Failure to reconfigure will result in incorrect operation or permanent equipment damage. Aluminium wire is NOT permitted — copper wire only. Amerec warranty is voided by aluminium wiring.

4.4  Water Quality Requirements for Steam Generator Feed

Jeddah municipal water is generally hard. Before first start-up, the water supply to SG-01 must be tested and compared against Amerec's required range. If not within specification, an inline water softener or de-scaler must be installed upstream of the generator.

ParameterAmerec Required RangeRemarks
Water Hardness10 – 30 ppm (0.5 – 1.75 gpg)High calcium causes scale on heating elements and probes. Most critical parameter in Jeddah.
Total Alkalinity150 – 700 ppm (8.75 – 40.8 gpg)Low alkalinity can cause foaming, which affects water level sensors and controller accuracy.
Silica15 – 25 ppm (1.28 – 1.45 gpg)Higher silica causes hard scale deposits on tank and elements.
pH10.5 – 11.5Must be within range to prevent foaming and scale formation inside tank.
WARNING:If Jeddah municipal water hardness exceeds 30 ppm, install an inline water softener or de-scaler on the cold water feed line to the generator. Calcium scale build-up on heating elements is the most common cause of AK14 element failure in the Middle East. This is NOT covered by Amerec warranty if water quality was not properly managed.
5.  HEALTH, SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT
5.1  Key Hazards & Controls
HazardRiskControl Measure
Steam — 100°C Saturated Steam Severe scalding burns. Steam head, 1/2" copper steam pipe, and PRV discharge are all severe burn hazards during and after operation. Never touch the steam head or steam pipe during or after operation. Min. 300 mm safe zone around steam outlet. PRV discharge must terminate at floor drain, never into steam room.
Electrical — 415V 3-Phase Electrocution. Reconfiguring the terminal block jumper inside the generator requires the panel cover to be opened. LOTO at DB before opening generator panel. Licensed electrician only. Test zero volts with multimeter before touching any terminals inside generator.
PRV Discharge — 10 PSI Steam PRV discharge of 100°C steam/water at 10 PSI can cause severe burns to anyone nearby. Pipe PRV discharge via 3/4" copper to floor drain in mechanical room. Never direct PRV into steam room. Never stand over PRV discharge point.
Hot Water Scalding — Tank Drain Tank drain water may be up to 100°C if drained immediately after operation. Allow generator to cool minimum 30 minutes after use before draining tank for maintenance. Warn all maintenance personnel.
Wet Area / Slip Hazard Steam room floors become very wet and slippery during installation and operation testing. Non-slip safety footwear inside steam room. Post CAUTION — WET FLOOR sign during installation and commissioning.
Hot Copper Steam Pipe The 1/2" copper steam line reaches very high temperatures during normal operation. Insulate exposed steam pipe where accessible to occupants per IEC 60364-7-703. Post DO NOT TOUCH warning near steam pipe where accessible.
5.2  PPE & LOTO Requirements

All personnel in the installation area: safety helmet, safety shoes, high-visibility vest, chemical gloves, safety glasses. Inside steam room during installation: non-slip safety footwear, eye protection (steam/moisture). Electrician during terminal block reconfiguration: insulated gloves (CAT III minimum), face shield. LOTO procedure: switch OFF the SG-01 dedicated MCB at DB, apply personal lock and tag, test zero volts at generator terminals with calibrated multimeter, then proceed.

6.  INSTALLATION PROCEDURE
6.1  Pre-Installation Activities
6.1.1  Material Inspection
  1. Inspect SG-01 (Amerec AK14) and K3 controller upon delivery. Check for shipping damage: dents to generator body, damage to the 3/8" water inlet valve, bent 1/2" steam outlet nipple, or cracked K3 keypad face. Photograph any damage.
  2. Verify nameplate data: Model AK14, Serial/Code 10413683318, 14 kW, 400-415V. Submit MIR for SG-01 and K3 controller. Obtain consultant approval in writing before any installation begins.
  3. Confirm the 10 PSI Pressure Relief Valve (PRV) is included in the delivery package. This is factory-supplied with the AK14 and must not be substituted with any other valve.
  4. Confirm the Amerec AK IOM (Doc. 4211-1552) and K3 Owner's Manual (Doc. 4209-42) are physically present on site. These are required throughout the installation.
  5. Test the cold water supply pressure at the generator installation point: must be within 20–100 PSI (138–690 kPa). If below 20 PSI, install a booster pump. If above 100 PSI, install a pressure reducing valve upstream.
  6. Submit a water quality test from the cold water supply to SG-01. Compare hardness, pH, alkalinity, and silica against the Amerec requirements in Section 4.4. If water hardness exceeds 30 ppm, install an inline water softener or de-scaler before proceeding.
6.2  Generator Mounting
WARNING:The generator MUST be perfectly level — front-to-back and side-to-side. A generator that is not level will not fill or drain correctly and will not operate properly. Confirm with a spirit level. The arrows on the top of the generator must point straight up.
  1. Confirm the installation location per drawing SB-ASB-SD-ME-008-R1. The location must be: indoor only, protected from freezing, dry and well-ventilated, minimum 0.2 m³ (7 cu ft) space around the generator, with 330 mm service clearance on the element access side, and steam line run to steam room less than 6 m (3 m preferred).
  2. For WALL MOUNTING: locate and mark two solid wall studs at 368 mm (14.5") centres. Drill pilot holes and install the two #10 × 38 mm (1.5") wall screws (supplied by Amerec) into solid studs or rated wall backing. Verify the wall can support the generator weight.
  3. Carefully hang the generator on the two mounting screws. Tighten screws securely. Verify the generator is level using a spirit level — check front-to-back AND side-to-side. The arrows on the top of the unit must point vertically upward.
  4. For FLOOR MOUNTING (if applicable per drawing): place the generator on a level surface and secure against movement. The unit must still be confirmed level on both axes before any piping is connected.
  5. Do not connect any piping or electrical connections until the generator is confirmed level and securely mounted.
6.3  Cold Water Supply Connection
  1. Flush the building cold water supply line thoroughly before making the final connection to the generator. This removes installation debris that would foul the generator's internal water valve and water level probe.
  2. Install the 3/8" isolation ball valve (cold water rated) on the supply line close to the generator. This serves as the emergency shut-off and service valve. It must be in an accessible, clearly visible location.
  3. Install an optional 3/8" inline filter/strainer on the supply line upstream of the isolation valve. This is strongly recommended given Jeddah water quality.
  4. Install a 3/8" NPT male adapter and union at the generator water inlet valve connection. The union allows the generator to be disconnected for servicing without cutting pipes.
  5. Make the final connection of the 3/8" copper supply line to the generator water inlet valve. Use pipe thread compound rated for drinking water service above 212°F (100°C) on all NPT connections at the generator. Do not rely on PTFE tape alone for the generator port connections.
  6. Do NOT open the cold water isolation valve until all plumbing is complete and checked.
6.4  Steam Line Installation
CRITICAL — STEAM LINE RULES (Amerec IOM):These rules are mandatory. Violating them causes equipment damage or injury. (1) NO valves, shut-offs, or restrictions of any kind in the steam line — EVER. (2) NO interconnection of steam lines between separate generators. (3) NO dips or valleys in the steam line — condensation will collect and cause spitting of hot water from the steam head. (4) Steam line must slope continuously so condensation drains back to the generator or forward to the steam room — no level horizontal runs. (5) The PRV discharge must NEVER be connected to the steam line.
  1. Install a 1/2" NPT male sweat adapter directly into the generator steam outlet (top of tank). Follow immediately with a 1/2" union — this is for service disconnection only and must never be used as a valve.
  2. Run the 1/2" hard-drawn copper steam line from the generator union to the steam room wall penetration. The line must slope continuously — either back toward the generator or forward toward the steam room — with no dips, valleys, or level horizontal sections. A slope of approximately 5–10 mm per metre is sufficient.
  3. Support the steam line with copper or stainless clips at maximum 1.0 m intervals. Verify the slope is maintained at every support point.
  4. Keep the steam line as short as possible. Preferred maximum: 3 m (10 ft). Absolute maximum: 6 m (20 ft). Steam lines over 6 m must be insulated to prevent excessive condensation.
  5. Minimize elbows and bends. Where bends are unavoidable, maintain the continuous slope on both sides of the bend.
  6. At the steam room wall: drill a 35 mm (1-3/8") diameter hole at the steam head location. Centre the 1/2" copper pipe in this hole.
  7. The steam line must enter the steam room at 460 mm (18") above the finished floor level. For rooms with a bench or ledge, minimum 305 mm (12") above the bench rim. The pipe must protrude 9.5 mm (3/8") from the finished wall surface inside the steam room.
  8. Install a 1/2" NPT male adapter at the termination point inside the steam room. Fill the gap between the copper pipe and the 35 mm wall hole completely with 100% neutral-cure silicone caulk. This seal is critical — any steam escaping into the wall cavity will cause severe structural damage over time.
NOTE:For the AK14 (14 kW), the Amerec manual recommends installing TWO steam heads to distribute steam more evenly in larger rooms and reduce noise during operation. Confirm with the consultant/client whether two steam heads are specified before closing out the steam line installation.
6.5  Steam Head Installation
  1. Apply 100% silicone caulk to the back (wall side) of the steam head insulator plate.
  2. Slide the insulator plate over the 1/2" NPT male adapter stub protruding from the wall and press firmly against the finished wall surface.
  3. Thread the steam head onto the 1/2" NPT stub. Hand-tighten until the steam head outlet opening faces DOWNWARD. If hand-tight does not achieve the downward orientation, apply Teflon tape to the thread to make fine adjustments. The steam outlet MUST face downward to direct steam toward the floor and disperse it throughout the room.
  4. Tighten the hex-head retaining screw underneath the steam head with the Allen wrench provided to secure the head in position.
  5. Verify the steam head position meets all clearance requirements: minimum 150 mm (6") from any adjacent second steam head; minimum 305 mm (12") from any side wall; minimum 150 mm (6") from any bench or seating surface.
  6. Seal all fixture holes in the steam room walls (steam head, K3 conduit entry) with 100% silicone caulk — no gaps. Steam moisture entering wall cavities causes rot and mould behind the tile finish.
6.6  Pressure Relief Valve (PRV) Installation
WARNING:The PRV discharge must NEVER be directed into the steam room. It must NEVER be connected to the steam line. The PRV discharges at 10 PSI with 100°C steam/water — anyone in the steam room at the time would sustain severe burns.
  1. Install the supplied 10 PSI (38 kPa) pressure relief valve into its designated port on the AK14 generator tank. The PRV must be installed within 150 mm of the generator body. Use thread compound on the NPT threads.
  2. Run a 3/4" copper pipe from the PRV outlet to the floor drain in the generator room (pump room / mechanical space). This pipe must slope continuously downward to the drain with no uphill sections or dips.
  3. The PRV discharge pipe must not route back through the steam room. It must terminate freely above a floor drain in the mechanical room where the discharge can flow safely away.
6.7  Tank Drain Installation
  1. Install a 3/4" NPT nipple directly into the tank drain port on the bottom of the generator.
  2. Install a 3/4" brass ball valve on the nipple. This is the manual drain valve for tank cleaning and descaling maintenance. The valve must be accessible in the installed position.
  3. Install a union after the ball valve to allow easy generator disconnection during maintenance.
  4. Run a 3/4" copper line from the union to the nearest floor drain. All drain lines must run continuously downhill. No uphill sections at any point. Do NOT drain into the steam room.
6.8  Electrical Connections
WARNING:LOTO at DB before any electrical work inside the generator. Licensed electrician only. 75°C minimum copper wire is mandatory — Amerec warranty is voided by aluminium wire. The 3-phase jumper reconfiguration (Section 6.8.2) is CRITICAL and must be performed before energizing the unit.
6.8.1  Power Supply Wiring
  1. Run 4C × 4 mm² (minimum) 75°C-rated copper cable in 25 mm GI conduit from the dedicated 30A 4-pole MCB at the DB to the generator location. Leave 1.2 m (4 ft) of slack at the generator end for termination.
  2. At the DB: install a dedicated 30A 4-pole MCB (3P+N) and 30 mA RCD (if required by local authority). Label clearly: "SG-01 — Steam Generator AK14". No other loads may share this circuit.
  3. Route the supply cable through the POWER ENTRY knockout on the generator enclosure. Install an approved strain relief gland at the entry point.
6.8.2  3-Phase Reconfiguration (CRITICAL — Factory Is Single-Phase)
CRITICAL:Before connecting power, perform the 3-phase reconfiguration. The AK14 leaves the factory configured for single-phase. Energizing a single-phase configured unit on a 3-phase 415V supply WILL damage the unit. This step is mandatory.
  1. With LOTO applied at DB: open the generator electrical panel cover. Locate the power terminal block. Identify the factory jumper that configures single-phase operation.
  2. Reposition the internal jumper from the single-phase position to the 3-phase 400-415V~N3 position per the wiring diagram on pages 18-19 of the Amerec AK IOM (Doc. 4211-1552). Do NOT alter any other internal wiring. Have the licensed electrician sign the commissioning record confirming this step was completed.
  3. Connect supply wires to the power terminal block: L1, L2, L3 per the 3-phase wiring diagram. Connect Earth wire to the copper Earth ground lug inside the generator.
  4. Cover the supply wires inside the generator with the protective mesh cable sleeve (if supplied) to protect the cables from the water valve heating element.
  5. Perform insulation resistance test before energizing: minimum 1 MΩ between each phase and earth at 500V DC Megger. Record all three readings: L1-E, L2-E, L3-E.
  6. Verify phase sequence at the generator terminals before energizing.
6.9  K3 Controller Installation
  1. Select the K3 keypad mounting location inside the steam room. For the standard K3 (without a built-in temperature sensor), an external temperature sensor probe must be placed inside the steam room at the specified height — confirm per the K3 manual.
  2. Mount the K3 keypad at a comfortable height on the steam room wall: typically 1200–1400 mm above the finished floor. Position away from direct steam head spray and condensation drip zones.
  3. Run the low-voltage K3 control cable (2C × 0.75 mm² or as specified in the K3 Owner's Manual) from the K3 keypad location through a 16 mm conduit — in a separate conduit from the 415V power cable — to the generator control terminal block.
  4. Connect the K3 control cable to the designated control terminals in the generator per the K3 wiring diagram in the K3 Owner's Manual (Doc. 4209-42). Do not apply any external power supply to the control terminals.
  5. Seal the control conduit entry at the steam room wall with 100% silicone caulk to prevent steam and moisture from travelling back through the conduit to the electrical connections.
  6. Post the Amerec safety warning label on the outside of the steam room door or on the adjacent wall. Photograph the posted label for the QC record.
6.10  Pre-Start Checks
  1. Confirm all plumbing connections are complete: no visible leaks, all copper joints silver-soldered or correctly compression-fitted, all silicone seals at wall penetrations fully cured.
  2. Confirm steam line has continuous slope throughout, no dips or valleys, and absolutely no valves or restrictions anywhere between the generator steam outlet and the steam head.
  3. Confirm the PRV is installed and its discharge pipe runs continuously downhill to the floor drain without routing through the steam room.
  4. Confirm the tank drain ball valve is in the CLOSED position before filling.
  5. Slowly open the 3/8" cold water supply isolation valve to fill the generator tank. The tank fills automatically via the internal water valve. Listen for water flowing in, then stopping when the tank is full (typically 1–2 minutes).
  6. Inspect all water connections for leaks with supply on. Check: 3/8" supply union, PRV connection, and tank drain ball valve. No leaks or drips permitted before proceeding to start-up.
  7. Confirm electrical insulation resistance test passed (recorded readings ≥ 1 MΩ each phase) and 3-phase reconfiguration is confirmed by licensed electrician.
  8. Confirm K3 controller is wired and the keypad is mounted securely in the steam room.
6.11  First Start-Up
CAUTION:The first start-up must be attended by the IHCC Sr. Mechanical Engineer and the licensed electrician. The steam room door must remain closed during the functional test. Have a calibrated thermometer available inside the steam room.
  1. Confirm water supply to the generator is ON and tank is full (per Section 6.10, Steps 5-6).
  2. Energize the dedicated MCB at the DB. The K3 controller display should power on and show default settings.
  3. On the K3 keypad, press ON/OFF to activate the steam generator. The unit will begin heating the water in the tank.
  4. The generator will take 10–20 minutes to reach operating temperature and begin producing steam. During this time, monitor: any leaks from the tank, PRV, or water supply; any electrical fault codes on the K3 display; any unusual hissing noise from a pipe connection.
  5. Set the desired temperature setpoint on the K3 controller per the K3 manual. Typical steam room temperature: 40–50°C. Set the session time as required by the client.
  6. When steam begins flowing from the steam head, close the steam room door and allow the room to fill. Verify steam is flowing from all steam heads if two are installed.
  7. After 20–30 minutes of operation, verify room temperature is rising toward the setpoint using the calibrated thermometer inside the steam room. Record the temperature at 20 minutes and 30 minutes.
  8. Test the auto shut-off: confirm the unit shuts off automatically at the set session time. K3 default is 30 minutes.
  9. Measure running current on all 3 phases using a clamp ammeter at the MCB or generator terminals. Maximum allowable: 19A per phase at 415V~N3. Record L1, L2, L3 readings.
  10. After successful first start-up, complete all commissioning records in Section 8 and obtain IHCC Sr. ME signature.
