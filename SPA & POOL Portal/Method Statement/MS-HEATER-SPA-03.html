1.  PURPOSE & SCOPE
1.1  Purpose

This Method Statement defines the approved procedure for the installation, water piping connections, electrical connections, and full commissioning of both electric inline pool heaters within the SPA & Pool Facility of the CEO Villa Project. Both heaters are manufactured by Elecro Engineering Ltd (UK) and are 3-phase inline electric heaters designed for direct pool water heating.

1.2  Equipment Summary
TagDescriptionModel / Product CodeSerial No.PowerCurrentService
EWH-ODP-01Outdoor Pool HeaterSFSD-45-38030087645 kW69 AOutdoor Swimming Pool Water Heating
EWH-INP-01Indoor Pool / Jacuzzi HeaterE2-3-18-38030082218 kW28 AIndoor Pool & Jacuzzi Water Heating

Both heaters are identical in technology: stainless steel / titanium body inline electric heaters with built-in high-limit thermostat, installed in-line on the pool water return circuit after the sand filter and before the pool. The installation procedure is the same for both units; differences in electrical sizing are noted where applicable.

1.3  Scope of Work
  • Receiving, inspection, and MIR approval of both heater units
  • Installation of heater bodies on the pool water return pipework
  • Installation of flow switch upstream of each heater (safety interlock – prevents dry running)
  • Installation of isolation valves, unions, thermometers, pressure gauges, and non-return valves
  • 3-phase electrical supply connection: 380V / 3N / 50–60 Hz for both units
  • Control wiring: high-limit thermostat loop and flow switch interlock to heater controller
  • Temperature setpoint configuration, system filling, air purging
  • Commissioning: flow rate verification, performance test, safety cutout tests
  • Handover documentation
WARNING:Electric inline heaters must NEVER be energized without confirmed water flow. Running dry for even a few seconds will destroy the heating elements. The flow switch interlock is not optional – it is a mandatory safety requirement.

5.  HEALTH, SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT
5.1  Key Hazard Summary
HazardRiskControl Measure
380V 3-Phase Electrical Supply Electrocution, arc flash. EWH-ODP-01 draws 69A – very high current. LOTO at DB before any electrical work. Licensed electrician only. Test voltage before touching terminals. Insulated gloves (CAT III).
Dry-Running the Heater Element burnout and fire within seconds of energizing without water flow. Absolute rule: NEVER energize without confirmed water flow. Install and test flow switch interlock before first power-on.
Hot Surfaces Burns. Heater body and outlet pipe become very hot during operation (pool water exits at up to 40°C+; element surface is much hotter). Do NOT touch heater body during or after operation without gloves. Allow 30 min cool-down. Post HOT SURFACE warning sign.
High-Pressure Water Water jet injury and flooding if a connection fails under pressure. Pressure test at 1.5× max (6 bar) before energizing. Verify all unions are fully tightened. Stand clear during initial pressurisation.
PVC Solvent Cement Flammable, harmful vapours. Ensure ventilation in pump room during PVC cementing. No open flames. Store cement away from heat. Chemical-resistant gloves.
5.2  PPE & General Safety

All personnel: safety helmet, safety shoes, hi-visibility vest, safety gloves, safety glasses. Electrician: insulated gloves (CAT III) and face shield during electrical work. When PVC cementing: chemical-resistant gloves and safety goggles.

5.3  LOTO Procedure

Before connecting electrical supply to either heater: (1) Switch OFF the dedicated MCB at the DB. (2) Apply personal lock and "DANGER – DO NOT ENERGIZE" tag. (3) Test supply terminals with a calibrated meter to verify zero voltage. (4) Proceed with wiring. Remove LOTO only after all personnel have confirmed work is complete and all covers are reinstated.

6.  INSTALLATION PROCEDURE

The installation procedure below applies to both EWH-ODP-01 (45 kW) and EWH-INP-01 (18 kW). Where the two units differ (electrical cable size, MCB rating, pipe size, flow rate), the differences are clearly noted.

6.1  Pre-Installation Activities
6.1.1  Material Inspection
  1. Upon delivery, inspect both heater units for transit damage. Check: union connections for cracks, element terminals for damage or corrosion, heater body for dents, and controller housing for damage.
  2. Verify nameplates: EWH-ODP-01 must show Model SFSD-45-380 / S/N 300876 / 45 kW. EWH-INP-01 must show Model E2-3-18-380 / S/N 300822 / 18 kW. Both should show 380V / 3-Phase.
  3. Check that manufacturer installation manuals and wiring diagrams are included. These are essential for control wiring and commissioning.
  4. Submit MIR (Material Inspection Request) for both heaters and all accessories. Obtain Consultant signature before installation.
6.1.2  Site Preparation
  1. Confirm the heater installation locations per drawing SB-ASB-SD-ME-008-R1. Both heaters are located on the pool water return line, downstream of the sand filters and upstream of the pool return inlet.
  2. Confirm pipe stubs or spool pieces at the installation location are at the correct elevations and pipe sizes.
  3. Verify adequate clearance around each heater: minimum 600 mm on all sides, including access space to reach union connections and the electrical control panel.
  4. Confirm 380V 3-phase supply is available at the correct DB for each heater (dedicated MCBs: 100A for EWH-ODP-01; 40A for EWH-INP-01).
  5. Perform toolbox talk with the team covering: dry-run hazard, LOTO requirements, and hot surface risks.
6.2  Heater Body Installation
WARNING:The Elecro inline heater elements must be fully submerged in water at all times during operation. If the heater is mounted in a vertical pipe run, ensure flow direction is UPWARD (water enters from bottom, exits from top) so elements are always covered. If horizontal, the element must be at the lowest point of the heater body. Refer to the manufacturer's manual for the correct orientation marking.
  1. Position the heater body in the pipeline at the designated location. The heater inlet must be connected to the flow coming from the sand filter (filtered, pre-treated water). The outlet connects toward the pool.
  2. If wall-mounting or pipe-bracket mounting is required, secure the heater body firmly using the mounting provisions on the heater body. Do not allow the heater weight to hang on the piping unions alone.
  3. Wrap all BSP threaded connections with 3–4 layers of PTFE tape (in the direction of thread winding) before assembly. Apply thread sealant (Loctite 577 or equivalent) over the PTFE for additional security on the high-pressure side.
  4. Install the heater union connections: connect the union halves to the inlet and outlet piping first, then engage the union nut onto the heater body. Hand-tighten, then use a strap wrench for final tightening – do not overtighten plastic unions (max 2 turns past hand-tight, or per manufacturer instruction).
  5. Install isolation ball valve (true-union type) upstream (inlet side) of the heater. Install a second isolation ball valve downstream (outlet side) of the heater. These allow the heater to be isolated and removed for servicing without draining the system.
  6. Install a Non-Return Valve (NRV) on the heater outlet side to prevent backflow through the heater when the circulation pump stops.
  7. Install a pressure relief valve (PRV, set at 4 bar) on the heater outlet between the heater and the downstream isolation valve. The PRV discharge must be piped to the pump room floor drain – do not leave it discharging in the air.
6.3  Flow Switch Installation (Mandatory Safety Interlock)
WARNING:The flow switch is NOT optional. If the heater energizes without water flow, the elements will overheat and fail within seconds. The flow switch is the only primary protection against this. If the flow switch fails open-circuit, the heater must not be capable of energizing.
  1. Install the paddle-type flow switch on the heater INLET pipe, a minimum of 5 × pipe diameters upstream of the heater inlet union. This ensures the paddle has stable, non-turbulent flow for accurate sensing.
  2. Orient the flow switch with the arrow on the body pointing in the direction of water flow (toward the heater). The electrical conduit connection point should be accessible.
  3. Run the 2-core 1.5 mm² control cable from the flow switch to the heater's control panel. Connect as a normally-open (N/O) contact: flow present = contact closed = heater allowed to operate.
  4. The wiring diagram in the heater manual will show the exact terminals for the flow switch interlock. Wire the flow switch in series with the heater enable circuit – NOT in parallel.
6.4  Instrumentation Installation
  1. Install a thermometer with thermometer pocket on the heater INLET pipe (before the heater) to display the pool return water temperature entering the heater. Range: 0–60°C.
  2. Install a thermometer with thermometer pocket on the heater OUTLET pipe (after the heater) to display the heated water temperature leaving the heater. Range: 0–60°C.
  3. The temperature difference between outlet and inlet (Delta-T) is the primary commissioning performance indicator.
  4. Install glycerine-filled pressure gauges (0–6 bar) with root valves at the inlet and outlet of each heater. The pressure differential between inlet and outlet gives an indication of the flow resistance through the heater.
Delta-T Calculation (EWH-ODP-01): At 45 kW full load and minimum flow 8 m³/h → Delta-T = 45 / (8 × 1.16) ≈ 4.8°C. If measured Delta-T is significantly higher, flow is below minimum. If much lower, the heater may not be at full power.
6.5  Electrical Connections
WARNING:LOTO must be applied and confirmed at the DB before any electrical work begins. Verify zero voltage at the heater terminals before proceeding. 380V 3-phase is lethal.
6.5.1  EWH-ODP-01 (45 kW – 69A)
  1. Run 4C × 35 mm² (minimum) Cu/XLPE cable in 40 mm GI conduit from the 100A 4-pole MCB (and 30mA RCD) in the main DB to the EWH-ODP-01 electrical panel. Label cable at both ends.
  2. At the DB: terminate L1, L2, L3 to the 100A MCB. Terminate N to the neutral bar. Label MCB: "EWH-ODP-01 – 45kW Pool Heater". Install 30mA RCD upstream of MCB.
  3. At EWH-ODP-01: open the electrical connection cover. Terminate L1, L2, L3, N to the corresponding terminals per the wiring diagram in the manufacturer manual. Terminate earth to the earth terminal (green/yellow, 16 mm²).
6.5.2  EWH-INP-01 (18 kW – 28A)
  1. Run 4C × 10 mm² (minimum) Cu/XLPE cable in 25 mm GI conduit from the 40A 4-pole MCB (and 30mA RCD) to the EWH-INP-01 electrical panel.
  2. At the DB: terminate L1, L2, L3 to the 40A MCB. Label: "EWH-INP-01 – 18kW Pool Heater". Install 30mA RCD upstream.
  3. At EWH-INP-01: terminate L1, L2, L3, N per wiring diagram. Earth cable (6 mm², green/yellow) to earth terminal.
6.5.3  Both Units – Post-Wiring Checks
  1. Before energizing either heater, perform insulation resistance test on each power cable at 500V DC (Megger): minimum 1 MΩ between each phase and earth. Record all six readings for both units (L1-E, L2-E, L3-E, L1-L2, L2-L3, L1-L3).
  2. Check phase sequence at heater terminals with a phase rotation meter. Incorrect phase sequence will cause a 3-phase resistive heater to malfunction. Correct sequence must be confirmed before power-on.
  3. Connect flow switch control cables to heater terminals per wiring diagram. Test continuity of the control circuit.
  4. Verify that with the flow switch in the de-energized (no-flow) position, the heater control circuit is open. The heater must not be able to energize in this state.
6.6  System Filling, Air Purging & Pre-Start Checks
  1. Open inlet and outlet isolation valves on both heaters. Fill the pool and associated pipework with clean water.
  2. Open any manual air vents at the heater inlet and outlet connections to bleed trapped air. Air pockets inside the heater body can cause local element overheating even with water flow.
  3. Start the pool circulation pump. Confirm water is flowing through both heaters by checking that the outlet pipe is warm (heater not yet on) and the flow switches are in the "flow OK" position.
  4. Verify the minimum flow rate is met for EWH-ODP-01: using a flow meter or timed bucket test, confirm flow is at least 8,000 L/h (8 m³/h). If flow is below minimum, investigate before energizing.
  5. Record initial water temperature (both inlet and outlet should be at pool ambient – same reading = no heating yet). This is the baseline.
  6. Confirm all piping joints are dry and there are no leaks under system water pressure before energizing.
6.7  First Start-Up & Commissioning
CAUTION:Never start a heater on a pool that has just been filled with cold water and has not been circulated for at least 10 minutes. Air must be fully purged before the heater is energized.
EWH-ODP-01 – 45 kW Start-Up
  1. With circulation pump running and flow confirmed (minimum 8,000 L/h), energize EWH-ODP-01 from the DB (MCB ON).
  2. Set temperature setpoint on the heater controller to 28°C (or as specified by Consultant/Client). The heater should begin drawing full current.
  3. After 10 minutes of operation, measure and record: inlet water temperature (°C), outlet water temperature (°C), running current on all 3 phases (A). Actual current should be close to but not exceeding 69A per phase.
  4. Verify the outlet temperature is rising above the inlet temperature. Delta-T at 45 kW and 8 m³/h flow should be approximately 4–5°C at full load.
  5. When pool water reaches the setpoint, observe that the heater cycles correctly: it should reduce power output or cut off to maintain the setpoint temperature.
EWH-INP-01 – 18 kW Start-Up
  1. With indoor pool circulation pump running and flow confirmed, energize EWH-INP-01 (MCB ON).
  2. Set temperature setpoint to 30°C (or as specified for indoor pool / jacuzzi by Consultant/Client).
  3. After 10 minutes, measure and record: inlet temp (°C), outlet temp (°C), running current per phase (A – max 28A), supply voltage L-L (V).
Flow Switch Interlock Test – Both Units
  1. With each heater running (one at a time), close the inlet isolation valve slowly. Within 15–30 seconds, the heater must cut out and display a low-flow or no-flow fault on the controller.
  2. Re-open the isolation valve. Confirm water flow resumes. Reset the heater fault (if manual reset required per controller). Confirm heater restarts and operates normally.
  3. Document the test: time from valve closure to heater trip, fault code displayed, time from valve re-open to heater restart.
High-Limit Thermostat Test
  1. Verify that the built-in high-limit thermostat (typically set at 40–45°C for pool heaters) is present and wired into the heater control circuit. This is factory-set and should not be adjusted on site.
  2. Record the high-limit setpoint value from the heater manual. This safety device protects against overheating if flow is reduced but not fully stopped.
  3. Do NOT attempt to trigger the high-limit in the field – this would require running the heater at reduced flow, which risks element damage. Confirm factory setting by visual inspection of the thermostat dial and cross-check with the manual.
