1.  PURPOSE & SCOPE
1.1  Purpose

This Method Statement establishes the approved procedure for the mechanical installation, water piping connections, electrical connections, and full commissioning of the Cold Plunge Pool Chiller (Tag: CPC-01) within the SPA & Pool Facility of the CEO Villa Project. The document ensures compliance with the manufacturer's installation requirements, the approved shop drawing SB-ASB-SD-ME-008-R1, international mechanical standards, and the project quality and safety plan.

  • Receiving, inspection, and handling of the CPC-01 chiller unit
  • Foundation / housekeeping pad verification and unit positioning using crane or forklift
  • Water piping connections: DN63 inlet (from cold plunge pool return) and DN63 outlet (to cold plunge pool supply)
  • Installation of isolation valves, flexible connections, inline strainer, flow switch, pressure gauges, and thermometers
  • Electrical supply connection: 380V / 3N / 60 Hz, 27A rated current
  • Control wiring and BMS interface (if applicable)
  • First start-up, system air purging, temperature setpoint configuration
  • Performance testing and commissioning data recording
  • Handover documentation and O&M manual submission

5.  HEALTH, SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT
5.1  Critical Safety Hazards
HazardRiskControl Measure
Heavy Lift – 420 kg Unit Crush injury, dropped load, structural damage Certified crane or forklift only. Lift plan required. Clear exclusion zone. Rigger to direct lift. Use manufacturer lifting points only.
Electrical – 380V 3-Phase Electrocution, arc flash LOTO before working on wiring. Licensed electrician only. Test voltage before touching terminals.
Refrigerant R410A Asphyxiation in confined space (displaces oxygen); frostbite from liquid refrigerant release; high pressure (4.4 MPa) Do NOT cut refrigerant pipes. Never expose refrigerant to open flame. On release: evacuate, ventilate, call licensed technician. Manifold gauges required to check pressures.
Working at Height Falls during installation if unit is elevated Use certified scaffolding or MEWP. Safety harness if working above 2 m.
Noise Hazard 62 dB(A) continuous – hearing damage over extended exposure Wear ear protection during commissioning and testing. Limit prolonged exposure near running unit.
Hot Surfaces Burns from condenser coil after operation Do not touch the condenser coil or refrigerant pipes when unit is running. Allow 30 min cool-down before inspection.
5.2  PPE Requirements

All personnel: safety helmet, safety shoes (steel toe), hi-visibility vest, safety gloves, safety glasses. During electrical work: insulated gloves (IEC 60903 class 00 minimum) and face shield. During refrigerant inspection: safety goggles (chemical splash protection). During unit running / commissioning: ear protection (NRR 25+ or equivalent).

5.3  LOTO – Lock-Out Tag-Out

Before making any electrical connections to CPC-01: (1) Switch OFF the dedicated MCB in the distribution board. (2) Apply a personal lock and "DANGER – DO NOT ENERGIZE" tag. (3) Test supply terminals with a calibrated meter to confirm zero voltage. (4) Only then proceed with wiring. No other person may remove a lock that belongs to another worker.

5.4  Environmental – R410A Refrigerant

R410A has a Global Warming Potential (GWP) of 2088. Do not vent refrigerant to atmosphere — this is a regulatory violation. If the refrigerant circuit is found to be leaking, isolate the unit and call a licensed refrigerant technician immediately for recovery using certified equipment. Dispose of old refrigerant through an approved reclaim facility.

6.  INSTALLATION PROCEDURE
6.1  Pre-Installation Activities
6.1.1  Document & Drawing Review
  1. Obtain and study the approved shop drawing SB-ASB-SD-ME-008-R1 showing the CPC-01 location, water piping routing, electrical routing, and clearance requirements.
  2. Review the CZCHCPI103T manufacturer installation manual in full. Pay specific attention to: minimum clearances, water connection requirements, electrical specifications, and first start-up procedure.
  3. Confirm electrical supply specifications with the MEP electrical engineer: verify 380V/3N/60Hz supply is available at the designated DB, with a dedicated 40A 4-pole MCB allocated for CPC-01.
6.1.2  Material Inspection
  1. Upon delivery of CPC-01, inspect the unit for shipping damage before signing the delivery note. Check: outer casing for dents or cracks, condenser coil fins for physical damage, refrigerant service ports for cap integrity, and water connections for damage.
  2. Verify the unit nameplate: Model = CZCHCPI103T, Serial = 8D00240407003C10. Confirm it matches the Asset Register.
  3. Check that the manufacturer's installation manual, wiring diagram, and warranty card are included in the delivery package. These documents must be preserved for the O&M manual.
  4. Submit Material Inspection Request (MIR) for CPC-01 and obtain consultant approval before installation.
6.2  Foundation & Unit Positioning
WARNING:The CPC-01 unit weighs 420 kg. A certified crane or forklift with minimum 1-tonne SWL (Safe Working Load) is required. A qualified rigger must direct the lift. All personnel must remain outside the exclusion zone during the lift.
  1. Confirm the concrete housekeeping pad or structural steel frame at the CPC-01 installation location is complete, cured (minimum 7 days for concrete), and structurally rated for 420 kg plus dynamic loading. Verify level: tolerance ± 5 mm across the pad.
  2. Confirm minimum clearances are available per manufacturer manual at the selected location. Typical minimum clearances: Air inlet side 1500 mm, Air discharge (top) 2000 mm free height, all other sides minimum 600 mm for maintenance access.
  3. Install anti-vibration mounting pads (rubber isolators or spring mounts) at each unit leg position. Verify pads are rated for the unit weight.
  4. Attach certified lifting slings to the manufacturer-designated lifting lugs ONLY. Do NOT lift from refrigerant pipes, water connections, or the condenser fan frame.
  5. Crane or forklift the unit into position. Set the unit down slowly onto the anti-vibration mounts.
  6. Check level in both directions with a spirit level. Adjust levelling feet or pads as needed. Maximum allowable deviation: 5 mm across the entire base frame.
  7. Anchor the unit base frame to the pad using M12 stainless steel anchor bolts. Torque to 60 Nm. Do not overtighten – this would compress the anti-vibration mounts excessively.
  8. Photograph the final positioned unit showing tag number (CPC-01), location, and all clearances. Submit photo for QC record.
6.3  Water Piping Connections
CAUTION:The water-side maximum pressure for CPC-01 is 0.55 MPa (5.5 bar). All piping, valves, and fittings on the chiller water circuit must be rated for at least 10 bar working pressure to provide a 2:1 safety factor. Pressure test at 1.5 × max working pressure = 0.825 MPa (8.25 bar) before connecting to the chiller.
6.3.1  Strainer & Flexible Connector Installation
  1. Install a DN63 inline Y-strainer (60–80 mesh stainless steel basket) on the chiller INLET water line, upstream of the chiller water inlet. The strainer protects the titanium or stainless steel heat exchanger from debris and scale. Ensure the strainer blow-down valve is accessible for cleaning.
  2. Install DN63 flexible rubber connectors (EPDM, wire-braided) directly at both the chiller water INLET and OUTLET connections. These absorb compressor and fan vibration and prevent it from transmitting into the piping system. Flexible connectors must be installed before any rigid piping.
6.3.2  Water Inlet & Outlet Piping
  1. Connect the cold plunge pool RETURN water line (water coming back from pool, warmer) to the chiller water INLET connection (DN63) per drawing. Install true-union isolation ball valve before the flexible connector on the inlet side.
  2. Connect the chiller water OUTLET connection (DN63) to the cold plunge pool SUPPLY line (chilled water going to pool). Install true-union isolation ball valve after the flexible connector on the outlet side.
  3. All uPVC piping connections to be made using ASTM D1785 SCH40 uPVC pipe with solvent-cement joints. Allow minimum 24 hours curing at room temperature before pressure testing.
6.3.3  Instrumentation
  1. Install a glycerine-filled pressure gauge (0–10 bar) on the water inlet line, downstream of the inlet isolation valve and upstream of the strainer. Thread: 1/4" BSP with gate valve root valve.
  2. Install a glycerine-filled pressure gauge (0–10 bar) on the water outlet line, upstream of the outlet isolation valve. This allows the pressure differential across the chiller to be monitored.
  3. Install a bi-metal dial thermometer (0–60°C) with thermometer pocket at the water inlet to the chiller – the "return temperature" indicating pool water temperature entering the chiller.
  4. Install a bi-metal dial thermometer (0–60°C) with thermometer pocket at the water outlet from the chiller – the "supply temperature" indicating chilled water leaving the chiller. The difference between inlet and outlet temperature confirms the chiller is working.
6.3.4  Flow Switch & Interlock
  1. Install a paddle-type flow switch on the chiller water inlet line (after strainer) with stainless steel paddle and EPDM seal. Connect the flow switch to the chiller control panel. This is a critical safety interlock: if water flow drops below minimum (e.g., pump failure), the chiller compressor must shut down automatically to prevent heat exchanger freeze-up or dry running.
  2. Verify flow switch operation by briefly closing the inlet isolation valve during test (Section 8). The chiller must trip and display a low-flow fault.
6.3.5  Air Venting & Drain
  1. Install an automatic air vent (float type) at the highest point of the chiller water circuit. Trapped air causes cavitation, reduced flow, and incorrect temperature readings.
  2. Install a DN25 drain ball valve at the lowest point of the chiller connections for draining the unit during maintenance or winterization.
6.3.6  Pipe Insulation
  1. Insulate all chilled water piping (outlet from chiller) with minimum 19 mm wall elastomeric foam insulation (e.g., Armaflex Class O or equivalent). This prevents condensation dripping onto floors and reduces cooling losses. All joints and fittings must be fully insulated with no gaps. Secure insulation with adhesive and aluminium tape at all seams.
6.4  Electrical Connections
WARNING:ALL ELECTRICAL WORK MUST BE PERFORMED BY A LICENSED ELECTRICIAN ONLY. Apply LOTO before starting. Rated supply: 380V / 3N / 60Hz. Never connect single-phase supply to this 3-phase unit.
  1. Run the 4C × 6 mm² (minimum) power cable in galvanized conduit or on cable tray from the dedicated 4-pole MCB (40A) in the distribution board to the CPC-01 electrical connection panel.
  2. At the distribution board: terminate L1, L2, L3, and N to the 40A 4-pole MCB. Install a 30mA RCD (GFCI) upstream of the MCB for personnel protection. Label the MCB clearly: "CPC-01 – Cold Plunge Chiller".
  3. At the CPC-01 electrical panel: open the panel cover (unit must be de-energized). Terminate L1, L2, L3, N per the manufacturer's wiring diagram. Terminate the earth cable to the dedicated earth terminal in the panel.
  4. Before energizing, perform insulation resistance test: min. 1 MΩ between each phase and earth using a 500V Megger. Record all readings.
  5. Check phase sequence with a phase sequence indicator (phase rotation meter). CPC-01 requires correct phase rotation for the compressor. Incorrect phase sequence will damage the compressor.
  6. Connect control wiring: the flow switch (Section 6.3.4) connects to the chiller's low-flow protection terminals per wiring diagram. Connect any BMS digital output signal cable if BMS integration is specified.
  7. Energize the MCB (pump off, chiller off). Measure supply voltage at the CPC-01 terminals: L1-L2, L2-L3, L1-L3 should each read 380V ± 10% (342–418V). If voltage is outside this range, do not start the unit.
6.5  Pre-Start-Up Checks
  1. Confirm all water connections are complete and visually inspected. The 24-hour PVC curing period is complete.
  2. Confirm pressure test has been completed and passed (see Section 8.1).
  3. Open the water inlet and outlet isolation valves. Fill the cold plunge pool and chiller water circuit with clean water.
  4. Slowly open the air vent valve to purge trapped air from the circuit. Leave air vent open until a continuous stream of water (no air bubbles) comes from the vent. Close the air vent.
  5. Start the cold plunge circulation pump (FP-CPC-01). Confirm water is circulating through the chiller.
  6. Verify the strainer basket is clean and free of debris. Check that the strainer differential pressure is within acceptable range.
  7. Verify the flow switch is receiving sufficient flow: the switch indicator light (if any) should show "FLOW OK" condition.
  8. Check electrical supply voltage one final time at CPC-01 terminals. Verify phase sequence. Confirm all covers are in place.
  9. Check the area around CPC-01 is clear of all personnel except commissioning engineers. Erect a commissioning-in-progress barrier.
6.6  First Start-Up & Commissioning
CAUTION:Only IHCC Sr. Mechanical Engineer and the licensed commissioning engineer may be present during first start-up. No other trades should be working near CPC-01 during commissioning.
Step 1 – Initial Power-On
  1. Energize the CPC-01 power supply (MCB ON). The unit display / controller should illuminate and run its self-check routine.
  2. Do NOT start the compressor yet. Navigate the controller menu and set: (a) Operating Mode = COOLING, (b) Target Water Temperature setpoint = 12°C (or as directed by client/consultant), (c) Verify no fault codes are displayed.
Step 2 – Starting in Cooling Mode
  1. With circulation pump FP-CPC-01 running and water flow confirmed, start the CPC-01 chiller unit in COOLING mode from the controller.
  2. Observe the unit: the compressor should start within 3 minutes (full inverter units have a brief soft-start delay). The condenser fan(s) should also start. A low hum from the compressor and fan airflow from the top of the unit is normal.
  3. Monitor the controller display: verify no error codes appear during the first 10 minutes of operation.
Step 3 – Performance Monitoring
  1. After 15 minutes of operation, measure and record: water inlet temperature (°C), water outlet temperature (°C), supply voltage (all 3 phases), and running current (all 3 phases with clamp ammeter).
  2. Verify that the outlet water temperature is lower than the inlet water temperature. The Delta-T will be small initially as the pool water mass is large – this is normal. Over the next few hours, the pool water temperature will gradually reduce toward the setpoint.
  3. Running current should be less than or equal to the nameplate FLA of 27.0A on each phase. Record actual readings.
  4. The licensed refrigerant technician checks refrigerant suction and discharge pressures with R410A manifold gauges (suction typically 7–10 bar, discharge 20–25 bar at design conditions). If pressures are abnormal, stop unit and investigate.
  5. Measure unit noise level at 1 metre from the unit body with a decibel meter. Must not exceed 62 dB(A).
  6. Check for excessive vibration on the unit body, water pipes, and structure. Vibration isolation mounts should prevent transmission.
Step 4 – Temperature Setpoint Verification
  1. Allow the unit to run for a minimum of 4 hours. Record the cold plunge pool water temperature at start, at 1 hour, 2 hours, and 4 hours to confirm the pool is cooling toward the setpoint.
  2. Confirm the chiller cycles correctly: it should modulate its inverter output as the pool temperature approaches the setpoint, not simply switch on/off.
  3. Once the cold plunge pool water reaches the target temperature (e.g., 12°C), verify the unit enters its maintenance/holding mode and maintains the temperature.
Step 5 – Safety Interlock Tests
  1. Test flow switch interlock: with unit running, briefly close the inlet isolation valve. The chiller must shut down and display a low-flow alarm within 30 seconds. Re-open valve – unit should restart automatically or require manual reset per controller settings.
  2. Test high-pressure cutout: consult manufacturer manual for test procedure. Do NOT manipulate the refrigerant circuit to test this unless specifically instructed.
  3. Verify that when CPC-01 is running and the pool circulation pump FP-CPC-01 trips, the chiller also shuts down (BMS or flow switch interlock).
