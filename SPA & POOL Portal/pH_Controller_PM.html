# CHECKPOINT OK ✓ NOT OK ✗ Remarks
A.  FULL WATER CHEMISTRY TEST
1Test and record: pH _______ (target 7.2–7.8; ideal 7.5); maintain pH within range before relying on automated control
2Test and record: Free Chlorine _______ ppm (target: 1.0–3.0 ppm; ideal 2.0 ppm)
3Test and record: ORP _______ mV (minimum: 650 mV); ORP is a direct measure of sanitiser activity — readings below 650 mV indicate inadequate disinfection
4Test and record: Total Alkalinity _______ ppm (target: 80–120 ppm); maintain TA to ensure pH stability — pH instability is a primary cause of sensor calibration drift
5Test and record: Calcium Hardness _______ ppm (target: 200–400 ppm)
6Test and record: Cyanuric Acid _______ ppm (maximum: 100 ppm); elevated cyanuric acid suppresses ORP reading relative to actual chlorine residual — ORP setpoint may need adjustment
7Adjust water chemistry as required to bring all parameters within range per manual guidelines before relying on automated control for the following week
B.  pH SENSOR CALIBRATION CHECK
8Compare controller pH display reading against fresh manual test kit result — if difference exceeds 0.2 pH units, calibration adjustment is required
9If calibration is required, perform pH calibration adjustment using the CALIBRATE function on the HCC2000 controller per Section 4 (Calibration) of the HCC 2000 Owner's Manual
10Record: Pre-calibration pH (display): _______ | Post-calibration pH (display): _______ | Manual test pH: _______
11NOTE: For precision calibration, use commercially available reference calibration solutions — these provide instrument-grade accuracy exceeding liquid-standard test kits per Hayward manual
C.  SENSOR INSPECTION & CLEANING (perform if any of the following signs are present)
12Assess sensors for signs requiring cleaning: slow response to water chemistry changes; increased frequency of pH calibration needed; inconsistent or drifting pH or ORP readings — if any sign is present, proceed with cleaning below
13SAFETY: Disconnect power to controller before removing sensors; disconnect sensor cables (pH and ORP) from BNC connectors on left side of controller; reinstall BNC protective covers on controller to prevent ESD damage
14Carefully remove pH sensor (PRO15-2) and ORP sensor (PRO25-2) from flow cell by unscrewing sensor bodies — handle sensors with care; do not drop
15Clean reference junction (white teflon ring at bottom of each sensor body) with soft toothbrush and regular toothpaste — a household liquid dishwashing detergent may also be used to remove oil deposits per manual
16Rinse both sensors thoroughly with fresh water after cleaning; replace teflon thread-seal tape on sensor threads before reinstalling
17CRITICAL: Never allow pH or ORP sensor to dry out completely — drying will damage the reference junction and will void the sensor warranty per Hayward manual; if sensors are removed for longer than 1 hour, store immediately in soaking caps filled with original storage solution or clean water
18Reinstall sensors into flow cell — hand-tighten only; do not use tools to overtighten sensor bodies; reconnect sensor cables to BNC connectors as labelled
19Restore power and confirm sensors are reading correctly; perform pH calibration check after sensor reinstallation
D.  FLOW CELL & TUBING CHECK
20Inspect flow cell body and all tubing connections (influent port, effluent port, sample stream port) for leaks, blockages, or chemical deposits
21Confirm flow cell valves are open and water is passing through the cell — sensors require continuous water exposure to provide accurate readings; no flow = no accurate reading
22Inspect 3/8" OD poly installation tubing runs for kinks, blockages, UV degradation, or chemical attack; inspect 3" to 6" sample stream tubing at sample stream port for correct installation
23Confirm 1/4" NPT x 3/8" True-Seal fittings at both pipe tap locations (downstream of filter; suction/low pressure side) are secure, leak-free, and unblocked

1Perform complete sensor removal and cleaning per the weekly cleaning procedure (disconnect power, remove sensors, clean reference junctions with toothbrush and toothpaste, rinse thoroughly, replace teflon tape, reinstall hand-tight) — perform this regardless of apparent sensor condition at 3-monthly service
2After cleaning and reinstallation, perform pH calibration using commercially available calibration reference solutions for instrument-grade accuracy per Hayward manual recommendation
3Record: Pre-clean pH reading: _______ | Post-clean/post-calibration pH reading: _______ | Reference solution pH: _______
4Assess pH sensor (PRO15-2) performance after deep clean and calibration — if sensor still provides slow response, drifting readings, or requires excessive calibration, sensor replacement is required; replace with genuine Hayward PRO15-2
5Assess ORP sensor (PRO25-2) performance — if ORP reading is consistently incorrect or non-responsive after cleaning, sensor replacement is required; replace with genuine Hayward PRO25-2
6Record: pH Sensor PRO15-2 condition: Satisfactory / Requires monitoring / Replace | ORP Sensor PRO25-2 condition: Satisfactory / Requires monitoring / Replace
B.  FLOW CELL FULL INSPECTION & CLEAN
7Inspect flow cell body for cracks, chemical deposits, discoloration, or UV degradation
8Clean flow cell body and housing with clean soft cloth moistened with mild soap and water solution — do not use abrasives, solvents, or harsh chemical cleaners; these will damage the flow cell housing per Hayward manual
9Inspect all three port connections (influent, effluent, sample stream) for chemical scale buildup or blockage; flush ports with clean water if scaling is present
10Inspect flow sensor condition — confirm flow sensor cable is intact, undamaged, and correctly connected; confirm flow sensor is responding to flow changes (controller disables feed when flow stops)
11Inspect flow cell valves — confirm both influent and effluent port valves open and close freely; no leaks from valve bodies
C.  TUBING & FITTINGS FULL INSPECTION
12Inspect full length of 3/8" OD poly installation tubing from influent port to pipe tap (downstream of filter) and from effluent port to suction/low pressure tap — check entire run for kinks, blockages, UV degradation, hardening, or chemical attack; replace any damaged sections
13Confirm combined tubing run (influent + effluent) does not exceed 30 feet maximum per installation requirement; excessive run length reduces sample flow through flow cell
14Inspect both 1/4" NPT x 3/8" True-Seal connector fittings at pipe tap locations — confirm fittings are secure, leak-free, and threads are in good condition; confirm taps are not blocked by scale or debris
15Inspect 3" to 6" sample stream tubing — confirm still correctly inserted in sample stream port; replace if kinked or degraded
D.  CONTROLLER ENCLOSURE & ELECTRICAL INSPECTION
16Inspect controller enclosure exterior for damage, corrosion, or moisture ingress — clean enclosure and front panel membrane with clean soft cloth and mild soap/water or glass cleaner only; no abrasives per Hayward manual
17Inspect front panel membrane switch for damage, lifting, or delamination — if membrane is damaged, controller functionality is compromised; report for replacement
18Inspect power cord for damage, abrasion, kinking, or heat marks — the manual states "All power cords should be inspected frequently; any damaged power cords must be replaced immediately to reduce the risk of electric shock"
19Confirm GFI (GFCI) circuit breaker protecting controller circuit is functional — test GFCI trip function; confirm GFCI resets correctly; Hayward requires controller to be installed on a dedicated GFI-protected circuit
20Inspect all BNC connectors (pH sensor, ORP sensor, Flow sensor) at controller — confirm connections are fully seated, clean, and free of corrosion or moisture; confirm BNC protective covers are stored safely for use during transport or storage
21Inspect pH and ORP sensor cables (24" each) and flow sensor cable for kinking, chemical attack, or physical damage — NEVER cut or splice sensor cables per Hayward manual; damaged cable requires full sensor/cable replacement
22Inspect pH feed output wiring (120/240VAC, 4A) and ORP dry contact wiring (5VDC, 1A max) connections to chemical dosing equipment — confirm terminals are secure and wire insulation is undamaged
23Confirm controller is mounted in safe area away from moving objects, physical hazards, and corrosive materials; confirm it is not accessible to the public per Hayward installation requirement
E.  SETPOINT & CONFIGURATION VERIFICATION
24Verify pH setpoint — factory preset 7.5; confirm current setpoint is correctly configured: pH setpoint _______ (acceptable range 7.2–7.8); adjust if required based on current water chemistry programme
25Verify ORP setpoint — factory preset 650 mV; confirm current setpoint: ORP setpoint _______ mV; note: if goal is to maintain consistent chlorine residual in ppm (rather than consistent ORP), setpoint should be adjusted periodically as pool conditions change per manual
26Confirm both channel mode selections are set to AUTO for normal automated operation
27Confirm BREAKPOINT function settings (if configured) are correctly programmed per facility requirements
28Test alarm functions — confirm audible alarm activates for out-of-range pH and ORP conditions; confirm visual alarm indicators on display are functional
F.  CHEMICAL FEED OUTPUT VERIFICATION
29Verify pH feed output (120/240VAC, 4A, 60Hz) is activating the connected pH dosing pump correctly — introduce a small pH demand and confirm controller activates feed output; confirm feed LED cycles
30Verify ORP/sanitiser feed dry contact output (5VDC, 1A max) is activating the connected chlorine dosing device correctly — confirm output activates when ORP is below setpoint
31Verify Chem Feed dry contact (5VDC, 1A max) is functioning correctly if connected to additional dosing equipment
32Confirm flow protection interlock is functioning — chemical feed must stop when flow sensor detects absent or insufficient flow; test by temporarily stopping circulation pump and confirming feed disables
33Record: pH feed output verified: OK / Fault | ORP feed output verified: OK / Fault | Flow interlock verified: OK / Fault
