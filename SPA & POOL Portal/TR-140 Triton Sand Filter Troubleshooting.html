TR-140 Triton Sand FilterPool water not sufficiently cleanPool chemistry not adequate to inhibit algae growth.Maintain pool chemistry or consult pool service technician.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterPool water not sufficiently cleanToo frequent a backwash cycle.Allow pressure to build to 10 psi above clean filter condition before backwashing.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterPool water not sufficiently cleanImproper amount or wrong sand size.Check sand bed Freeboard and sand size or consult a pool service technician.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterPool water not sufficiently cleanInadequate turnover rate.Run system for longer time or consult dealer or pool service technician.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterHigh filter pressureInsufficient backwashing.Backwash until effluent runs clear.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterHigh filter pressureSand bed plugged with mineral deposits.Chemically clean filter.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterHigh filter pressurePartially closed valve.Open valve or remove obstruction in return line.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterShort cyclesImproper backwash.Backwash until effluent runs clear.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterShort cyclesPool chemistry not adequate to inhibit algae growth.Maintain pool chemistry or consult pool service technician.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterShort cyclesPlugged sand bed.Manually remove top 1 inch surface of sand bed, replace with new sand and chemically clean entire sand bed as described in the Chemical Cleaning Procedure.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterShort cyclesFlow rate too high.Restrict flow to capacity of filter.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterReturn flow to pool diminished, low filter pressureObstruction in pump hair and lint strainer.Clean basket in pump strainer.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterReturn flow to pool diminished, low filter pressureObstruction in pump.Disassemble and clean pump.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterReturn flow to pool diminished, low filter pressureObstruction in suction line to pump.Clean skimmer basket. Remove obstruction in lines. Open valves in suction line.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterSand returning to poolBroken under drain lateral.Replace broken or damaged laterals.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterSand loss to wasteBackwash rate too high.Reduce backwash flow rate.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterSand loss to wasteImproper sand size.Change to proper sand.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterSand loss to wasteAir strainer is damaged or missing.Replace damaged components.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterLeak at closureImproperly tightened closure.Shut off pump, relieve tank pressure, open air bleeder, tighten closure properly.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterLeak at closureDirt or contamination on sealing surface.Shut off pump, relieve tank pressure, open air bleeder, remove closure and clean all sealing surfaces. Reassemble closure properly.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterLeak at closureDamaged part.Same as above except replace damaged O-ring, closure, tank or any combination of parts as required.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterLeak at bulkheadImproper tightened bulkhead assembly.Shut off pump, relieve tank pressure, open air bleeder, remove closure and remove sand to access leaking bulkhead on TR40, 50, 60, TR60 ClearPro, 100, 100C, 140 or 140C. Hold the 2 inch bulkhead and tighten the 2 inch internal locknut. On the TR100C-3/TR140C-3, using the special wrench, P/N 154020, hold the 3 inch flange spacer and with wrench, P/N 154019, tighten the 3 inch flange adapter. Hand tighten plus 1/2 turn.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterLeak at bulkheadDirt or contamination on sealing surfaces.Shut off pump, relieve tank pressure, open air bleeder, remove closure and remove sand to access leaking bulkhead. Remove attached tank internals and remove bulkhead assembly. Clean all mating surfaces and seals. Replace the bulkhead assembly, being careful to assemble properly. Tighten assembly as indicated above.
TR-140 Triton Sand FilterLeak at bulkheadDamaged part.Same as above except replace damaged part or combination of parts.
Safety Warning - High Pressure Filter
Before serviceTurn the pump OFF and shut OFF automatic controls.
Air releaseOpen the manual air bleeder valve and wait until all pressure is relieved.
ClosureNever service or tighten closure while the system is pressurized.
Start-upAfter service, follow initial start-up instructions exactly.
PartsReplace worn/damaged closure, pressure gauge, valves, O-rings immediately.
