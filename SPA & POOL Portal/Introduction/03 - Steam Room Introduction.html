Facility Overview
The Steam Room is a wet-heat wellness facility located in the basement level of the CEO Villa SPA & Pool Facility. Covering a floor area of 7.7 m² and designed for 2 persons, the room delivers a full steam bath experience at temperatures of 40–50°C and 100% relative humidity. Steam is generated by an Amerec AK14 steam generator (14.0 kW at 400V) installed in the adjacent SPA & Pool Pump Room, with steam delivered into the room through a chrome ComfortFlo steam head.
The room features two marble-finished seating benches — a north wall bench and a freestanding central bench — with concealed 3000K LED strip lighting beneath both seating surfaces to create a warm, atmospheric environment. An aromatherapy fragrance injection system is connected to the steam line, enabling essential oil diffusion through the steam. The steam generator is supplied with de-mineralised water from a dedicated reverse osmosis system to protect the heating elements from scale and mineral deposit.

2.2 Seating Benches
Two marble-finished concrete benches provide seating for up to 2 persons. The North Wall Bench is fixed against the north wall, extending 540 mm from the wall with a seated height of 450 mm from the finished floor. It runs along the north wall from east of the wash basin to the east wall corner, with marble cladding to all exposed surfaces. The Central Bench is a freestanding bench, 2,000 mm × 600 mm × 650 mm high, positioned in the center of the room. This elevated bench provides a second-tier seating level where higher positions experience greater steam heat. Both benches are clad in marble to all exposed surfaces.
2.3 Wash Basin & Mixer
A round ceramic wash basin (ROCA Model A3270MJ000) is installed in the north-west corner of the steam room, on the left-hand side as you enter. Cold water rinsing is available through a wall-mounted mixer tap by FANTINI (Model 5302R113B with outlet assembly 4400R010A), providing a cool-down facility directly within the steam room. The mixer is finished in polished chrome consistent with the steam head and room fittings.
3
Lighting System
The steam room is lit entirely with concealed 3000K warm white LED strip lighting, providing indirect low-level illumination consistent with the wellness ambience of the facility. No exposed fittings are used inside the steam room. LED strips are positioned as follows:
  • Under the north wall bench — LED strip hidden beneath the front edge of the bench seat, directed toward the floor, creating a warm glowing perimeter light at low level along the north wall
  • Under the central bench — LED strip along both long sides (2,000 mm) of the central bench, creating a floating light effect from the central bench and illuminating the floor area on both sides
All LED strip lighting within the steam room is rated for wet/high-humidity environments, consistent with the 100% RH saturated steam conditions inside the room.

Water Treatment – RO Feed Water System
The Amerec AK14 steam generator requires de-mineralised water to prevent scale build-up on the heating elements. A dedicated reverse osmosis (RO) water treatment system is installed in the pump room to supply the steam generator with softened, mineral-free water.
