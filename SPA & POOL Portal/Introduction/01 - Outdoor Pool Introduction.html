Facility Overview
The Outdoor Swimming Pool is the primary aquatic centerpiece of the CEO Villa SPA & Pool Facility, located at the ground floor level of the villa complex besides the east wall. The pool is a custom-designed freeform swimming pool with a total water volume of 73 m³ and a uniform pool width of 5.0 metres throughout its length. The design incorporates a progressive depth profile that transitions from a children's shallow zone to a full adult swimming depth.
All pool mechanical equipment — including filtration, heating, UV disinfection, and chemical water treatment — is housed in the dedicated SPA & Pool Pump Room in the basement. The pump room serves as the technical heart of the entire facility.
2
Physical Description – Pool Geometry & Depth Profile
The pool floor is designed with three distinct depth zones. The pool maintains a constant width of 5.0 metres across all zones. The depth transitions from 1.20 m in the children's shallow end to 1.97 m in the main swimming zone, connected by an 81° incline slope.

Pool Features & Hydraulic Fittings
The following hydraulic fittings and accessories are installed within the outdoor pool structure:
  • Perimeter overflow gutter drainage system (AKUA4CE, 2" × 4", 1.5") — continuous perimeter overflow collection and return to filtration system
  • Stainless steel AISI 316 wall inlet nozzles (ATECPOOL Model 0592858-4) — distribute filtered and treated water back into the pool
  • Stainless steel AISI 316 suction inlets (ATECPOOL Model 0592860-1) — draw pool water to the filtration and treatment plant
  • Main drain — 300 × 300 mm stainless steel grating at the pool floor for controlled complete pool drainage
  • Jet nozzle system — connected to dedicated WhisperFlo P-WFL-751 jet pump for hydrotherapy-style water movement effects

Water Treatment & Mechanical System
4.1 System Philosophy
The outdoor pool operates a fully automated, multi-stage water treatment system combining sand filtration, UV-C disinfection, pH and ORP chemical control, and electric inline heating. The dual-pump (duty/standby) configuration ensures continuous pool operation without filtration interruption during pump maintenance.

Feature Wall
A prominent architectural water feature wall is located immediately adjacent to the outdoor pool at the ground floor level. The feature wall measures 4.7 metres in width by 3.0 metres in height and forms the primary visual focal point of the pool terrace.
  • Custom-fabricated SS 316L overflow waterfall gutter — 4,700 mm long × 150 mm wide × 170/210 mm height — delivering a continuous full-width sheet waterfall effect across the face of the wall
  • Dedicated Pentair WhisperFlo P-WFL-801 recirculation pump (Tag: NP-ODP-01) drawing from the collection sump and supplying the waterfall gutter head continuously
  • RGBWW LED wall washer luminaires illuminating the feature wall surface with full-colour ambience effects
6
Lighting System
The outdoor pool and feature wall are equipped with a fully programmable RGBWW LED lighting scheme designed to provide luxury ambience lighting with complete colour customization:
  • Underwater RGBWW LED pool lights — embedded in pool walls below the waterline, producing vivid coloured underwater illumination with multi-colour programmable scenes
  • LED strip lighting — installed around the full perimeter of the pool deck, providing edge definition and surface reflection effects
  • RGBWW LED wall washer lights — dedicated fixtures projecting full-colour wash lighting across the 4.7 m feature wall
  • Wireless remote control system — operates up to 20 metres range; provides full control of all pool and feature wall lighting including colour selection, colour-change programmes, dimming levels, and manual on/off switching
