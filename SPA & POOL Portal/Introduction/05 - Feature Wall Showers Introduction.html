Facility Overview
The Feature Wall Showers are two decorative water cascade installations located on the north and south walls of the ground floor outdoor pool area (FW-01 – North Wall and FW-02 – South Wall). Each feature wall is a self-contained, closed-loop water recirculation system in which a submersible pump (PEDROLLO TOP3) draws water from a tiled reinforced concrete storage basin at the base of the wall and delivers it via a rising pipe to a custom stainless steel overflow gutter (AISI 316L, marine grade) mounted at the top of the 2,880 mm wall. Water overflows the gutter continuously and cascades as a full-width sheet down the tiled wall face, returning to the storage basin below by gravity.
Each wall is served independently by its own dedicated submersible pump (WFSP-01 / WFSP-02), stainless steel overflow gutter (WFC-01 / WFC-02), 20 mm mains cold water supply with manual isolation valve (for initial filling and evaporation top-up), and a 1½" DN40 UPVC drain pipe with ball valve for complete basin drainage during maintenance. An AQUA AQ-XT005 purpose-built RGB underwater LED linear wall washer (1,200 mm, IP68) is submerged in each storage basin, projecting dynamic colour-changing light upward across the falling water curtain — cycling through blues, aquas, amber, and gold tones to create a dramatic visual feature in the outdoor pool area.
Both the wall face (the full shower surface from 0 to 2,880 mm) and the interior of each water storage basin (1,600 × 945 × 500 mm deep) are tiled with Caesar Ceramiche AL CHEMY/TC – Mint Matt (R9 DIN 51130, 9 mm thickness) for a consistent, high-quality wet-area finish throughout.

3.2 Wall Face & Tile Finish
The wall face surface — the full height tiled surface over which the water sheet cascades — is finished entirely with Caesar Ceramiche Italy, collection AL CHEMY/TC, colour Mint Matt. The tile has a matte surface texture with R9 anti-slip classification per DIN 51130, 9 mm thickness, and is applied from finished floor level (0 mm) continuously up to the top of the wall (2,880 mm). The same tile is applied across both FW-01 (North Wall) and FW-02 (South Wall) for a uniform appearance throughout the outdoor pool area.
3.3 Water Storage Basin
Each water storage basin is constructed in reinforced concrete (RC) and is fully tiled internally with the same Caesar Ceramiche AL CHEMY/TC Mint Matt tile as the wall face above. The basin measures 1,600 mm wide × 945 mm front to back, with a design water depth of 500 mm. The basin houses the submersible pump (WFSP-01 or WFSP-02) permanently submerged at the base, and the AQUA AQ-XT005 LED wall washer, also submerged and directed upward toward the cascading water. A 20 mm cold water supply with manual isolation valve serves both initial basin filling and ongoing top-up for evaporation losses. A 1½" DN40 UPVC drain pipe with ball valve is provided at the basin base for complete drainage during maintenance or shutdown periods.
4
Water Circulation System
4.1 System Description
Each feature wall operates as an independent, self-contained closed-loop recirculation system. The submersible pump (PEDROLLO TOP3) sits permanently submerged in the water storage basin. On energising, the pump draws water from the basin and delivers it under pressure to the stainless steel overflow gutter at the top of the wall. Water fills the gutter and overflows continuously across the full 1,250 mm gutter opening, cascading as a uniform water curtain down the full 2,880 mm tiled wall face back into the basin below. This gravity return completes the closed loop. Evaporation losses are compensated by the 20 mm mains water supply with isolation valve. No filtration or chemical dosing system is included — water is regularly drained and refilled as part of routine maintenance.

5
Underwater RGB Lighting
Each feature wall basin is fitted with one AQUA AQ-XT005 purpose-built underwater RGB linear LED wall washer light, permanently submerged in the storage basin and directed upward across the falling water curtain. The AQ-XT005 is 1,200 mm in length, matched to the 1,600 mm basin width to provide even, diffuse illumination across the full water cascade width. The fixture is IP68 rated for permanent submersion and produces colour-changing RGB light — cycling through blues, aquas, amber, and gold tones — creating a dynamic, high-impact visual element in the outdoor pool area, particularly effective at dusk and night-time.

6
Operation
6.1 Start-Up Procedure
  1. Confirm water level in basin is at design level of 500 mm. Top up via 20 mm supply valve if required.
  2. Confirm submersible pump is fully submerged in basin water.
  3. Energise the pump (WFSP-01 / WFSP-02) at the distribution board.
  4. Allow 30–60 seconds for circulation to stabilise and water to reach the overflow gutter.
  5. Confirm even cascade of water across full 1,250 mm gutter width.
  6. Energise RGB LED light (LT-FW-01 / LT-FW-02) and confirm colour operation.
6.2 Shutdown Procedure
  1. Switch off the RGB LED light (LT-FW-01 / LT-FW-02) at the controller.
  2. Switch off the submersible pump (WFSP-01 / WFSP-02) at the distribution board.
  3. For extended shutdowns (maintenance / seasonal): close the 20 mm supply isolation valve.
  4. For extended shutdowns: open the 1½" drain ball valve slowly to drain the basin completely.
  5. Inspect basin, tile, pump, and LED fitting while drained.
6.3 Routine Checks
  1. Daily: Check basin water level is at 500 mm.
  2. Monthly: Inspect overflow gutter for algae, debris, and scale build-up. Clean as required.
  3. Monthly: Inspect tile and grouting on wall face and basin for cracks or delamination.
  4. Monthly: Clean basin interior — drain, scrub, rinse, and refill.
  5. Monthly: Check pump for vibration, noise, or loss of flow. Log.
