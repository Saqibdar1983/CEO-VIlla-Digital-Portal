Facility Overview
The Indoor Pool — commonly referred to as the Jacuzzi — is a heated spa pool located in the basement level of the CEO Villa SPA & Pool Facility. With a total water volume of 11 m³ and a uniform depth of 900 mm, the pool is designed as a full hydrotherapy and relaxation experience, equipped with floor and wall hydro jets, a dedicated air blower, and a warm 3000K LED perimeter lighting atmosphere.
The pool also incorporates a Cold Plunge unit located on the west wall — a pool-within-pool concept — providing cold water therapy directly accessible from within the jacuzzi environment. All mechanical plant for both the jacuzzi and the cold plunge is housed in the basement SPA & Pool Pump Room, with the cold plunge chiller/heat pump unit installed at the outdoor/roof level.

Physical Description
2.1 Pool Geometry & Dimensions
The indoor pool is of irregular shape designed to maximise seating and hydrotherapy use within the available basement footprint. The pool transitions from a narrow stair-access zone at the north end to a wider main pool area.

2.2 Stair Access (North Side)
Pool entry is via four concrete steps on the north side, each with an approximate riser height of 225 mm, descending from the pool deck level to the full pool depth of 900 mm. The stair zone is 2.1 metres wide and extends 1.5 metres into the pool. The pool operation start button is located on the left-hand side of the pool at the stair entrance.

2.3 Concrete Seating Bench
Continuous concrete seating benches are built into the pool structure along all perimeter walls, including the south wall. Each bench measures 350 mm in depth (seat) × 450 mm in height, providing comfortable seating at the 450 mm submerged level for all occupants using the jacuzzi and hydrotherapy jets.
2.4 Cold Plunge — Pool Within Pool (West Wall)
A Cold Plunge unit is integrated into the west wall of the jacuzzi, forming a self-contained pool-within-pool installation. The cold plunge has external wall dimensions of 1,400 mm × 1,400 mm with a water volume of 1.2 m³. It is independently served by its own filtration, chiller, and chemical treatment system entirely separate from the jacuzzi water circuit. One wall inlet nozzle and one suction nozzle serve the cold plunge circulation.

Pool Hydraulic Fittings
  • Perimeter overflow gutter drainage system (AKUA4CE, 2" × 4") — continuous perimeter overflow collection returning water to the filtration circuit
  • Stainless steel AISI 316 wall inlet nozzles (ATECPOOL Model 0592858-4) — distribute filtered and treated water back into the pool
  • Stainless steel AISI 316 suction inlets (ATECPOOL Model 0592860-1) — draw pool water to the filtration and treatment system
  • 300 × 300 mm stainless steel main drain grating at the pool floor for controlled complete pool drainage
  • Adjustable wall hydro jets (ATECPOOL Model 00590059) — 4 m³/h per jet; provide targeted water massage therapy on pool walls
  • 15 No. floor-mounted hydrotherapy jets — air and water mixing nozzles creating a full-floor effervescent hydrotherapy effect underfoot
4
Hydrotherapy System
4.1 Floor Jets & Air Blower
The jacuzzi hydrotherapy system consists of 15 floor-mounted jet nozzles that inject a pressurised mixture of air and water from the pool floor, creating an immersive full-body hydrotherapy experience for all occupants. The jets are fed by the dedicated WhisperFlo P-WFL-901 jet pump, combined with air injection from the Air Supply Silencer blower (model 1-6320220F) rated at 105 CFM open flow. Wall-mounted adjustable hydro jets supplement the floor jets with targeted directional water massage.
4.2 Operation Control
The hydrotherapy system — comprising the jet pump and air blower — is activated by a dedicated push-button control panel located on the left-hand side wall at the stair entrance (north side). The filtration pumps and heater run on their own automatic control circuit and are not affected by the hydrotherapy start button.

5
Lighting System
The indoor pool is fitted with continuous LED strip lighting installed at low level along all four perimeter walls inside the pool, positioned close to the pool floor and concealed within the wall profile. The lighting creates a warm, atmospheric glow at 3000K colour temperature (warm white), providing comfortable underwater ambience suitable for relaxation and hydrotherapy use. The 3000K warm white spectrum was selected to complement the spa environment, creating a calm and inviting atmosphere without the cooler clinical tone of higher colour temperature lighting.
6
Jacuzzi Water Treatment & Mechanical System
6.1 System Philosophy
The jacuzzi operates a fully automated water treatment system consisting of sand filtration, chemical dosing (pH and chlorine), and electric inline heating. Dual filtration pumps in duty/standby configuration ensure uninterrupted operation. Chemical dosing is handled by two CHEM-TECH dosing pumps providing precision chemical injection for pH and disinfectant balance.

7
Cold Plunge System
7.1 Overview
The Cold Plunge is a compact independent pool installed within the west wall of the jacuzzi, providing cold water immersion therapy at 10°C – 15°C immediately adjacent to the hot spa environment. The pool-within-pool arrangement allows users to alternate between hot hydrotherapy (jacuzzi, 36–40°C) and cold plunge immersion (10–15°C) without leaving the immediate pool area — a contrast therapy method recognised for its benefits in muscle recovery, circulation, and wellbeing.
7.2 Dimensions & Capacity
The cold plunge has external wall dimensions of 1,400 mm × 1,400 mm and contains 1.2 m³ of water. One stainless steel wall inlet nozzle and one suction nozzle serve the cold plunge water circulation independently of the jacuzzi system.
