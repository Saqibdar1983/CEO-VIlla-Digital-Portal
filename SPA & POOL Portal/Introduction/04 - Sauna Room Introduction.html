1
Facility Overview
The Sauna Room is a traditional dry-heat Finnish-style sauna located in the basement level of the CEO Villa SPA & Pool Facility. The room covers an approximate floor area of 8.2 m² and is designed for 2 persons. It is heated by an AQUA ZL16.2 sauna heater (16.2 kW, 400V three-phase) located inside the room against the south wall, providing dry heat at operating temperatures of 70–90°C with relative humidity of 10–20% in normal dry mode.
The interior is fully lined with Thermory ALDER STP tongue-and-groove wall panels and fitted with two-tier Thermory ALDER SHP bench boarding — a lower L-shaped bench running along the north and east walls and an upper bench on the east wall at a higher level. All timber is natural Alder in sauna-specific profiles, selected for low resin content, stability under heat, and smooth splinter-free surfaces. The heater control keypad is installed outside the sauna room for safe, convenient operation without entering the room.

2.2 Bench Layout
The sauna is fitted with a two-tier bench arrangement providing the choice of lower (cooler) and upper (hotter) seating positions, consistent with traditional Finnish sauna practice where temperature increases significantly with height. The Lower Bench is a continuous L-shaped bench attached to the north wall and east wall, 500 mm wide from each wall with a seating height of 500 mm from the finished floor. It also serves as the step up to the upper bench. The Upper Bench is a single-tier elevated bench on the east wall only, 500 mm wide at a height of 1,000 mm from finished floor — the primary sauna seating position at the highest temperature zone. Both benches are faced with Thermory ALDER SHP bench boards in 28 × 90 mm profile, installed with gaps between boards for air circulation, drainage, and thermal safety.

3
Lighting System
The sauna room is fitted with two sauna-grade light fittings installed inside the room. One fitting is provided with a Sauna Light Cover Araita Aspen Oval (SAU-ACC-402) — a natural Aspen wood lamp surround designed specifically for sauna environments, providing a warm diffused glow while protecting the fitting from direct heat and steam contact. All lighting inside the sauna room is rated for high-temperature, high-humidity operation consistent with sauna conditions. No LED strip lighting is used inside the sauna room, consistent with the traditional wood-lined sauna aesthetic.

5.3 Sauna Stones (S-169)
The stone basket of the AQUA ZL16.2 heater is loaded with sauna stones (Product Code: S-169, 1 set). Sauna stones are selected igneous rocks (typically peridotite or olivine) that store and radiate heat effectively. When cold water is poured over the heated stones using the sauna ladle — Löyly (the Finnish term for the steam burst) — a short intense wave of humid steam is released into the room, raising the perceived temperature and humidity briefly for a more intense sauna experience. Stones should be inspected annually and replaced if cracked, flaking, or significantly deteriorated.
