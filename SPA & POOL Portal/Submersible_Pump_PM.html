#CHECKPOINTOK ✓NOT OK ✗Remarks
A.  PRE-REMOVAL INSPECTION
1Isolate pump power at circuit breaker before removing pump from sump pit
2Inspect sump pit water level and note any unusual accumulation, oil sheen, or discoloration before removing pump
3Note condition and smell of water in pit — unusual odor or appearance may indicate drainage system issue
B.  PUMP REMOVAL & INSPECTION
4Remove pump from sump pit carefully — place on clean surface for full inspection
5Inspect suction filter (technopolymer strainer screen) — remove and clean all debris, sludge, and sediment (Pedrollo TOP 4 can drain from 30mm above bottom; check for blockage in lower strainer area)
6Inspect pump body (technopolymer) for cracks, impact damage, or deformation
7Inspect impeller (Noryl material) — check for debris blockage in impeller passages; manually rotate to confirm free movement
8Inspect double mechanical seal area (SiC/Graphite/NBR motor side; SiC/SiC/NBR pump side) — check for oil seepage from interposed oil chamber indicating seal deterioration
9Inspect motor sleeve and motor cover (AISI 304 stainless steel) for corrosion or damage
10Inspect motor shaft (AISI 431 stainless steel) for corrosion or scoring
11Inspect full length of 5m power cable (H07 RN-F type) — check for cuts, abrasion, heat damage, or deterioration
12Inspect Schuko plug connections — check for corrosion, loose or bent pins; clean contacts with contact cleaner
13Inspect float switch body and cable — manually cycle float to confirm switch activates and deactivates cleanly
C.  SUMP PIT SERVICE
14With pump removed, clean sump pit interior thoroughly — remove all accumulated sludge, debris, and sediment from pit base and walls
15Inspect pit walls and base for cracks, water ingress, or structural damage — report any findings
16Inspect discharge pipe non-return valve — confirm free operation and no reverse flow; clean valve seat if required
17Inspect discharge pipe above pit for corrosion, blockage, or leakage
D.  RECOMMISSIONING
18Reinstall pump in correct vertical orientation — confirm float switch is unobstructed by pit walls (minimum clearance required around float)
19Confirm pump cable is routed without kinks or pinch points at pit edge
20Restore power at circuit breaker
21Fill sump pit with water to float switch activation level — confirm pump starts, runs, and discharges water normally
22Confirm pump stops when water level drops below float switch cutoff level
23Record test result and confirm normal operation restored

A.  FULL PUMP INSPECTION (ALL 3-MONTHLY CHECKS PLUS)
1Carry out all 3-monthly checks: suction filter clean, pump body inspection, impeller inspection, cable inspection, float switch test
2Remove suction cover (AISI 304 stainless steel) — inspect for corrosion, pitting, or damage to suction filter screen
3Inspect capacitor — check for swelling, bulging, or electrolyte leakage indicating capacitor failure
4Inspect motor winding and motor casing for moisture ingress — pump is IPX8 rated; any moisture inside indicates seal failure
5Measure motor insulation resistance with insulation resistance tester (megger) if available — record reading: _______ MΩ; value below 1 MΩ indicates winding deterioration
B.  SUMP PIT FULL SERVICE
6Drain sump pit completely — pump out all remaining water
7Clean pit interior thoroughly — high-pressure wash if accessible; remove all sludge, scale, and biological growth
8Inspect pit walls and base for cracks or structural damage — report and repair any defects
9Inspect pit cover or grating for corrosion or damage — replace if deteriorated
10Inspect non-return valve on discharge pipe — service or replace if not seating correctly
C.  PUMP SUSPENSION & INSTALLATION CHECK
11Verify pump is installed at correct depth and vertical orientation in pit
12Confirm pump cable is properly supported — no tension on cable at pump; cable protected at pit edge
13Verify float switch travel is unrestricted across full range of motion in pit
14Confirm discharge pipe is properly secured and routed to building drainage without sag or low spots
D.  OPERATIONAL COMMISSIONING TEST
15Restore power; fill pit to activation level; confirm pump starts and discharges normally
16Confirm pump stops at low water level cutoff
17Note pump current draw if clamp meter available: _______ A (rated 5.0A at 220V 60Hz)
18Confirm full automatic operation through complete duty cycle (fill, pump, stop)
E.  DOCUMENTATION
19Record all maintenance activities, inspection findings, and corrective actions in equipment history log
20List all parts replaced with description
21Record motor insulation resistance reading if measured
22Update next service dates in maintenance planner
23Submit checklist to Supervisor for review and countersignature
