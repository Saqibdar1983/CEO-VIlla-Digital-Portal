TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE - PEDROLLO TOP 3 FEATURE WALL SUBMERSIBLE PUMPS
Sr.Observed ProblemMost Probable CauseSite Check / TestCorrective Action
1Pump not startingNo power supplyCheck 220 V supply at socket/panel, breaker, and isolator.Restore power supply. Check breaker, plug/socket, and isolator.
2Pump not startingFloat switch not activated or stuckLift float manually and confirm pump response.Clean float area. Remove obstruction. Adjust float position.
3Pump not startingThermal protection trippedCheck if motor body is hot or recently stopped after running.Switch OFF, allow motor to cool, then restart after checking cause.
4Pump starts then stops quicklyLow water level / dry runningCheck if pump is properly submerged and water level is adequate.Maintain minimum water level before operation. Avoid dry running.
5Pump runs but no water dischargeAir lock in discharge linePump is running but no water comes from outlet.Stop/start pump. Re-route discharge line if air trap exists.
6Pump runs but no water dischargeSuction filter blockedInspect bottom suction strainer and inlet openings.Clean suction filter and remove dirt/debris.
7Pump runs but no water dischargeDischarge pipe/valve blocked or closedCheck discharge pipe, hose, valves, NRV, and feature wall outlet.Clean discharge line. Open valves. Repair/replace blocked parts.
8Pump flow is weakPartial blockage in discharge lineCheck flow at discharge and compare both Pump-01 and Pump-02.Clean discharge pipe, nozzle, and non-return valve.
9Pump flow is weakActual head/pipe resistance is higher than pump capacityCheck vertical lift and long/small-size discharge pipe route.Reduce discharge height/losses or confirm pump selection.
10Pump trips breakerShort circuit, cable damage, or water ingressInspect cable, plug, junction box, and socket for damage/moisture.Stop immediately. Do not operate. Call electrician.
11Pump trips breakerImpeller jammed by debrisAfter isolation, inspect suction/impeller area.Remove debris and clean pump base/impeller area.
12Pump trips thermal overloadDry running or poor coolingCheck water level and whether pump is exposed during operation.Keep pump submerged. Correct float switch level.
13Pump trips thermal overloadBlocked suction or impeller causing high loadRecord running current and inspect strainer/impeller.Clean pump. Re-test current. Normal nameplate current is 3.7 A.
14Pump noisy/vibratingDebris inside impeller or pump bodyListen for abnormal noise; inspect pump base after isolation.Remove debris. Clean suction and impeller area.
15Pump noisy/vibratingPump not stable / not verticalCheck pump position in tank/sump.Place pump vertically on stable base.
16Electric shock/leakage concernDamaged cable, plug, or insulationCheck cable/plug without touching water. Use proper electrical test only.Stop immediately. Do not touch water. Call electrician.
17Burning smell / hot motorMotor overheating or winding issueCheck current against 3.7 A and check for blockage/dry running.Stop pump. If current remains high after cleaning, send for service.
18Pump running continuouslyFloat switch stuck ONCheck free movement of float switch.Clean/adjust/replace float switch.
19Pump does not stopWater level not dropping or float not reaching OFF levelObserve water level and float switch position.Check discharge flow; adjust float; clean line if flow is weak.
20Water returns after pump stopsNon-return valve missing, blocked, or passingObserve backflow from discharge line after pump stops.Install, clean, or replace non-return valve.
