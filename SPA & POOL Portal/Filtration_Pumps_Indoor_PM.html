#CHECKPOINTOK ✓NOT OK ✗Remarks
A.  VISUAL & MECHANICAL INSPECTION
1Inspect pump body, volute, and strainer pot for cracks, corrosion, or physical damage
2Inspect all union fittings (1.5" and 2") — check O-rings and union nuts (P/N 351157 union kit) for tightness and leaks
3Inspect suction drain covers at indoor pool main drain — confirm covers are undamaged, firmly secured, and ANSI/ASME A112.19.8 compliant
4Inspect check valves on discharge line — confirm correct operation; no reverse flow
5Check pump mounting feet and base fixings — tighten loose fasteners; inspect base for vibration cracks
B.  STRAINER BASKET SERVICE
6Isolate pump power at circuit breaker before opening strainer pot — WARNING: do not open if pump recently ran dry (scalding vapor hazard per SuperFlo manual)
7Open sand filter air relief valve to relieve all system pressure; confirm system pressure is fully released before proceeding
8Remove SuperFlo Cam and Ramp lid (turn counter-clockwise to unlock) — verify pot is cool to touch and pressure-free before opening
9Remove strainer basket (P/N 355667) — inspect for cracks; replace if cracked
10Clean basket thoroughly — remove all debris and rinse with clean water
11Clean strainer pot interior and lid sealing surface
12Inspect lid O-ring (P/N 357255) — clean, lubricate with silicone grease, and replace if compressed, cracked, or deteriorated
13Reinstall basket (align notch with rib); fill pot with water; reinstall lid (turn clockwise until fully locked)
14Open sand filter air relief valve; stand clear of filter; start pump; close valve when steady stream of water appears — confirm pump is primed and running
C.  ELECTRICAL CHECKS
15Inspect power supply cable from circuit breaker to motor — check insulation, routing, and terminations for damage or heat marks
16Test GFCI — press TEST button: power must interrupt; press RESET: power must restore; report any failure immediately
17Check and tighten all electrical terminal screws at motor terminal block
18Inspect motor fan guard and cooling vents — clear all accumulated dust and debris
D.  DUTY / STANDBY CHANGEOVER TEST
19Switch from duty pump to standby pump — confirm standby starts, primes (self-priming to 10 ft suction lift), and runs without unusual noise or vibration
20Inspect standby pump strainer basket condition through see-through lid
21Return system to duty pump after confirming standby is fully operational — confirm smooth transition
E.  PERFORMANCE VERIFICATION
22Record suction pressure: _______ PSI; discharge pressure: _______ PSI — verify within rated working pressure of 36 PSI max at 104°F
23Confirm flow continuity through sand filter → heater (Evolution 18KW) → indoor jet nozzles
24Confirm no abnormal noise, vibration, or cavitation from operating pump

#CHECKPOINTOK ✓NOT OK ✗Remarks
A.  VISUAL & SAFETY INSPECTION
1Inspect pump body, volute, strainer pot, all fittings, and pipe connections for cracks, corrosion, or damage
2Inspect all union fittings — replace deteriorated O-rings (union kit P/N 351157); tighten union nuts
3Inspect indoor pool main drain suction covers — replace if cracked, loose, or non-compliant with ANSI/ASME A112.19.8
4Inspect check valves — confirm correct operation and no reverse flow
5Check and tighten all pump mounting fixings
B.  STRAINER BASKET & POT FULL SERVICE
6Isolate power at circuit breaker; open filter air relief valve to fully depressurize system
7Remove SuperFlo Cam and Ramp lid carefully — verify cool and pressure-free
8Remove strainer basket (P/N 355667) — clean thoroughly; replace if cracked
9Clean pot interior, drain plug ports, and lid sealing surface thoroughly
10Remove both drain plugs (P/N 071131Z) and O-rings (P/N 192115Z) — replace O-rings; reinstall plugs securely
11Replace lid O-ring (P/N 357255) — lubricate with silicone grease before fitting
12Reinstall basket; fill pot with water; reinstall and lock lid; open filter air relief valve; start pump; close valve when steady water confirms prime
C.  SHAFT SEAL & INTERNAL INSPECTION
13Inspect pump body around seal plate for water seepage or mineral deposits indicating seal deterioration
14If seal leakage confirmed: isolate power; drain pump; remove 4 bolts (P/N 355621) securing motor/hydraulics sub-assembly to strainer pot; gently separate halves
15Remove 3 diffuser screws (P/N 356213Z); remove diffuser (P/N 356358Z); inspect diffuser seal (P/N 355030) and seal plate O-ring (P/N 355619)
16Inspect shaft seal set (P/N 354545SS) — rotating and ceramic faces must be free of scoring or damage; replace if damaged; do not scratch polished seal faces
17Inspect impeller (P/N 356356Z) for wear or blockage — clean passages; remove impeller screw (P/N 071652Z) using 1/4 inch hex-key wrench on motor shaft; impeller screw is LEFT-HAND thread (tighten counter-clockwise)
18Reassemble in sequence: seal plate to motor (4 bolts); diffuser quad ring and seal plate O-ring greased; diffuser aligned with molded line; motor sub-assembly to strainer pot (4 bolts finger-tight then torqued in sequence)
D.  ELECTRICAL INSPECTION
19With power isolated, inspect power supply cable — check insulation, routing, and all termination tightness at both ends
20Tighten all electrical terminal connections at motor
21Test GFCI — confirm TEST trips power; RESET restores; record result and date
22Inspect motor fan, fan guard, and cooling vents — clean all obstructions
E.  DUTY / STANDBY RECOMMISSIONING
23Prime duty pump; open filter air relief valve during startup; close when steady water stream confirms prime
24Switch to standby pump — confirm primes and runs; record suction/discharge pressures
25Return to duty pump; record suction pressure: _______ PSI; discharge pressure: _______ PSI (max 36 PSI at 104°F)
26Confirm flow: sand filter → heater (Evolution 18KW) → indoor jet nozzles; visible flow at all nozzles
27Confirm no leaks at any connection following service
F.  DOCUMENTATION
28Record all maintenance activities and findings in equipment history log
29List all parts replaced with description and part number (P/N)
30Update next service dates in maintenance planner
31Submit checklist to Supervisor for review and countersignature
