A.  VISUAL INSPECTION
1Inspect steam generator body, tank, and all external fittings for water leaks — check around the water supply valve (3/8" NPT), steam line connection (1/2"), and drain valve (3/4") for drips or moisture
2Inspect steam head inside the steam room for leaks, blockage, or damage — steam head must point downward and be at least 12" (305 mm) from walls or surfaces to either side; confirm silicone seal around steam head entry is intact
3Inspect all plumbing unions (3/8", 1/2", 3/4") for weeping or drips — unions were installed to allow servicing; any leaking union must be tightened or re-sealed
4Confirm steam room door seals are intact — steam must not escape into wall cavity or adjacent areas (silicone caulk seals around all penetrations must be undamaged)
5Confirm water supply shut-off valve (3/8") is in the OPEN position — never shut off water supply while generator is in use or about to be used
6Inspect manual drain valve (3/4" ball valve) — confirm handle is pointing straight out (CLOSED position) and not dripping
B.  OPERATIONAL CHECK
7Activate steam bath using K3/KT3 control ON/OFF switch — confirm control display illuminates and shows current room temperature and time remaining (display alternates every 5 seconds)
8Confirm water valve activates and tank fills within approximately 1 minute of switching ON — audible water flow should be heard
9Confirm steam appears at steam head within 10–20 minutes of switching ON — steam should be steady with minor flow pauses of up to 10 seconds each time unit calls for water (this is normal)
10Check for error codes on K3/KT3 display — E001 (open sensor/cable), E002 (shorted sensor/cable), E055 (room too hot), or display showing >>>deg (temperature over 132°F / 55°C); any error code must be reported immediately
11Confirm unit shuts down automatically at end of set bath time (maximum 60 minutes on K3 default setting) — steam stops and control display goes dark at shutdown
12After session: confirm drain valve is CLOSED and water supply remains ON ready for next session

A.  TANK FLUSH / BLOWDOWN PROCEDURE
1SAFETY: Confirm generator is COOL before starting — do not perform flush immediately after a steam bath session; allow generator to cool fully
2STEP 1 — Activate bath: Press K3/KT3 ON/OFF switch to start a bath, then immediately go to the generator (do not wait for steam — act immediately after pressing ON)
3STEP 2 — Open drain: Open the 3/4" manual drain ball valve (rotate handle so it is IN LINE with the drain pipe = OPEN); allow tank to drain for approximately 2 minutes — calcium-laden and sediment-carrying water will drain out
4STEP 3 — Refill: Close the drain valve; allow tank to refill for approximately 1 minute — this dilutes the remaining water with fresh supply
5STEP 4 — Extended flush: Open the drain valve again and allow water to run continuously with the bath turned ON for a full 10 minutes — this flushes accumulated calcium, sediment, and contaminants from the tank
6STEP 5 — Shut down: Use the K3/KT3 control to turn the bath OFF; go to the generator and wait until water flow from the drain stops completely, then CLOSE the drain valve
7After flush: inspect the drained water for visible calcium scale particles, orange/brown sediment, or foam — increased sediment or foam indicates deteriorating water quality; increase flush frequency if required
8NOTE: If water sputters excessively from steam head (sudden increase), this indicates foam/contaminants in tank — perform this flush at least 3 times consecutively per Amerec troubleshooting instructions
B.  POST-FLUSH INSPECTION
9Inspect manual drain valve (3/4") after closing — confirm no dripping; if valve weeps after closing, report for valve service
10Confirm steam room is clean and dry after session — no water pooling at steam head or on benches from excessive condensation
11Check K3/KT3 control display is functioning correctly — no persistent error codes after flush session

A.  VISUAL & PLUMBING INSPECTION
1Inspect steam generator body, casing, and tank for corrosion, calcium deposits, rust stains, or physical damage — note any new areas of corrosion vs. previous inspection
2Inspect all plumbing connections: 3/8" water supply line, 1/2" steam line, 3/4" drain line, and all unions — tighten any weeping joints; re-seal with pipe compound rated above 212°F (100°C) if required
3Inspect 3/8" water supply shut-off valve — confirm free operation; no stiffness or corrosion on valve body or handle
4Inspect 3/4" manual drain ball valve — operate through full open/close cycle to confirm free movement and no internal leakage; confirm valve closes fully water-tight
5Inspect 10 PSI (38 kPa) Pressure Relief Safety Valve — visually inspect for corrosion, calcium scale on valve seat, or damage; confirm relief valve drain pipe is clear and directed away from occupants (relief valve discharge must not be directed into steam room — scald hazard per manual)
6Inspect steam line (1/2" copper) for the full accessible run — confirm no dips or low points where condensation could collect; confirm slope is continuous toward steam room; confirm no additional valves or shutoffs have been added to steam line (prohibited per Amerec manual)
7Inspect steam head inside steam room — confirm secure, pointing downward, no scale buildup blocking outlet, silicone seal around wall entry intact; minimum clearances maintained (12" from walls, 18" above floor)
B.  LEVEL PROBE & TANK INSPECTION
8Inspect water level probe inside the tank for calcium / mineral scale buildup — per manual: calcium buildup on the level probe causes level measurement errors, false water calls, and foaming
9If probe has scale: descale probe carefully using food-grade descaling solution or citric acid solution; do not use abrasive tools on probe; rinse with clean water after descaling
10After probe cleaning: run a bath session and confirm unit fills correctly, does not overfill, and calls for water approximately every 2 minutes — erratic water fill cycles indicate probe still fouled or damaged
11Inspect heating element access cover (as shown in Diagram 7 of IOM) externally — check for corrosion, calcium weeping, or evidence of element seal failure; do not open unless service is required
C.  ELECTRICAL & CONTROL CHECKS
12With power ON, confirm K3/KT3 control display is functioning — both temperature and time readings visible; display alternates between room temperature and time remaining every 5 seconds
13Test ON/OFF switch operation on K3/KT3 control — unit must start and stop cleanly with no display faults
14Confirm bath time setting: press TIME switch briefly to view current setting (default 30 min, max 60 min on K3; or up to 24 hr if jumper set); confirm setting is appropriate for commercial SPA use
15Confirm bath temperature setting: press TEMP switch briefly to view current setting (range 84–125°F / 29–52°C; default 120°F / 49°C); confirm setting is correct
16Inspect K3/KT3 control unit(s) inside and outside steam room — confirm silicone seal around control tube is intact (steam environment requires airtight seal at wall penetration)
17Inspect temperature sensor (if separate K3 sensor installed) — confirm sensor tip is pointing downward, silicone seal around 7/8" wall penetration is intact, sensor cable is undamaged
18Inspect circuit breaker for steam generator circuit at distribution board — confirm rated correctly and no signs of overheating, discoloration, or tripping history
19Confirm earth (ground) connection at generator is intact — electrical grounding is required on all Amerec steam generators per manual
20Visually inspect power cable and conduit entry at generator — check for moisture ingress, damage, or loose strain relief
D.  WATER QUALITY CHECK
21Test water supply for hardness — recommended range: 10–30 ppm (0.5–1.75 gpg); record: _______ ppm. Values above 30 ppm require increased flush frequency and possible water treatment
22Test water pH — recommended range: 10.5–11.5; record: _______. Outside this range causes scaling and corrosion inside generator tank
23Test silica level — recommended range: 15–25 ppm; record: _______ ppm
24If water quality is outside recommended ranges, consult a local water treatment company for appropriate treatment; document findings and treatment applied
E.  PERFORMANCE VERIFICATION
25Run full bath session after inspections — confirm steam appears at steam head within 10–20 minutes; confirm unit calls for water approximately every 2 minutes during steady-state operation
26Confirm no water runs from steam head (water discharge from steam head indicates faulty water valve or generator fault — turn off breaker and water supply immediately and contact Amerec Support)
27Confirm no error codes (E001, E002, E055) appear during or after bath session
28Confirm automatic shutoff activates at set bath time — steam stops and control display goes dark

A.  FULL VISUAL & PLUMBING INSPECTION
1Inspect entire steam generator externally — body, casing, tank, all fittings, unions, and pipe connections for corrosion, calcium scale, or damage
2Inspect and service 3/8" water supply shut-off valve — operate through full cycle; if stiff, lubricate or replace
3Inspect and service 3/4" manual drain ball valve — operate through full cycle; confirm fully water-tight when closed; replace valve if it fails to seat completely
4Inspect 10 PSI (38 kPa) Pressure Relief Safety Valve — test relief valve operation carefully (have scald protection in place before testing): briefly lift test lever to confirm valve opens and releases then re-seats fully with no dripping; if valve weeps after testing, replace immediately
5Confirm Pressure Relief Valve drain pipe is clear, properly routed, and not directed into steam room or any area where discharge could cause scalding or water damage
6Inspect full run of 1/2" steam line — confirm no dips, proper slope toward steam room, insulation intact on lines over 20 ft (6m), no unauthorized valves or shutoffs added
7Inspect and clean steam head — remove steam head from 1/2" NPT adapter; inspect for calcium scale blockage in the steam outlet; descale with citric acid solution; rinse and reinstall with Teflon tape; confirm downward orientation; re-seal silicone at wall entry
8Inspect all silicone caulk seals at steam line wall entry, control(s), and temperature sensor penetrations — re-apply 100% silicone caulk anywhere seal is cracked, peeling, or separated
B.  HEATING ELEMENT INSPECTION
9SAFETY: Isolate power at circuit breaker before opening heating element access; allow generator to cool fully
10Access heating element cover (per Diagram 7 in AK IOM manual) — inspect element terminals and body for calcium scale, corrosion, or discoloration indicating overheating
11Inspect element flange gasket / seal for evidence of weeping — any moisture around element mounting indicates failed seal requiring immediate service
12If calcium scale is present on elements: perform descaling service using manufacturer-approved descaling procedure; calcium buildup on heating elements causes overheating and premature element failure per Amerec manual
13NOTE: Heating element replacement requires draining the tank and qualified service; contact Amerec Technical Support (1-800-363-0251) for element service
14Reinstate element access cover; restore power; verify normal operation
C.  LEVEL PROBE FULL SERVICE
15Isolate power and allow generator to cool; drain tank via 3/4" drain valve before accessing probe
16Remove and inspect water level probe — check for heavy calcium / mineral scale accumulation
17Descale probe using food-grade citric acid solution: soak for 30 minutes, brush gently, rinse with clean water — do not use mechanical abrasives or wire brushes on probe
18Inspect probe connection and cable for corrosion or damage; clean electrical contacts
19Reinstall probe; refill tank; run test session — confirm even water fill cycles (water called approximately every 2 minutes in steady operation); erratic cycles or foaming after service indicate probe damage or extreme water quality issue
D.  ELECTRICAL FULL INSPECTION
20With power isolated: open generator electrical panel; inspect all power cable connections at terminal block — check for corrosion, heat marks, or loose terminations on Phase A, B, C, Neutral (if applicable), and Earth wires
21Tighten all terminal block connections — AK14 is 400-415V 3-phase; loose connections at this voltage create arc flash and fire hazard
22Inspect earth (ground) wire and ground lug — confirm earth wire is undamaged, firmly landed, and lug is corrosion-free
23Inspect protective mesh covering supply wires inside generator — confirm mesh or protective cover is in place to protect supply wires from water valve heating (required per AK IOM installation instructions)
24Inspect circuit board (PCA) — check visually for corrosion, moisture tracks, burned components, or blown fuses; do not attempt component-level repairs; contact Amerec support if PCA issues found
25Check fuses on PCA — per troubleshooting guide: if control will not turn on, use voltmeter to check voltage across the two fuses; replace if blown; if fuse blows again after replacement contact Amerec Support
26Close electrical panel; restore power; confirm control powers up correctly and displays room temperature and time
E.  TANK DEEP CLEAN & DESCALE
27Drain tank completely via 3/4" drain ball valve with generator cool and power isolated
28Inspect tank interior through drain opening or element access — check for calcium scale, rust, or sediment buildup on tank walls and base
29If scale present: perform tank descaling using manufacturer-approved de-scaler — circulate descaling solution through tank per product instructions; do NOT use hydrochloric acid or harsh chemicals that could damage the stainless steel tank
30Flush tank interior thoroughly with fresh clean water after descaling — drain and refill minimum 3 times to remove all descaling solution residue
31Inspect drain valve seat after draining — confirm valve seats cleanly with no dripping after closure
32Refill tank; run test bath; confirm normal fill cycles, steady steam output, and no foaming or sputtering at steam head
F.  PERFORMANCE COMMISSIONING CHECK
33Run full bath session: press K3/KT3 ON/OFF — confirm display illuminates; water fills within 1 minute; steam appears at head within 10–20 minutes
34Monitor during steady-state: confirm unit calls for water approximately every 2 minutes; flow pauses up to 10 seconds during water refill are normal; longer pauses indicate blockage or probe fault
35Confirm steam room temperature rises toward setpoint — K3/KT3 sequential steam control will reduce to 30% power near setpoint and increase power if temp drops
36Confirm no error codes (E001, E002, E055) appear during or after session
37Confirm automatic shutdown at set bath time — steam stops, no water flow, control display goes dark
38Confirm no water running from steam head after shutdown — any water discharge from steam head indicates generator fault; turn off breaker and water supply; contact Amerec Support at 1-800-363-0251
G.  DOCUMENTATION
39Record all maintenance activities, findings, and corrective actions in equipment history log
40Record water quality test results: Hardness _______ ppm | T-Alkalinity _______ ppm | Silica _______ ppm | pH _______
41List all parts replaced or services performed with description
42Note condition of heating elements and level probe — record any descaling or cleaning performed
43Update next Weekly flush schedule, 3-Monthly, and 6-Monthly service dates in maintenance planner
44Submit completed checklist to Supervisor for review and countersignature
