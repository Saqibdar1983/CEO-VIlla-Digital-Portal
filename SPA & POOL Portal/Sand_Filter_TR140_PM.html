A.  VISUAL INSPECTION
1Thorough inspection of tank body — surface defects, fogginess, or rough texture indicating UV degradation
2Inspect tank foot/base — check mounting tape and foot condition; re-secure if loose (P/N 154407)
3Inspect all bulkhead fittings — signs of seepage or cracking at fitting/tank interface
4Inspect all external piping — support brackets secure, no stress or strain on filter connections
5Check closure lid for thread condition (6 in. buttress thread) — replace if cross-threading or wear evident
B.  VALVE DISASSEMBLY & INSPECTION
6Shut down pump and relieve all tank pressure before proceeding with any valve work
7Open manual air bleeder on closure — wait until all pressure is fully relieved
8Remove multiport valve top cover following manufacturer procedures
9Inspect internal spider gasket and diverter assembly — no splitting, deformation, or tearing
10Inspect all internal valve O-rings — replace any showing deterioration with silicone-lubricated replacements
11Lubricate all internal O-rings and diverter with silicone lubricant (Dow #33 or equivalent)
12Reassemble valve — confirm all six positions function correctly after reassembly
C.  INTERNAL FILTER INSPECTION
13Remove closure using closure wrench (P/N 154512) — follow closure removal safety procedure exactly
14Inspect diffuser assembly — pivot mechanism functions freely, no cracks or blockage (P/N 154906)
15Inspect upper and lower piping assemblies — no cracks, scale buildup, or physical damage
16Inspect air vent tube assembly — no cracks, blockage, or corrosion (P/N 150042 for TR140)
17Inspect laterals at base (visible from top) — no broken or cracked laterals (P/N 154540, 12 in., 8 req.)
18Measure sand freeboard — must be 13.5 in. from tank top; add No. 20 silica sand if level is low
19Assess sand media condition — check for mud balls, channeling, clumping, or hardening of bed
20Reinstall closure — follow safety instructions exactly; hand-tight plus 1/4 turn minimum with wrench
D.  SAND DRAIN & BASE
21Remove 1-1/2 in. drain cap from base — inspect drain port and spigot for blockage or damage
22Flush sand drain with garden hose — confirm drain flows freely
23Re-install drain cap securely — hand tight
E.  CHEMICAL CLEANING (IF REQUIRED)
24If starting pressure after backwash remains 4–6 PSI above normal — proceed with chemical cleaning
25Mix approved sand filter cleaner solution per manufacturer label instructions
26Follow full chemical cleaning procedure per O&M manual — soak minimum 12 hours overnight
27Backwash after soak — verify post-cleaning pressure returns to normal clean baseline
F.  PERFORMANCE TEST & SAFETY
28Record clean filter operating pressure — compare to original commissioning baseline: _________ PSI
29Observe flow rate at pool return fittings — design rate: 106 GPM at 15 GPM/sq.ft.
30Confirm pressure gauge accuracy — replace if reading is incorrect or glass is damaged
31Confirm maximum operating pressure not exceeded: 50 PSI maximum for TR140
32Inspect equipment room — adequate ventilation, no chemicals within 10 ft of filter, clean and dry
33Update maintenance records with all findings, parts replaced, and next scheduled service date
