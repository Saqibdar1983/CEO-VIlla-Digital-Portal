Sharky-Select RO System - Troubleshooting Guide
Problem / SymptomPotential CauseInspection & Checking StepsCorrective Action / Solution
No water or very slow water flow from the faucetFeed water valve closed or low incoming pressure.Check the main feed water valve connection and measure municipal line pressure.Fully open the feed water valve. Ensure supply pressure meets minimum requirements.
No water or very slow water flow from the faucetStorage tank has lost its internal air pre-charge.Lift the tank. If it is heavy but empty of usable water, check the air valve pressure.Shut off system, drain tank, and use a bicycle pump to restore pressure to 7-8 PSI.
No water or very slow water flow from the faucetStage 1, 2, or 3 pre-filters are severely clogged.Check the installation date or inspect the clear Stage 1 housing for heavy sediment accumulation.Replace pre-filters immediately (recommended maintenance cycle is every 6-12 months).
Booster pump runs continuously without stoppingFouled or degraded Stage 4 RO Membrane.Check if pure water production has dropped while wastewater continuously flows to the drain.Replace the Stage 4 High-Rejection RO Membrane.
Booster pump runs continuously without stoppingDefective high-pressure switch or leaking check valve.Check if the pump shuts off when the tank valve is manually closed. Inspect the membrane check valve.Replace the faulty high-pressure switch or the inline one-way check valve.
Water leaks from tubing or quick-connect jointsTubing not fully seated or cut at an uneven angle.Inspect the joint. Check if the plastic tubing is scratched or cut diagonally.Turn off water, depress the fitting collet, pull tube out, make a clean square cut, and reinsert firmly.
Off-taste, flat, or metallic odor in drinking waterDepleted Stage 5 Inline Post-Carbon Polish Filter.Check the age of the yellow-top inline post-carbon filter.Replace the Stage 5 polish filter to restore crisp taste.
Off-taste, flat, or metallic odor in drinking waterExhausted Stage 2 & 3 carbon blocks letting chlorine pass.Test for chlorine breakthroughs or check filter service logs.Replace Stage 2 and 3 carbon filters to protect the RO membrane from chlorine damage.
Excessive noise or heavy system vibrationAir bubbles trapped inside the system housings/lines.Listen for sputtering at the faucet or visible air gaps in the tubing lines.Run the faucet for a few minutes continuously to completely flush out trapped air.
Excessive noise or heavy system vibrationLogiC booster pump vibrating against the cabinet base.Check the mounting of the pump assembly and ensure rubber feet are isolation-aligned.Securely mount the pump and ensure it does not directly contact hard cabinet walls.
