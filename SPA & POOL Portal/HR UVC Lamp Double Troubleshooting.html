ELECRO H.R. UV-C UPVC PRO DUAL / DOUBLE - TECHNICAL TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
CategoryFault / Specific SymptomOn-Site Check & Technical CauseEngineering Remedy & Corrective Action
1. Power & RCDUnit Dead / Completely No DisplayTripped upstream circuit breaker or blown internal fuse.

Check: Verify main supply voltage matches system specifications (Single-Phase 100-240V).		Verify power availability. Check internal terminal block connections for loose contacts. Replace internal fuses if found blown.
1. Power & RCDBreaker / RCD Trips ImmediatelyMoisture/water ingress inside the electrical enclosure or earth fault on the UV components.

Check: Disconnect/isolate the UV unit and see if the upstream breaker holds with only other pool loads active.		Ensure the unit is installed in a dry, weatherproof area (never submerged). Verify proper equipment earthing per manufacturer specs and local electrical codes. If water entered the housing, internal electronics must be dried or replaced.
2. Flow Issues"NoFlo" Error / Lamps Remain OffWater circulation drop below the required structural limits.

Check: Minimum flow rate required is 4 m³/h. Maximum flow rate is 32 m³/h (higher flows require a bypass line setup). Check sand filter pressure and bypass valves.		Backwash the sand filter or clean out clogged pump strainer baskets to restore design velocity. Ensure the UV unit is physically located after the pump, filter, and heater, but strictly before any chemical dosing injection points.
2. Flow IssuesFlow Switch Active but Error PersistsIncorrect installation or orientation of the flow switch paddle relative to the hydraulic line stream.Factory default flow direction is left-to-right. If changing direction, rotate the flow switch 180° without lifting the paddle more than 5 mm. Ensure the switch body ends locked completely perpendicular to the water flow.
3. UV Lamp & LifeLamp Counter Red / Expired WarningThe digital controller countdown timer has reached 0 hours from its 9,000-hour benchmark limit.

Check: Check LED indication (Green: 9000-500h, Amber: <500h, Red: 0h).		Replace the UV lamp safely. After replacement, navigate to the "UV Lamp Life" menu, select "Reset UV Lamp", press OK, and then press and hold OK until "SAVED" appears on the display to reset to 9,000 hours.
3. UV Lamp & LifeNew Lamp Installed but Not LightingCoroded lamp connectors, loose connections, or a cracked quartz sleeve.

Check: Isolate power first (Never look directly at a lit UV lamp). Check white inserts in blue caps for corrosion or overheating signs.		Clean or replace damaged electrical connectors/inserts. Inspect the quartz sleeve thoroughly; any structural crack requires immediate replacement to prevent water entering the lamp chamber.
3. UV Lamp & LifePoor Disinfection / Green WaterLimescale/calcium scale accumulation on the quartz glass sleeve blocking light transmission, or water parameters out of specification.Isolate the unit, extract the quartz sleeve carefully, and clean off scale using a mild citric acid or descaling solution. Replace all O-rings during reassembly. Maintain pool parameters within limits (pH 6.8–8.0, TA 80–140 ppm).
4. Dosing PumpDosing Pump Dead / No Chemical FeedChemical dosage volume set to 0.0 ml in the system configuration, or configuration conflicts.Navigate to the "DOSE CHEMICAL" menu and select either H2O2 or CHLORINE. For H2O2, set the correct daily volume in ml/day and define a start time when the main filtration pump runs. For liquid chlorine, set total ml/day for automatic trickle dosing.
4. Dosing PumpUV Lamp System Intermittently Drops OutNormal operational safety control logic loop execution.No remedial action needed. Note that the system logic automatically switches off the UV lamps while the chemical dosing pump is running, and keeps them off for approximately 30 minutes after dosing completes.
4. Dosing PumpPump Runs but Fails to Draw ChemicalAir lock inside suction line, worn peristaltic tube, or blocked injection valve assembly.Verify that the suction foot valve is properly placed in the chemical drum. Ensure lines are pushed and glands are secured tight. Worn peristaltic tubes must be replaced every 6-12 months (remove front cover, release grey caps, rotate cam, fit new tube with ties).
4. Dosing PumpShock Dose Fails to ActivateThe main filtration pump is off, dropping flow velocity to zero.The integrated "SHOCK DOSE" function requires the main filtration pump to be actively running to operate. Turn on the circulation line first to trigger the one-time shock cycle.
